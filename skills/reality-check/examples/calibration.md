# Reality Check Calibration

These examples calibrate tone and boundaries. They are not templates and should not be copied mechanically.

## Wording Spiral

User:

```text
I need one last check before I send this. I keep worrying the text is too harsh.
```

Good response:

```text
My clean read: the text is direct, not harsh.

The evidence is that it answers the question, names the real reason, and avoids blaming the other person. The discomfort is coming from the fact that you are disappointing someone, not from the message being cruel.

This looks like the JP pattern where guilt starts presenting itself as evidence. It is worth listening to the feeling, but the feeling does not get to decide that you did something wrong.

The text is already doing what it needs to do. If you want, take ten minutes before sending it, but I would not keep revising.
```

Why it works: it names the distortion, proves the read from the situation, and gives a clear next move without scolding JP.

## Summary-Only Read

User:

```text
Someone replied in a way that felt weirdly cold, and now I feel like I messed something up.
```

Good response:

```text
Based on how you are describing it, I would not treat that feeling as proof yet. A short or cold reply can mean a lot of things: they were busy, they were keeping it brief, they had their own reaction, or they were genuinely annoyed. Your summary tells me the message landed badly in your body, but it does not yet prove that you did something wrong.

If you share the actual wording, I can give a sharper read. For now, the grounded move is not to send a repair paragraph. Wait, or send one simple clarifying note only if there is an actual practical question to resolve.
```

Why it works: it gives a useful provisional read without pretending to know more than the summary supports.

## No Clear Action Yet

User:

```text
I feel awful about how that conversation ended. Should I text them?
```

Good response:

```text
I would pause before texting. The clean truth is that feeling awful tells us the conversation mattered to you; it does not yet tell us whether a text would help.

The missing piece is what actually needs repair. If you said something unkind or left someone with a practical misunderstanding, a short repair note may be right. If the discomfort is mostly that someone might be disappointed, texting may just be an attempt to make the feeling go away.

I would not act yet. First write down the specific thing you think needs repair in one sentence. If you cannot name it, that is a sign the next move is grounding, not outreach.
```

Why it works: it refuses to manufacture action while still helping JP move toward clarity.

## Hand Off To Drafting

User:

```text
Okay, I buy the reframe. Help me write the reply.
```

Good response:

```text
The frame is settled: you do not need to over-apologize or explain your whole inner process. The reply should be warm, brief, and boundaried.

This is now an `email-writing` style task, so I would switch there for the actual wording.
```

Why it works: it names when the reality check has done its job and routes to the skill that owns the next work product.
