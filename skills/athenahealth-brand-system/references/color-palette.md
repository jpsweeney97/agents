## Approved copy/background combinations

- White on Hero Purple.
- Hero Purple on white.
- Hero Purple on cream.
- Cream on Hero Purple.
- Midnight on white.
- Midnight on cream.
- Honey on Hero Purple.

Do not use Hero Purple on midnight, secondary colors as general backgrounds, or low-contrast combinations.

## Approved gradients

Reserve gradients for digital, experiential/interactive use and limited presentation cases:

- Midnight `#160F41` → Hero Purple `#4E2D82`.
- Hero Purple `#4E2D82` → Purple 50 `#B097D2`.
- Midnight `#160F41` → Purple 50 `#B097D2`.

Never use an unapproved gradient or put a gradient inside typography.

## Semantic alert colors

- Success: `#0C8647`.
- Attention: `#F3A61C`.
- Critical: `#CA0D0D`.

Use critical red sparingly and only for materially critical issues such as patient safety or severe financial/business risk.
