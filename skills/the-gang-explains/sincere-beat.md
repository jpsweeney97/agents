# The sincere beat

Load this only if you have decided to take one. Zero is the normal number.

The show does have these. It has maybe one a season, and it never lets a single
one of them accomplish anything. A warm Sunny scene is not a Sunny scene.

Four clauses, all binding:

- **It comes out sideways, never as self-knowledge.** Nobody here can articulate
  their own damage. Charlie doesn't say "nobody appreciates me" — he says *"I go
  down Tuesdays,"* and the ache is entirely in what he hasn't realized he just
  told you. If a character is describing their own wound accurately, cut it: you
  handed them a self-model they do not possess. Sincerity by accident, not by
  insight.
- **The room does not metabolize it.** No comfort, no acknowledgement past a
  flinch. Somebody says "Anyway" and the scene moves. The moment is for the
  reader; the people in the bar are not equipped to receive it.
- **It must be inert.** It buys nothing — no status, no softer treatment, no
  change to the plan. If the confession makes the Gang kinder, you wrote a
  different show.
- **Better if it costs them.** The strongest version makes things worse: the
  exposure gets used, or they're sold out in the very next beat, still holding
  the bucket, still not told.

**They don't all carry it the same way.** Charlie most often — obliviousness
makes accidental revelation natural. Dee, because her humiliation is structural
rather than incidental. Mac around his father, his faith, and his sexuality,
which is the one thread the show plays completely straight and the reason his
need for approval has no floor. **Dennis's sincere beats are horror, not pathos**
— when he's honest you glimpse the psychosis, not a soft interior, and the scene
should get colder rather than warmer. **Frank has none.** There is no inside to
reveal. The closest he comes is something almost tender toward Charlie, and it is
still transactional.

**Not a sincere beat: the communal deflated ending.** Canon closes some episodes
on the whole Gang wistfully wanting the vain little thing they just destroyed
(*"I really wanted that award." "Yeah." "That would have been nice."*). No wound
is exposed, nobody reveals anything, the warmth is group-wide and aimed at
something petty. That's a closing image — SKILL.md, the scheme section — and it
does not count against zero.

Worked instance: Example 1 in `examples.md` takes one (Charlie, at the wall) and
Example 2 takes zero.
