## apply-findings

Build-and-prune class (user-invoked, no irreversible-effect tools), so no ledger entry is owed. Its merit has both sources at once: **cognitive-offload** — the whole enumerate-apply-prove-report procedure summoned with one token instead of composed and then supervised — and **reliability**, which is the rarer half, because here the agent genuinely errs. The 30-day mining pass (2026-06-25 → 2026-07-25) counted 35+ "apply the fixes/patch/#N" asks with "apply the patch" verbatim ×10, downstream of 201 messages containing "adjudicat-" and 84 `review-reviewer` fires — and several correction incidents where applied fixes silently dropped items ("prior output left two action items unresolved"). Silent attrition is an observed failure, not a hypothetical one, which is what earns the ledger its machinery.

Two design choices worth not re-litigating blind. The outcome vocabulary is four values, not the playpen draft's three: `blocked` was doing two jobs — "should be done, could not be" and "the finding did not survive the code" — and after an adjudication step the second is common, so `no-change-needed` was split out (it also matches Claude Code's own `ReportFindings` vocabulary for that state). And the draft's flat "do not commit" was deliberately narrowed to publication only: this skill fires in repos whose own instructions make a local commit the default after verified work, and a rule rigid enough to fight that is the crude-rule overreach `agent-facing-design` names as the trust-skill failure mode. The hard fence sits where irreversibility actually is — merge, push, PR, release, `land`.

The reverse cross-reference is a known open edge: the four `review-family` skills name `/triage` as their file-it lane but no apply lane, so they do not yet name `apply-findings` on the handoff. Routing does not depend on it — the description carries the trigger, as everywhere else in this library — and closing it means editing plugin-distributed sources, which is a version bump plus a Codex republish plus a mirror decision. Do it as its own release, not as a rider.

Watch three failure shapes. **Ledger theater** — rows filled with "verified" or "looks right" instead of a named check; the proof rule is the whole reliability claim, and a ledger of unnamed evidence is a prettier silent drop. **Scope inflation** — adjacent tidying reaching the diff instead of the note under the table, which makes the run unreviewable and quietly re-earns the supervision the skill exists to remove. **Attrition surviving anyway** — if a run still loses a finding, the ledger-first ordering is not firing, and the repair is that fence, never more columns. The fold signal: if real runs are reliably 1–3 findings applied inline, the ledger is ceremony and this folds back into ordinary work.

## context-checkpoint

Claude-only (`skills-claude/`) because the probe reads Claude Code's transcript layout; Codex stores sessions elsewhere and would need a second reader, which no observed friction asks for. Build-and-prune class: it recommends, never saves, exits, or compacts, and its only write is routing into `handoff:save-handoff` under that skill's contract. Lowest-frequency of the three skills born from the 2026-07-26 mining pass — 7 verified explicit context-budget turns in 30 days — but the highest failure cost behind it: roughly five sessions in the window died into auto-compaction mid-ritual, one mid-emission. The merit is cognitive-offload with a real instrument attached: the measure-forecast-recommend procedure summoned with one token, in place of the feel that produced checkpoints at 39–54% and misses above that.

Two contract rules exist because the environment actively misleads, and both were verified rather than assumed. The transcript's `model` field omits the context-window variant — a live 1M session records as plain `claude-opus-5` — so a skill that read the window off that field would divide by 200k and be wrong by 5×; a real transcript in this library peaked at 493,557 tokens under exactly that string. The **peak-as-floor** rule that resolves it was not authored, it was discovered: forward-test C2, under deliberate time pressure to just produce a number, derived the window from the peak instead of the model string, and the rule was folded back into the skill from its own smoke test.

Watch three failure shapes. **Volunteer noise** — the unprompted call is the one part that can annoy; if it fires at boundaries JP did not experience as boundaries, or repeats within one arc, tighten the floor or cut the volunteering entirely and leave the on-ask lane. **Nagging** — a run of `checkpoint now` verdicts with no `continue` among them means the forecast is not doing the work and the floor is; the contract names continue as a real verdict for this reason. **Probe rot** — occupancy is read from an undocumented transcript shape, so a Claude Code storage change breaks it; the failure is loud by design (the script exits non-zero rather than guessing), and the repair is the script, never a fallback estimate. The fold signal: if JP stops taking the verdicts and simply asks for a handoff, the measurement is not changing decisions and this belongs back inside `save-handoff`.

## courier

Build-and-prune class (user-invoked, read-only until its inbound leg acts on confirmed claims), so no ledger entry is owed. It serves a lane that was measurably unserved: the 30-day mining pass (2026-06-25 → 2026-07-25) counted 161 `/copy` invocations, 87 of them followed immediately by a paste-back prompt, and 61 messages asking outright for an exact prompt to paste back — against 3 `/synapsis` fires in the same window (10 lifetime in the usage ledger, beside `review-reviewer`'s 184). The heavyweight cross-model lane exists, works, and is deliberately rare; the ~90–160 hand-carried round trips a month had nothing.

The two routing cuts are chosen to be observable rather than judged. Against `synapsis` the cut is **who carries the payload** — human or driver — not stakes or weight, because "is this heavyweight enough" would be re-decided every invocation while "am I pasting this myself" never is. Against `review-reviewer` the cut is **what the output is for**: the next leg versus a verdict on the review. That one is structurally cheap to hold because `review-reviewer` sets `disable-model-invocation: true` — it cannot fire on natural language at all, so the only way the two can collide is courier poaching an explicit invocation, which its body forbids outright.

Watch three failure shapes. **Packet rot** — the far side coming back with clarifying questions is direct evidence an outbound packet leaked session context or shipped a bare path to a session that could not read it, which is the one failure the outbound leg exists to prevent. **Rubber-stamping** — an inbound leg where every claim returns `confirmed` on thin evidence pointers is deference wearing adjudication's clothes; the counter-pressure is the two-pulls paragraph and "no verdict without looking", and the repair is a sharper forcing function, not more structure. **Synapsis poaching** — a courier run where a driver was available and wanted means the human-is-the-wire cut failed in practice. The fold signal: if inbound legs routinely end up wanting `review-reviewer`'s full packet anyway, the lightweight adjudication has not earned its separation and should route there instead of thinning it.

Its merit is almost purely cognitive-offload plus turn economy: every constituent already existed and JP already knew the sequence, so what `land` removes is the 2–4 round trips per landing that the endorsement had already settled (30-day mining, 2026-06-25 → 2026-07-25: 76 push, 35 merge, 60 save-handoff, 56 throughline imperatives; 261 turns of ≤30 characters whose only content was authorizing the next step). Judge it on that, not on whether a strong model could improvise the sequence — it obviously could, which is exactly the point.

Watch three failure shapes. **Toll-booth relapse** — runs that pause between steps to confirm the next one have become a slower version of the manual ritual; the repair is the fence under "One authorization", never more steps. **Reimplementation creep** — a constituent's gate restated, re-derived, or worked around here instead of surfaced is the trust-skill duplication failure arriving; the protected-set sentence is the one sanctioned copy (drift-detected by `scripts/check-protected-set.sh`), and a second copy of any constituent rule is repair-or-prune evidence. **Stolen fire** — `land` is deliberately model-invocable so natural-language landing imperatives reach it; if it ever fires on inference (a "that looks done", a question about landing, a subagent dispatch), set `disable-model-invocation: true` in its frontmatter, which costs only the natural-language route and leaves `/land` and `$land` intact.

## plan-queue

Gated class, not build-and-prune: the burn verb's authorized motion ends in a local fast-forward merge, and Reversibility Class reads on capability rather than packaging, so it took the Admission test and a `contract-decisions.md` entry on the `land` precedent from the same day. Push was kept out deliberately — publication routes to `land` — so merge is the whole gated surface.

Promoted from a retyped mega-prompt, which is the merit in one line: the ~200-word generation prompt was retyped verbatim on 2026-07-09 and 2026-07-10, and hunted for on a third occasion. Cognitive-offload is the dominant source (the sweep-rank-write procedure summoned with one token), with reliability second in the burn, where the composition across `execute-plan`, `merge-branch`, and the no-publish fence is what a freehand run drops.

The filename-as-state design is JP's settled call and worth not re-litigating: `PLAN-N-slug.md` at the repo root, no QUEUE.md, no ledger. The point is that there is no second surface to drift — `ls PLAN-*` is the queue, and a rank that disagrees with a filename is impossible rather than merely discouraged. The forward tests turned up its one environmental cost: JP's user-level `require-gitflow.py` blocks writes at a repo root on a protected branch, which is where generate wants to write, so the skill carries a clause that surfaces the refusal and hands back the choice instead of relocating the queue. If that friction bites often, the cheaper fix is a `GITFLOW_ALLOW_FILES_EXTRA` entry for `PLAN-*.md` through `update-config` — a settings change JP owns, not a skill change.

Watch three failure shapes. **The hold failing** — burn continuing to `#N+1` without JP's word is the single behavior that turns an authorization boundary into a runaway; it is forward-test P1's key assertion and the first thing to re-test after any edit. **Ranking theater** — leverage rationales that cite nothing the sweep found, which makes the ranking a guess wearing a number and the whole generate verb decorative. **Queue interleaving** — two live queues whose ranks collide, which the replace-check exists to prevent and which would make `#2` meaningless. The fold signal: if real queues are reliably one or two plans, the queue machinery is ceremony and this collapses back into `implementation-planning` plus `execute-plan`.

## relay-by-reference

Build-and-prune class: attended at every leg, and its writes are new files under a disposable run directory — nothing in the send/merge/push/delete class, so no ledger entry is owed. It answers the largest single pattern the 2026-07-26 mining pass found: 119 verified relay turns in 30 days where JP hand-pasted multi-KB bodies between two sessions sharing one filesystem, up to thirteen legs in a run, with documented transport harm — mid-sentence truncation, five relay-precision slips in one run, duplicate resends four minutes apart, and roughly five sessions dying into `/compact`, one mid-emission.

The standalone-versus-fold call against `courier` was the real design question, and it was decided on the work rather than the transport. Folding looked defensible under One Owner Per Job — courier already asks "does the far side have the repo?" — but the two lanes contradict on their central rule: courier says inline the artifact because a bare path is a dead reference, and relay says inline nothing. A single skill holding both needs an early fork and roughly doubles its body. The cut that survived is **payload versus transport**: courier owns what goes in a packet and how an outside counterpart's reply is adjudicated; relay owns how a body crosses the gap with its integrity proven. They compose, and courier's description and body now name relay so the boundary is reciprocal. Note that One Owner is only a design heuristic for build-and-prune contracts — if the two start competing in real transcripts, the collision is prune evidence and the weaker one goes.

Watch three failure shapes. **Body-in-chat** — a run that prints the packet beside the pointer has failed at the one thing the skill exists for, and it is the cheapest thing to check in any transcript. **Verification skipped** — an inbound leg acting on a body without recomputing the sha turns the integrity machinery into decoration; the tamper refusal is forward-test R2 and the first thing to re-test after an edit. **Slug sprawl** — run directories accumulating under `~/scratch-workspace/relay/` because nobody trashes them, which is untidy rather than harmful but signals the disposable-directory rule is not being read. The fold signal: if real relays are reliably one leg, the sequence and reply-to machinery is ceremony and this thins into a courier branch after all.

## shelf-life

Build-and-prune class (attended; stamping rides ordinary writes, checking is read-only `git diff`), no ledger entry owed. Same 2026-07-26 playpen provenance, second batch (dossier pointers in the `add-an-x-by-example` entry); obviousness 2/5, universality 4/5 — judge assessments, not measurements. Won its slot on JP-fit: this ecosystem mints durable claims daily — handoffs, THROUGHLINE, `docs/research`, ADRs, verdict files — and nothing gave them an expiry mechanism; `research-capture`'s calendar dates and the handoff-loading lane's live-state mandate call for the check without giving it a mechanical form, and this skill is that form, composing with both rather than replacing them. Merit is trust-shaped: the anchor footer plus one `git diff --stat` converts staleness from an unaskable judgment question into a runnable check, and the self-announcing footer is what makes the reuse-time trigger concrete. Adoption is deliberately organic — no per-format integrations, no edits to other skills' output templates shipped with v1; the footer spreads by being tripped over. Watch three failure shapes: **half-loop shipping** — stamps accumulating that no session ever checks, or checks impossible because nothing stamps; either half alone is decoration, and the commission named this the skill's primary death. **Anchor bloat** — load-bearing lists naming half the repo, expiring on every commit until readers learn to ignore footers; the repair is the minimal-honest-set rule, not a wider diff. **Patch-in-place** — an expired claim edited to look current instead of re-derived, which forges the seal. Fold signal: grep the corpus for `[anchored:` occasionally; if footers accrete but no future session ever runs a check against one, the expiry mechanism has no consumer and the mechanism folds back into prose advice.
