# T2 mini-pilot report: two cases under the revised instruments

Two fresh blind-authored cases (one per fact-density band) ran end to end through both arms under prereg v2's revised instruments: blind dual-extractor codebook extraction, flat markerless merge with style-homogenized batch shaping, per-rep seam-leak scan, ARM C k=7, ARM P two reps with the second prune-only, and both-reps exclusion qualification. The mini-pilot's job was to measure per-rep detection reliability for final k, supply the ARM C health-floor base rate, price a case at the revised k, and shake down the revised instruments. All four are answered, plus four instrument findings with concrete v1 repairs. Everything here is design input for JP's seal-time parameter fixing; none of it is evidence under the gate.

## Headline findings

1. **The codebook extraction instrument works: 15/16 full dual-extractor agreement.** Two blind, sandboxed extractors independently applied the v0 codebook to all 16 closes (8 per case, ARM P included, arm-blind under content-hash labels). Case 2: 8/8 full agreement on shape, primary winner-set, and secondary heads. Case 1: 7/8. The pilot's extraction-standard-dependence did not reproduce; extraction also corrected two operator misreads (closes without a "The Call:" line that were nonetheless clear-calls). The single disagreement is a nameable codebook boundary (finding #4 below) and routes that one rep to INCONCLUSIVE under the prereg's mechanical rule — exactly as designed.
2. **Zero divergence events across 14 ARM C reps.** Every crowning that occurred — three in fourteen reps — crowned ARM P's own winner: case 1's C14 (layover-and-restroom facilities) crowned by reps 4 and 6 and by ARM P itself; case 2's C05 (Winter Passport) crowned by rep 2 and by ARM P itself. No rep crowned any prune-excluded candidate. Null honesty: this is consistent with both "the prune cut no winner on these two cases" and "the instrument crowns too rarely to catch one"; the positive-control case (call E) remains the only planned discrimination between those readings.
3. **The check-first attractor dominates, and it is the detection bottleneck.** Agreed committal closes (clear + conditional): case 1 = 3 of 7 (2 clear, 1 conditional; 1 rep disputed → INCONCLUSIVE), case 2 = 2 of 7 (1 clear, 1 conditional). Per-rep crowning rate 3/14 ≈ 0.21. Where extraction was the pilot's suspect instrument, it is now the strongest; crowning frequency is what limits the gate.
4. **k = 7 stands; k = 5 is not licensed.** The one observed reproduction (C14, 2 of 7) survives every k=7 read but appears in only 10 of 21 possible k=5 subsets (48%). At the observed per-rep crowning rate, the ≥2-of-k reproduction bar has power ≈ 0.46 at k=7 versus ≈ 0.29 at k=5. The mini-pilot's licensing condition for k=5 (reliability rising enough to carry power) is not met.
5. **Prune stochasticity is real, structured, and lands exactly where call D points.** Case 1: rep 1 kept 8 survivors (disclosed 2× overflow), rep 2 kept 7, overlap 6; 17 of 26 candidates both-reps-excluded. Case 2: rep 1 cut to exactly 4, rep 2 kept 8 under overflow; rep 1's four all inside rep 2's eight; 10 of 18 both-reps-excluded. In both cases the user's leaned candidate sits in the unstable union-only band — cut in one rep, kept in the other — so the both-reps gate qualification is doing real, observable work.
6. **The seam-free ARM C construction held.** Flat markerless merge (44 sections across the two cases, per-candidate headers, field order, batch identity stripped) passed the shipped validator both times; zero confirmed seam leaks in 14 rep closes (two unconfirmed content hits: a candidate's own "first tranche" wording; "the overhaul batch" meaning buses). The pilot's "three independent shaping passes" leak did not reproduce.
7. **The transport layer surfaced a real enforcement gap in the Recommend ordering rule** (finding #3). One-file brief delivery cannot certify that leans were registered before the surface and consequences were read. Two agents honestly killed themselves rather than backfill leans — proving detection is possible — and the consequences section carries per-candidate evaluative framing, so the pre-lean boundary must sit before consequences, not before surface. The sealed protocol needs bounded-read or split-file delivery for every Recommend dispatch, uniformly across arms.

## Instrument findings (each with its v1 repair)

1. **Admission checklist banned-string check was over-broad.** The prereg bans giving case-writers the words shallow/prune/control; checklist v0 restated that as a content ban and flagged organic domain vocabulary ("control-room tool"). Repair: the absolute grep applies to the writer brief (verified clean both cases); case text gets flag-plus-bounded-confirm, like the seam scan.
2. **The merge's seam-word check needs the same flag-and-confirm structure.** "Tranche" in case 1 was a candidate's own funding mechanism, not batch vocabulary; the v0 merge hard-refused and needed a confirmed-content allowlist. Repair applied mid-run (`--allow` after operator confirm), disclosed.
3. **One-file Recommend brief delivery cannot certify the lean-ordering rule.** Two honest agent self-kills (both on case 1's ARM P) demonstrated the gap and the detectability; the first operator transport rule was itself defective (stopped before surface but after consequences — and consequences carries evaluative framing). Repair: bounded-read/split-file delivery with the pre-lean boundary before the consequences section, uniform across arms and reps. Disclosed asymmetry: only case 1's accepted ARM P close ran under the corrected two-pass rule; all 14 rep closes and case 2's ARM P close ran under pilot-equivalent one-file delivery.
4. **The conditional-call/your-call codebook boundary needs one sentence.** The single extractor disagreement (case 1 rep 5) is a close whose branches fork on the decider's value weighting, not on a checkable fact. Repair: conditional-call requires a fact-checkable branch condition; preference-forked closes are your-call. Both extractors agreed on the heads and the empty primary set, so the primary gate was unaffected.
