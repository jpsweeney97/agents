# Evidence the user supplied for the hiring decision

Eleven excerpts.

## E1. Stage timings, last thirty candidates who reached an offer or were lost after the final stage (median days per stage; stage medians do not sum exactly to the overall median of 71)

| Stage | Median days |
| --- | --- |
| First contact to first screen | 6 |
| First screen to loop start | 7 |
| The four-stage loop (first to final interview) | 12 |
| Final interview to hiring decision meeting | 34 |
| Decision meeting to offer sent | 4 |
| Offer sent to accepted or declined | 6 |

## E2. Lost finalists, last quarter

Nine candidates reached the final interview; five were lost. Four of the five withdrew during the wait between the final interview and the decision meeting, each citing another offer accepted; exit notes include "no response for three weeks" and "assumed I had been rejected." The fifth declined on compensation.

## E3. The decision meeting (coordinator's notes)

The meeting is booked ad hoc when the four interviewers and the hiring manager are all free for an hour. Calendar analysis over the last quarter: the first hour all five people share is a median of 19 working days out from the request. Interviewers' written feedback is submitted within one working day of each interview (median), so the wait is not for feedback.

## E4. Agency history (finance and coordinator)

Two agency-sourced hires in 2025 took 68 and 80 days; the agency delivered finalists within 9 days of engagement and the same internal wait followed. Agency fee: 20% of first-year salary; finance's note: fees are paid from the headcount budget, and fees on six hires equal about one headcount.

## E5. Scheduling

The coordinator already uses a scheduling tool linked to interviewers' calendars; candidates pick slots. The seven days from screen to loop start are mostly candidate-chosen. The company's applicant-tracking system, in place since 2024, includes this tool.

## E6. Take-home exercise

Three hours; candidates return it in a median of two days, inside the loop's twelve days, not on the critical path.

## E7. Top of funnel

About 140 applicants per posting; nine finalists per quarter; hiring managers describe finalist quality as good. First contact to first screen is six days.

## E8. Offer stage

The four days from decision to offer are finance's sign-off on the specific package. Offers are within band. Two candidates in the last year asked for more time than 48 hours and both accepted after a week.

## E9. Hiring-manager time (calendar audit, last quarter)

Median 3.4 hours a week per manager on interviews, feedback, and the decision meeting. A single onsite day per finalist would take a manager's full day (seven to eight hours) per candidate.

## E10. Feedback timing

See E3: feedback within one working day. Interviewers were trained on structured feedback in 2025.

## E11. Compliance memo

The four-stage loop is fixed in content and order for every candidate. Additional pre-loop screens are permitted but count toward candidate-experience metrics the company reports.
