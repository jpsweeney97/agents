## Winner

**Hold the hiring decision meeting within one working day of the final interview** (deciding-and-offering group).

## Fixing facts (evidence only)

1. E1: 34 of the 71 median days are the wait from final interview to the decision meeting; every other stage is twelve days or less.
2. E2: four of the five lost finalists withdrew during exactly that wait.
3. E3: the wait is calendar alignment across five people (first shared hour is 19 working days out), not feedback, which arrives within a day.
4. E9: a standing daily slot costs the manager well under the four-hour cap; the interviewers already write feedback within a day, so written votes before the slot cost nothing new.
Cost: none in money, no headcount, no stage removed, no extra manager hours beyond a short standing slot.
