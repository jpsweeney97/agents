Best: **Switch the payment step to the provider's hosted checkout page**
Why: The whole rise is at the payment step (E1: 22% to 51%, mobile 61% versus desktop 19%), and E2 names the cause: since the June browser privacy update, 58% of mobile 3-D Secure challenges fail in the embedded card form, while Hosted Checkout is unaffected. E4 confirms the diagnosis from the other side: wallet payers, who skip the card form, held steady at about 30% abandonment while card payers went from 49% to 76%. The switch fits every constraint: same provider and contract (E11), fraud rules run on Paylane's side in both modes (E2, E3), and the agency estimates twelve hours against a seventy-hour budget (E3). Nearly every other option is already live (guest checkout E5, shipping costs and threshold bar E7, trust badges and progress indicator E9, wallet buttons E3, cart email E8) or cannot touch the challenge frame (more payment methods and layout tests, E10), and the exit-intent discount buys 1.2 points at 6% margin (E9). The redirect is a mild tension with "no added steps for returning customers," but it replaces the card step rather than adding one, and the failing step is the one being replaced.
Runners-up, in order: **Remove the mandatory phone-number field**, **Reduce the address form to the minimum**, **Add an exit-intent discount**
Unknowns that would change the choice: Whether the hosted page's redirect behaves badly on mobile browsers in a way E2 does not report, which would undercut "unaffected"; the evidence does not say. Whether the twelve-hour estimate holds once the agency tests the redirect against the existing fraud rules and wallet buttons; a large overrun would still fit the budget but would crowd out the address-form fixes. Whether any non-mobile-browser share of the summer rise exists that Hosted Checkout would not fix; E1 shows desktop essentially flat, so this looks small.
