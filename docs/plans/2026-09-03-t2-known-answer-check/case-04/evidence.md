"Moisture found in the air lines and inside both gripper cylinders. Replaced two cylinders and one valve. Recommend restoring air drying on the supply line; the replaced parts will fail again if air quality is not corrected. No fault found in the controller or drive."

## E3. Shop maintenance notes

- January 18: the compressed-air dryer failed. Bypassed so the machines could keep running. The air-supply contractor's quote to restore drying (replace the dryer, drain and clean the lines): $3,200 installed, one day, no work on the press itself. Not ordered: "press was running fine."
- April onward: gripper and valve faults began as the weather turned humid.
- June: two sets of gripper cylinders and valves stocked ($1,900). Both sets used up by August (see E1 events).

## E4. Press manual, troubleshooting section (excerpt)

"Condensate in the pneumatic system is the leading cause of gripper and valve faults. The compressed-air supply must be dried to a pressure dew point of 3 °C or lower. Operating with undried air voids the pneumatic components' warranty."
