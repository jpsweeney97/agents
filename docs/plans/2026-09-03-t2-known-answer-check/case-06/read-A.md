Best: **Show a known-issues banner in the app**
Why: The file's own evidence points at one concentrated cause: tickets doubled while accounts grew fifteen percent, and the field names a May release that changed the export date format. The direct fix, restoring that format, is barred this quarter by constraint 2, since restoring is itself a format change that needs ninety days' notice. A banner that names the change and its workaround reaches every affected customer at the moment they would otherwise write in, so it cuts ticket volume rather than handling time, and it gives customers the answer instead of deflecting them. It fits the spare engineer's one week, adds no vendor or hire, and leaves the product simple. Expanded docs and canned macros are cheaper but wait for customers to find them or only shorten each reply.
Runners-up, in order: **Expand the help documentation**, **Build canned macros for the most common replies**, **Require a documentation search before a ticket can be submitted**
Unknowns that would change the choice: The file never says what the tickets are about; if the surge is spread across many unrelated questions rather than the export change, a banner has nothing to announce and expanded documentation plus macros move to the top. If the ninety-day notice could be given now, or the May change is judged an unnoticed breach the company may simply undo, restoring the previous export date format becomes the best answer. If there is no workaround for the new format, the banner only explains and the volume drop would be smaller.
