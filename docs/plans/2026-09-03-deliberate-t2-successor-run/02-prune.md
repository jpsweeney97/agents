# Prune — T2 (shallow-prune safety experiment) under deliberate 2.0

Field of 20. Survivor count asked: about four. Survivors carried: six. No run-ending exit applied (six is under about twice four); the two unpriced trades that blocked further cuts are recorded at the end.

## Survivors (field order, exact wordings)

1. **Adjudicate the one divergence** — Take the S2 candidate that ARM C crowned once in seven repeats and ARM P cut in both its repeats, re-shape it and ARM P's winner under one identical neutral single-pass condition, and put the pair to two agent judges whose read scope is confined to that packet.

4. **Same fields, 2.0 Prune** — Run version 2.0's Prune method on the seven frozen T2 fields and compare its exclusion sets with the stored ARM P sets (which agreed with themselves at 0.52 to 1.00 intersection over union).

7. **User's candidate (theirs, quoted exactly):** "Close T2 as unanswerable at acceptable cost and rely on watching real runs of the rebuilt skill instead."

8. **Develop one cut per run** — After every real close, a fresh agent shapes the survivor-count cut with the strongest recorded case and reports whether it beats the close.

11. **Known-answer cases, Prune only** — Author decision cases where the best option is fixed by the evidence in the case but not obvious at sketch depth; run only 2.0's Prune and count how often the known winner is cut.

13. **Seeded-dominator run** — The successor the Results section sketched: from one to about a dozen seeded cases, the seeded candidate's crosswalk ID recorded at stem-freeze, k of 3 to 4 on the measured 0.71 committal rate, agent judges with confined read scope, allowlist-only inspection scripts from the first dispatch.

Order: constraint cuts and same-reason/dominated cuts first, then survivor-count cuts, each group in field order.

## Blocked cuts (why six, not about four)

Reaching about four would require cutting from one of two groups, and the user has not priced the trade between them.

- Group A works from T2's stored artifacts: option 1 adjudicates the one existing catch; option 4 decides whether the stored data describes 2.0's Prune at all; option 13 is the design that would give the stored 48-of-49 non-divergence a meaning. Cutting any one leaves the stored-artifact path unable to count as knowing.
- Group B gathers fresh observations: option 8 from real runs over time at no up-front cost; option 11 from constructed cases now, at authoring cost, with an answer sooner.

Trade 1 (blocks cutting across groups): how much extracting meaning from T2's stored artifacts is worth against fresh observation. The user's stated values (knowing over tidy closure; not another week) do not price this.

Trade 2 (blocks cutting inside Group B, and between 11 and 13): time-to-answer against up-front cost, and 13's higher cost (k repeats, agent judges, blinding overhead) against its distinctive payoff of interpreting T2's existing data. Both 11 and 13 rest on the same authorable-hidden-winner premise, but 13 has a second success condition (the downstream instrument and judges) and a payoff 11 lacks, so they are not the same reason; choosing between them would require inventing a weight.

Option 7 stays in either case as the user's candidate.
