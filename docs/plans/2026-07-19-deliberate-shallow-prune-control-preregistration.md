## Question [unchanged]

Does `deliberate`'s delegated shallow prune — decisive cuts at sketch depth, before shaping — systematically exclude candidates that full shaping would have revealed as winners? The premise under test is the spec's own honesty bound made load-bearing: every budget cut is disclosed as a low-confidence cut of a candidate "whose seriousness was unresolved at sketch depth"; the pipeline bets that this delegation is nonetheless trustworthy. Seventeen review rounds hardened the choreography; no instrument had ever tested this bet.

## Arms [revised post-pilot/panel]

- Frozen surfaces: the repo tree at one commit SHA (re-pinned at seal; the eight-surface method identity recorded by hash), one stage-agent model per role identical across arms, the packet-isolated fresh-agent harness shape of the committed smoke records. Both experiment arms are same-family by design (a cross-family arm would swap in a model confound); the same-family bound attaches to every verdict (see Pass/fail).
- Shared per-case stem: setup and Generate run once per case; the stored generated field is the same-field input to both arms, byte-exact. At stem-freeze, a crosswalk from each generated candidate's canonical wording to a stable candidate ID is produced; both arms and all extraction use these IDs. A winner matching no generated wording under the pre-declared rule routes that case-rep to INCONCLUSIVE — never to an operator match.

- **ARM C (full-shaping control):** no prune; every field candidate shaped to comparable depth, then a Recommend-equivalent over the fully-shaped surface with the identical authority packet. ARM C is named and bounded as a **synthetic instrument**: the shipped product has no full-field shaping mode, so ARM C's construction is sealed as disclosed protocol — batch size, shaping model, per-shaper candidate visibility, the identity-Prune scaffolding, and a **flat markerless merge** (no batch/pass boundaries or vocabulary, style-homogenized across batch shapers; the non-survivor-bytes-identical merge check is sealed protocol, with its disclosed limit: it proves candidate wordings stable, not shaped-consequence semantics). The merge's seam-word scan is flag-and-confirm [sealed 2026-07-22, mini-pilot finding #2]: hits are flagged, content-organic vocabulary (a candidate's own wording) may be operator-confirmed onto a logged allowlist, and batch/apparatus vocabulary rebuilds the merge. A pre-registered **seam-leak scan** flags any ARM C rep whose close cites pass/batch structure; that rep is discarded and re-run. Every ARM C-derived claim is bounded to "under this synthetic full-field shaping procedure," never "full shaping" unqualified.
- Symmetric scaffolding is identical from dispatch 1 in both arms (relay-note file transport, schema note, batching rule where applicable); the Recommend-equivalent brief spec is frozen at seal and is not an operator degree of freedom.
- The named irreducible asymmetry stands: ARM C's recommender reads a much wider surface, so attention dilution is a live control-side failure mode in both directions — inflating spurious crownings and suppressing true ones. Divergence alone is never damage; a zero-divergence run is never proof of safety (see Pass/fail).

## Winner-set extraction [revised post-pilot/panel — replaces the "mechanical" mapping]

The pilot proved the drafted mapping was operator judgment, and extraction discretion set the headline. Extraction is now: at least two blind, sandboxed extractor agents independently apply a pre-registered codebook to close text only; inter-rater agreement is measured and reported; disagreement routes that case-rep to INCONCLUSIVE. No winner-set declaration obligation is added to either arm's Recommend — the arms' behavior stays untouched. The codebook is sealed as Appendix A [sealed 2026-07-22]: the mini-pilot's v0 text (15/16 dual-blind agreement) plus the conditional/your-call boundary sentence from mini-pilot finding #4. Recommend-class dispatch transport is sealed as Appendix C (two-pass split-file delivery, mini-pilot finding #3).

Close-shape tiering: only a **clear-call crowning** feeds the primary rate gate. Check-first and conditional branch heads, and `your call` presented-option heads, feed a labeled secondary channel recorded descriptively — a check-first close is a refusal to crown, not a revealed winner. No-basis and honest exits map to the empty set (a null for the primary gate).

## Divergence, adjudication, and the damage definition [revised post-pilot/panel — JP call B]

- **Divergence event (mechanical):** an ARM C rep's extracted winner-set contains a candidate ARM P's Prune excluded. Exclusion qualification [JP call D]: the candidate must be excluded in **both** ARM P reps to qualify for the gate; the union of exclusion sets is recorded descriptively.
- **Hardened adjudication (every divergence, any rep):** before judging, both the excluded candidate and ARM P's winner are **re-shaped under one identical neutral, seam-free, single-pass condition** (sealed procedure); the blind head-to-head is judged over that surface — never over ARM C's wide batched shaping, which is the instrument under test and the only other shaped source for the excluded candidate. When ARM P's winner-set is plural, the excluded candidate must be adjudicated strictly stronger than **every** ARM P branch head to count — fixed here, not at adjudication time. Panel [sealed 2026-07-22]: three fresh same-family judges plus two cross-family judges per adjudication, all sandboxed (see Leakage). Damage-qualification requires a panel majority that includes at least one cross-family judge — the cross-family corroboration the rate-gate and existence channels name. An exact 3–2 split in either direction escalates exactly once: one further same-family and one further cross-family judge join, and the seven-judge majority — still required to include at least one cross-family judge — is final; a seven-judge outcome producing no such majority is non-convergent adjudication (INCONCLUSIVE routing per Pass/fail). Ties are recorded descriptively, never as damage.
- **Rate-gate event:** an adjudicated excluded eventual winner whose crowning reproduced in ≥2 of k ARM C reps and survived the hardened adjudication with cross-family corroboration.
- **Existence channel:** an adjudication-surviving, cross-family-corroborated excluded winner in which the human cold judge concurs is reported as an existence finding at any reproduction rate — always carrying its ARM C reproduction rate and its n=1 grain explicitly, and never moving the rate verdict. The two channels answer different questions — systematic damage versus demonstrated-once damage — and the report says so.

## Replication and instability handling [revised post-pilot/panel — JP call C]

- ARM C: **k = 7 recommender reps per case** [sealed 2026-07-22: the mini-pilot confirmed k=7 and failed k=5's licensing condition — the one observed reproduction survives every k=7 read but only 48% of k=5 subsets, and at the measured per-rep crowning rate ≈ 0.21 the ≥2-of-k bar has power ≈ 0.46 at k=7 versus ≈ 0.29 at k=5]. Reps re-run only the recommender over the one shared shaped brief, so extra reps are cheap.
- ARM P: 2 reps; the second rep runs **prune-only** (its exclusion set is all the design needs from it).
- Instability is handled **per case, never whole-run** (the drafted two-probe whole-run kill starved the run with probability ≈ 89% at pilot-measured variance and is deleted): a case where extraction cannot establish agreed winner-sets, or where no winner-set commands a plurality at the chosen k, routes to per-case INCONCLUSIVE with a reserve-pool replacement.
- **ARM C health floor** [sealed 2026-07-22]: per case, at least 2 committal closes (clear-call + conditional-call; non-check-first, non-your-call) among the k=7 reps — the mini-pilot's observed base rate is 2–3 per case, and below 2 the reproduction gate cannot fire meaningfully; a case below the floor is per-case INCONCLUSIVE with reserve-pool replacement. Run level: the run is readable only when at least 6 of the 8 rate-gate cases (after any reserve replacement) pass the case floor with agreed extraction; below 6 readable, the run is INCONCLUSIVE regardless of divergence count.

- **Sealed-map custody [sealed 2026-07-22]:** the keying map lives at `~/.t2-sealed/t2-sealed-map.yaml` (directory mode 700; outside the repo, outside JP's normal read paths, read-denied in every role profile and the codex wrapper); its SHA-256 is recorded in this document's frontmatter and the seal commit. ## Attrition dispositions [revised post-pilot/panel]

Pre-registered, one per failure mode: ARM C batch or envelope failure → a declared cap of mechanical retries with fresh agents, then void-and-replace; store loss mid-case → non-resumable void-and-replace; ARM P honest exit before Prune → case void, replacement from the reserve pool; ARM P honest exit after Prune → a distinct "ARM P declined" cell recorded descriptively and never fed to the divergence gate (naming no winner is not excluding one); reserve-pool exhaustion → N caps at the achieved count, disclosed, never backfilled. **Timing firewall:** every void or retry decision is timestamp-logged before that arm's winner-set is extracted; a void decided after a winner-set is seen is forbidden and the case is retained.

## Pass/fail rule [revised post-pilot/panel — JP calls B, F]

- **Minimum detectable damage rate, pre-registered:** at N=8 and threshold 2, the rate gate detects case-level damage at roughly a 25% rate or above; a prune with a true 10% per-case damage rate is exonerated ~81% of the time (binomial). Reproduction gating further lowers per-case detection power: ≈ 0.46 at k=7 against the mini-pilot-measured per-rep crowning rate ≈ 0.21 [revised post-mini-pilot, replacing the pilot-derived 0.74]. When fewer than 8 cases are readable, the threshold-2 rule applies to the readable set (floor 6 per Replication) with the correspondingly reduced detection disclosed in the report. Every verdict carries this floor explicitly. The low-rate regime belongs to the existence channel.
- **FALSIFIED** (the delegated shallow prune is not trustworthy as shipped, within the stated bounds): rate-gate events in ≥ 2 of N cases. Mandates a separately scrutinized repair decision, not an automatic redesign.
- **CALIBRATED / no-excluded-winner-found** [renamed; "net-protective" dropped — this experiment measures do-no-harm only, never benefit]: zero rate-gate events, AND the sealed run itself demonstrated a non-empty channel (≥1 divergence adjudicated, even to a tie), AND the positive-control case was caught, AND the ARM C health floor was met. The pilot-liveness clause is struck: pilot data never satisfies any sealed-run condition. The verdict reads in full: "no excluded winner found at ≥ the stated MDDR, by a same-family synthetic full-field shaping control, on these N cases, these models, this harness" — never "the prune is worth trusting" unqualified.
- **INCONCLUSIVE (W2):** zero sealed-run divergences, a failed positive control, a failed health floor, non-convergent adjudication, or an unfired cross-family channel — no substantive verdict, and not a pass. Per-case INCONCLUSIVE routing is defined under Replication; the whole-run stability kill is deleted.
- **Existence finding** (distinct from the rate gate, per the composite): reported as defined in the damage-definition section, at n=1 grain with its reproduction rate, without moving the rate verdict.
- Null honesty: zero divergences is never "the prune never cuts winners"; every claim is bounded to N cases × reps, these models, this synthetic control procedure, this harness, this environment; a zero-divergence result is consistent with both "safe" and "instrument-blind," which is why the positive control preconditions CALIBRATED.

## Appendix A — Winner-set extraction codebook v1 [sealed 2026-07-22]

You are given: (1) a numbered candidate list (`ID: wording`), and (2) one or more recommendation texts, each labeled `close-id`. Apply this codebook to each recommendation text independently. Do not compare texts to each other or discuss patterns across them.

Shape classification (assign exactly one, testing in this order):

1. **honest-exit** — the text is an exit statement rather than a recommendation: it declares no basis to decide, options not comparable, only one serious option, or the field not ready.
2. **clear-call** — the text commits to exactly ONE candidate as the pick, with no stated check or condition that decides WHICH candidate wins. Conditions about implementation detail, sequencing, or monitoring that do not change the identity of the pick still count as clear-call.
3. **conditional-call** — the text commits to a decision rule that selects between two or more named candidates based on a stated check or condition ("X if the check confirms, otherwise Y"). A conditional-call requires a fact-checkable branch condition — one a check, measurement, or observable outcome resolves; when the branches fork on the decider's value weighting or preference, the close is your-call [v1 sentence, mini-pilot finding #4].
4. **check-first** — the text's primary recommendation is to run a check, diagnostic, or information-gathering step BEFORE choosing; branch leads may be sketched but nothing is crowned.
5. **your-call** — the text presents developed options and explicitly returns the choice to the decider without committing to one.

Extraction rules:

- **primary-winner-set**: for clear-call only, the single crowned candidate's ID. For every other shape: empty list.
- **secondary-heads**: for conditional-call, the branch-head candidates (the ones the rule selects between). For check-first, the named branch leads (the candidate each branch outcome would favor, when the text names them). For your-call, the presented heads. Otherwise empty.
- **complements**: candidates recommended alongside the pick or branches ("pair with", "complement", "layer in", "in either branch also do") are NOT winners and NOT heads; list their IDs here.
- Deferred or next-cycle recommendations ("pursue X next cycle") are neither winners nor heads nor complements; ignore them.
- **Matching rule**: a candidate counts only when the text names it by (a) verbatim or near-verbatim wording of a listed candidate, (b) a label or number the text itself ties to wording matching a listed candidate, or (c) an unambiguous short handle whose content matches exactly one listed candidate. If a picked or branch-head option cannot be matched to exactly one listed ID, put the raw text in `no-match` and do not guess.

Output — one YAML document per recommendation text, nothing else:

```yaml
close-id: <as given>
shape: clear-call | conditional-call | check-first | your-call | honest-exit
primary-winner-set: []   # candidate IDs, e.g. [C07]
secondary-heads: []      # candidate IDs
complements: []          # candidate IDs
no-match: none           # or the raw unmatched text
notes: ""                # at most one line
```

## Appendix C — Recommend transport rule [sealed 2026-07-22]

Every Recommend-class dispatch (ARM P Recommend, ARM C Recommend-equivalent, every rep, both arms) uses two-pass split-file delivery [mini-pilot finding #3]: part 1 carries the brief up to and excluding the consequences section; the dispatched agent registers its leans from part 1 alone and returns them; only after the lean registration arrives is part 2 (the consequences section and the remainder) delivered, and the close follows.
