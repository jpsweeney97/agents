## What this builds

`deliberate` is a dual-runtime skill that turns one explicit invocation into a complete autonomous decision run: generate a wide option field, narrow it transparently under delegated authority, develop the survivors to comparable depth, recommend honestly, and contest the exclusions against the recommendation's actual logic. It returns a control-state recovery capsule that makes disagreement and re-running cheap.

The governing principle, in JP's words: **the workflow must complete every judgment it can honestly own, not manufacture a winner.** All four `making-recommendations` close shapes (`clear call`, `conditional call`, `check first`, `your call`) are successful completions — forcing a pick would delegate JP's values, not the process.

The pipeline: **Generate → Prune → Shape → Recommend → Contest**, orchestrated by a main context that is trust machinery plus exactly one named judgment surface — setup decomposition, constrained under Invocation contract and echo. Generate, Shape, and Recommend read and execute the live constituent contracts (`ideate`, `option-shaping`, `making-recommendations`); Prune and Contest are the only methods this skill owns, and the only deliberate-owned deviations from constituent behavior are the named composition seams (see Composition seams).

## The settled want (from the shaping arc)

- One invocation, no mid-run semantic intervention: the workflow never asks the user a mid-run decision question and judges alone when work is ready to cross each lane boundary. Host permission prompts or denials are runtime behavior outside this promise, handled under Stage rules — the contract never claims the runtime will not prompt.
- Decisive autonomous pruning at the `ideate` → `option-shaping` boundary, in the labeled-lean form: cuts by confirmed filters, fact-established judgments, and disclosed judgment cuts — never scores or invented weights.
- Every post-field exclusion gets an explicit, contestable reason plus a revival condition (options collapsed inside `ideate`'s private de-cluster never entered the field; user seeds are collapse-exempt, so nothing user-supplied dies off-ledger). The ledger is operational: revival conditions are re-run triggers, and re-run cost is the accepted safety price.
- JP's authorship relocates rather than disappears: front-loaded into the invocation contract, post-hoc into the ledger.
- Two-way door under the repo's build-and-prune model; the decisive dial is calibrated from run evidence.

## The authority model

The bounded grant, with the value-trade guard stated inside it:

> The invocation delegates stage transitions and field widening and narrowing under the echoed constraints and survivor budget. It does not authorize invented hard constraints, unstated value exchange rates — including a budget cut whose only defensible basis would price a trade the user never stated — evidence access beyond the echoed scope, side effects, or silent resolution of an option collision. Missing authority survives into an honest exit or close; it is never filled in.

Only explicitly supplied or directly evidenced confirmations become price-confirmed constraints; `inferred` constraints and values stay soft context and never authorize a hard cut.

The ledger separates **permission to decide from authorship of the decision** via the exclusion record's independent axes. Equivalence and dominance are agent determinations even when well grounded, and applying a confirmed constraint to a specific option still contains an agent judgment about predicate satisfaction.

## Invocation contract and echo

The invocation carries:

- **The decision frame** — mandatory; indiscernible → ask once before the run starts (pre-run, not mid-run).
- **Field mode** — `seed-and-widen` (default: supplied candidates seed Generate, which widens around them) or `closed-to-widening` (supplied field skips Generate and goes to Prune; required or echoed whenever candidates are supplied; echo states plainly: *closed to widening; the delegated Prune move may still narrow this field*).
- **Confirmed constraints**, each at its price. Unconfirmed constraints demote to soft preferences that never gate alone.
- **Stated values** (optional) — pre-answered exchange rates. Absent ones route the close toward `conditional call` / `your call`.
- **Soft preferences** (optional, labeled as such) — context, never gates.
- **Stakes context** (optional) — reversibility, stakes, blast radius. Echoed with provenance like every field; when absent, the echo says so and Recommend applies its own door-reading to the decision frame — the orchestrator never invents stakes.
- **Evidence inputs** — supplied facts, named paths, attached material, a compact capsule of relevant conversation context — kept separate from **evidence authorization**. Default: *supplied evidence inputs and named paths may be inspected by default; no additional sources, web research, or probes are authorized unless echoed.* Authorization may extend only to read-only inspection, web research, or explicitly non-mutating probes; experiments, prototypes, and side-effecting checks stay outside the run, and Recommend returns them as `check first`.
- **Survivor budget** — default 4; a provisional capacity budget, explicitly not a claim about correct comparison width.
- **Inline-degradation permission** (optional).
- **Re-run payload** (optional): a pasted prior capsule plus directives — directive-free only when the capsule records an unfinished run (Preflight).

The contract echo labels every material field `user-supplied`, `inferred`, `default`, or `absent`. Visible setup, correctable by live interruption where the session supports it, otherwise by re-run — no universal interrupt promise.

**Preflight** (pre-spend, before any stage launches): the run rejects an invocation whose fields cannot produce a valid run — `closed-to-widening` with an empty candidate set, a survivor budget that is not an integer of at least two (the floor of two makes smaller budgets unsatisfiable by delegated cuts), a re-run payload whose capsule fails validation under the current validator — a capsule under a schema version the current validator does not support resumes only through a shipped migration, otherwise it is refused here, never reinterpreted — a completed-run re-run payload carrying no directives (a completed run's pasted capsule alone requests nothing new — its close and capsule are already in the paste — while an unfinished-run capsule, one whose recorded terminal is a failure terminal or whose stored exclusion check line is `exclusion check unavailable`, is itself the resume request and passes directive-free as an implicit resume directive: the Re-runs rule), a re-run directive normalization cannot classify (the Re-runs rule), a re-run directive changing field mode to `closed-to-widening` without naming its candidate base (the Re-runs rule), or a declared-bound breach (Declared bounds below) — as the terminal `invalid invocation: <reason>`, echo only, no stage spend. An invalid invocation never masquerades as a zero-survivor or any other run outcome. Preflight also proves the validation helper runs and bootstrap-verifies (Stage output envelope): a helper that cannot execute — or that cannot be bootstrap-verified because the platform hasher is unavailable — is the capability-unavailable exit, pre-spend, and inline-degradation permission never waives it — the permission covers isolation, never mechanical validation. Setup creates the run-state store only after every other pre-spend check has passed (the re-run refusals included) and before any stage launches — a rejected invocation never creates a store; any failure there before the store durably holds its first write — creation, orphan retirement, or the echo write — is the pre-spend exit `store unavailable`, echo only (Run-state store). A pre-field failure capsule (`Original field: not produced`) is valid resume input, not a validation failure; its resume restarts at Generate.

**Declared bounds** (each a correctable invocation default, echoed when it binds): in-packet evidence — pasted facts, attachments, the conversation-context capsule — carries at most 256 KiB in total, and a larger payload is preflight `invalid invocation` naming the bound (supply the material as a named path instead, which travels by identifier and is read from disk); a named directory expands to at most 512 descendant files at setup, past which the pin is preflight `invalid invocation` (at re-run, `evidence unavailable`) — name a narrower path; the named evidence set carries a total expanded-byte bound of 64 MiB across every named file after directory expansion, past which setup is preflight `invalid invocation` naming the bound (at re-run, `evidence unavailable`), so content-identity hashing is work-bounded before it starts, never only count-bounded; a stage returns at most 32 retrievals, and an envelope past the cap fails validation as `stage failed: <stage>`; the capsule stores verbatim the initial invocation wording plus the most recent eight re-run directive texts, older directive texts collapsing to their content identifiers with the collapse named in the echo and proof boundary; and the whole rendered capsule carries at most 256 KiB — when construction would breach it, superseded history compacts in the declared order (Close and recovery capsule) with each collapse named, a capsule that cannot fit after full compaction closes honestly as `capsule bound exceeded` naming what remains (its branch row below), and a pasted capsule past the same bound is a preflight bound breach — so the capsule itself is bounded, not merely its directive history, and no history is silently dropped.

**Setup decomposition** (pre-run, before any stage launches): the orchestrator decomposes the invocation into a **candidate-free decision frame** — the decision restated so it names no candidate — the **candidate set** per field mode, and **per-candidate authority notes**: visible lean, stated preference, valuing language, each labeled `user-supplied` or `inferred` — plus the **stakes context** (reversibility, stakes, blast radius), extracted span-backed under the same rule, `absent` when no span grounds it. The raw invocation wording survives only in the capsule, stored non-recursively (see the capsule inventory); no stage packet carries it. This decomposition is what keeps stage isolation satisfiable when the invocation itself names candidates: candidate identity and candidate-attached authority travel per candidate, so any packet can carry exactly its stage's share. The decomposition is judgment, not transport, and is constrained: every authority note cites the invocation or echo span that grounds it; language too ambiguous to ground a note resolves to `absent`, never to an inferred lean; and the decomposition is displayed with the contract echo, correctable the same way. Valuing language is extracted into authority notes; where it is inseparable from a candidate's identity, the candidate's wording travels intact and the proof boundary names the wording-borne lean residual. Soft preferences are split the same way: candidate-attached preference language moves into that candidate's authority note and out of the `soft-prefs` item, which is candidate-free by construction. The split keeps both halves alive: the candidate association (the lean itself) lives only in the authority note, while any candidate-neutral comparison criterion the same language carries — "I lean A because ongoing operating burden matters" yields the criterion "ongoing operating burden matters" — is extracted, span-backed under the same rule, into `soft-prefs` (or into stated values when it prices an exchange rate), so Shape develops the criterion the user actually cares about without learning whose case it strengthens. Stated values are decomposed the same way and are candidate-free by construction in the matrix. Criterion language inseparable from a candidate's identity stays whole in the authority note, and the wording-borne residual in the proof boundary names the loss. The decomposition also extracts the **composition-provenance spans**: the minimal verbatim span of the invocation that names the workflow token (`/deliberate` or `$deliberate`) and the decision frame, and the span covering candidate-selection delegation (the field-mode framing and any supplied-candidate framing), each with candidate identities and lean language elided by named markers — candidate-free under the same rule as authority notes — so Shape's brief can evidence the authorized-composition seam without carrying the raw wording.

## Run shape

Five moves, **all five packet-isolated** in fresh, non-forked stage agents — Contest included, pinned isolated whenever it runs and mandatory on every Contest-eligible branch (the eligibility rule, under Close), so the proof boundary has one story. Stages receive ambient instructions and can read the filesystem; they do not receive earlier stage reasoning unless the seam packet carries it — "blind" is not claimed. Fresh agents unavailable at preflight → capability-unavailable honest exit, pre-spend; capability lost mid-run, after validated artifacts exist → `capability lost mid-run`, carrying the failure capsule. Either exit is avoided only when the invocation permits inline degradation (a named, deliberate divergence from `plan-panel-loop`'s degrade-and-label idiom, because here isolation is load-bearing bias mitigation), which continues the run inline from that point, labeled. The isolation rule throughout: **hide previous-stage judgments, never decision-controlling user authority.**

**Single-sourcing:** Generate reads and executes the current `ideate` contract; Shape reads and executes the current `option-shaping` contract plus its authorized-composition seam; Recommend reads and executes the current `making-recommendations` contract. Prune and Contest are the only methods `deliberate` owns. Methods are read live from the constituent files, never copied into this skill. Where this contract deliberately deviates from a constituent, the deviation is a named composition seam (see Composition seams); everywhere no seam is named, the stage obeys the live constituent.

**Global exit rule:** any constituent honest exit not explicitly transformed by this contract terminates the run as that exit. The orchestrator names the next lane; it never asks a mid-run permission question and never silently enters that lane.

**Stage rules, in every brief:** never ask the user a question — return assumptions, gaps, or an honest exit inside the bounded packet; behavioral containment (read-only, no nested agents or workflows, one bounded packet) is always included and is distinguished from **runtime-enforced containment**, which is claimed only when tool restrictions, sandboxing, or depth limits actually enforce it. In a git worktree, the orchestrator snapshots worktree state before and after each stage (the `plan-panel-loop` precedent); unauthorized mutation is reported and stops the run — and the snapshot pair is a detector of net Git-visible change only: ignored-file writes, transient writes reverted before the post-check, and effects outside the worktree all escape it, so the proof boundary claims detection at exactly that grain, never read-only execution proved. Stage agents request or preserve the session model where the runtime supports that guarantee; the proof boundary reports the effective model when observable and marks it unknown or overridden otherwise. Each stage's input packet is rendered by the bundled helper, never hand-assembled: the helper renders the stage brief deterministically from the canonical stage-brief template and the run-state store's byte-exact items per that stage's matrix column, refuses the render when a required item is absent, a requested item is off-column, or a requested item set is anything less than the stage's complete include column — a partial packet is never rendered, recorded, or dispatched — and records the rendered brief's content identifier in run state before dispatch — because output-packet validation cannot catch an isolation leak the orchestrator itself introduced on the way in, packet composition is mechanical or unclaimed; the orchestrator dispatching the rendered bytes unaltered stays behavioral, a named residual in the proof boundary. The fixed per-stage checklists authored in the skill — explicit include and withhold lists, never recomposed from memory at run time — are that column's authored rendering. The checklists are exhaustive two-sided lists implementing the stage packet matrix below: an item on neither list is withheld by default, and admitting it is a skill edit, never run-time judgment. Matrix, checklists, the stage-brief template, and schemas are single-sourced in the canonical machine-readable data file (Bundle topology): the validator loads that file rather than carrying its own copy, the file is part of the method identity, and the validation ladder mechanically compares every authored rendering — the matrix table, the five checklists, the schema prose — against it, so a drifted rendering fails the ladder instead of running. The template carries each stage's obligation side as well as its packet: the envelope schema and version, the stage's obliged-artifact list, every bound validation will enforce on the return (the per-stage retrieval cap), and the `retrievals` classification and `encounters` reporting rules — a stage is never held to a bound or shape its brief never stated. At setup the orchestrator resolves the constituent source set once — each constituent `SKILL.md` plus every reference and example file that `SKILL.md` names — and records a compact content identifier for each (one algorithm for the whole contract: Content identity, under Stage output envelope); every stage brief carries its constituent's resolved paths and identifiers, the stage verifies the match before executing (including any referenced file it actually loads), and a mismatch terminates the run as `constituent drift` — the checked-out tree is the live skill source, so a mid-run branch switch or rebase is a real hazard, not a hypothetical. The same pin covers evidence: setup records a content identifier for every named evidence path — the run's evidence identity — every stage brief carries it, the stage verifies each named path it actually loads before executing, and a mismatch terminates the run as `evidence drift`, the same mid-run hazard caught the same way. For a pinned directory, the setup expansion manifest is part of the identity: a stage that loads any descendant verifies it against that manifest, and an added, removed, renamed, or unlisted descendant is the same `evidence drift`. The pin also covers `deliberate`'s own method: the method identity (capsule inventory) spans every deliberate-owned behavior surface including the bundled validator, is recorded at setup, and is verified before every stage-boundary reread of the skill's own references and before every validator invocation, the validator's own identifier checked with the platform hasher, never the validator itself (the bootstrap rule under Stage output envelope) — a mismatch terminates the run as `method drift`, the same branch-switch hazard aimed at the skill itself. Web retrievals are stage-local by design: a stage's web facts enter shared run state only through its returned packet artifact, carrying source, retrieval time, and `concerns`, and no stage inherits another stage's raw retrievals. Host permission prompts are runtime behavior, not contract questions: the host may surface tool-approval prompts for a stage's operations, which violates nothing — a stage still never asks the user anything itself, an operation the host denies is absorbed as an evidence gap or honest exit inside the bounded packet, and a stage that cannot complete without the denied operation fails under the operational failure rule. The no-questions rule is a semantic promise; it never claims the runtime will not prompt.

**Progress visibility:** inherit host behavior; report stage transitions and counts without revealing hidden judgments — "Generated 9 options; pruning now," "Shaping 4 survivors."

**Operational failure rule** (small and global): the orchestrator validates each returned stage envelope against the output contract (Stage output envelope below) before accepting it; a failed, timed-out, or malformed stage → `stage failed: <stage>`; unauthorized mutation → `containment violation`, closing with a dirty-state receipt — the changed paths, the pre- and post-stage snapshot identities, the stage charged, and the statement that side effects outside the worktree remain unverified — and stopping for user direction: the orchestrator never silently restores the worktree and never adopts the mutation; only validated partial artifacts are preserved — carried in a **failure capsule**, the standard capsule shape with `not produced` marking every artifact the run never validated and the terminal recorded, because the pasted capsule is the only defined resume input — and any later resume restarts at the earliest stage whose artifact is absent or invalid. The failure capsule exists only while the recovery machinery itself survives: when the failed component is the validation helper (cannot run, cannot be bootstrap-verified because the platform hasher is unavailable, or is itself the drifted surface) or the run-state store's read path, the run closes instead with an **emergency receipt** — echo, terminal name, failed component, last validated stage by name, and the statement that no resumable capsule exists — explicitly non-resumable, never validated, never claiming capsule shape; pasting a receipt is preflight `invalid invocation`, and the next run starts from scratch. Recovery never depends on the component that just failed. If Contest alone fails on a Contest-eligible branch, the run reports `exclusion check unavailable` and never claims anything about exclusion stability.

The moves:

1. **Generate** — receives the frame, constraints (kept visible: ideate uses them as relaxation and inversion targets), seeds if any, and the generation-controlling supplied context per its matrix row: the evidence inputs whole and the stated values — never candidate-specific prior judgments, and never an orchestrator-side relevance filter over the evidence. Returns the un-ranked field plus the untouched-fixed-points line. **User-supplied seeds are collapse-exempt:** `ideate`'s de-cluster never merges, drops, or renames a seed in private scratch — a seed sharing a mechanism with another option survives to the field, and the shared mechanism passes to Prune as a recordable equivalence or collision judgment. A seed dies only on Prune's ledger; anything else is the silent option-collision resolution the authority grant forbids.
2. **Prune** — receives the field with per-candidate provenance flags, its matrix share of the effective contract, and the prune method below — never the authority notes or the soft preferences: no decomposition-controlled candidate-attached preference reaches Prune's packet (in-packet supplied evidence is the read-isolation residual), so it cuts **candidate-attached-lean-blind and value-aware** — stated values are deliberately in its column, because the value-trade guard cannot tell a resolved trade from an unresolved one without them (the evidence-content residual is the read-isolation class) — and seed protection runs on provenance flags, which carry identity, not preference. Returns survivors plus labeled exclusion records, plus any disclosed budget overflow with its blocked-cut disclosures.
3. **Shape** — receives the frozen survivors, frame, constraints, stated values, candidate-free soft preferences, including comparison cues extracted from candidate-attached language at setup — context for deriving live comparison questions, never gates — stakes context, evidence inputs, evidence authorization, and the **composition-provenance item**: the verbatim minimal spans, candidate and lean language elided by named markers, evidencing the user's explicit workflow invocation and the delegation covering candidate selection — extracted at setup decomposition, because the `option-shaping` seam's authorization is evidenced, never asserted, and a brief carrying no evidence falls back to user-provenance behavior. Hidden: the wider field, the kills, Prune's reasoning. Constraint-consequence ownership, split explicitly: a consequence of an **echoed, price-confirmed constraint** → record it, preserve the candidate, Recommend owns the filter; an **unconfirmed or newly inferred constraint** → no cut ever, and an authority-gap exit only if honest comparison cannot proceed; a **collision requiring merge, drop, or changed identity** → `field collision unresolved`, while recordable collisions are recorded and development continues.
4. **Recommend** — receives the comparison surface plus the complete authority packet: the **candidate-free decision frame** from setup decomposition; the **echoed price-confirmed constraints**; **stated values**; **candidate-free soft preferences** (context, never gates); each survivor's original wording and the survivors' relative ordering **with an order-provenance field** — `user-supplied order — may evidence lean` or `Generate-produced order — non-evaluative; never evidence of user lean` — plus explicit insertion provenance for any candidate that did not ride the original field order (revived, accepted, or adopted), which Recommend never reads as lean unless the user chose the position; the survivors' **per-candidate authority notes** with provenance — this is where `making-recommendations`' register-the-lean move gets its user lean; **any disclosed budget overflow**, carried as the named exception to the Prune-judgment withhold so the unpriced trade is posed priced — the blocked trade's existence and identity cross, Prune's per-cut reasoning does not; reversibility, stakes, and blast-radius context; evidence inputs, evidence gaps, and evidence authorization. Withheld, exhaustively: the raw invocation wording, excluded identities, excluded candidates' authority notes (they route to Contest), the excluded-associated `retrievals` share (it routes to Contest and the capsule), and every other Generate/Prune judgment — if the user's visible favorite was excluded, Contest owns that challenge. Owns filters on recorded constraint consequences and collapsed survivors, and all honest exits; a discovered unshaped alternative that could win → field-not-ready exit, recorded as a provisional rerun seed in Generate's minimum option shape (handle, core idea, distinct bet). Every exclusion Recommend creates — a filter on a recorded constraint consequence, a fact-established dominance end, a survivor collapse, or the rivals set aside by an `only one serious option` close — appends a disposition record to the ledger in the labeled record shape before the close is valid; the orchestrator routes each newly excluded candidate's authority note into Contest's packet, and the close carries the record's revival condition.
5. **Contest** — packet-isolated; runs on every Contest-eligible branch (the eligibility rule, under Close). Receives its matrix share of the effective contract, the full active ledger — Prune's exclusion records plus every Recommend disposition record, each carrying its excluded option's complete original wording; a record historized by revival is capsule-only history and never enters Contest's packet — the **excluded candidates' authority notes** (Prune-excluded from setup decomposition, Recommend-excluded routed by the orchestrator after Recommend returns), and — when they exist — the comparison surface and close (`not produced` otherwise), plus, on every close-less eligible terminal, the `terminal-claim` item: the terminal's claim verbatim, carrying the surviving candidate's identity on the one-survivor branch, so Contest tests the exclusions against a proposition its packet actually carries — on zero survivors, whether any recorded premise being wrong would revive a candidate; on one survivor, whether one would restore a rival to the named survivor. Detection only: identifies every recorded exclusion premise or revival condition the final logic — or terminal claim — makes live, names the one most worth contesting whenever any live challenge exists, never compares unshaped exclusions to shaped survivors, never substitutes a recommendation. **An excluded candidate carrying a visible user preference is always a live challenge**, whether or not its kill premise is load-bearing in the final logic.

## Stage output envelope

Every stage returns one transport envelope — a single fenced YAML document under the versioned schema `deliberate-envelope/v1` (UTF-8, fixed key set, prose values as block scalars; the schema ships in `references/schemas.md`) — transport, never a semantic form the judgment agent fills to feel done. Its fields:

- `status` — a three-class grammar validation enforces mechanically: `completed`, `exit: <the named honest exit>`, or `failed: <reason>`; any other form fails the stage, so an arbitrary status can never disable the artifact obligations keyed to it. A completed stage's conditional artifacts are values or `not applicable`, never `not produced`.
- `artifacts` — every artifact the stage's contract obliges (Generate: field plus fixed-points line; Prune: survivors, exclusion records, any overflow disclosure with its blocked-cut disclosures; Shape: comparison surface, recorded constraint consequences; Recommend: close, the two registered leans (the first-lean seam under Composition seams), any disposition records, any provisional seed; Contest: the exclusion check line), each present or marked `not produced: <reason>`.
- `retrievals` — every web fact the stage retrieved, each with source, retrieval time, and `concerns` (`candidate-neutral`, or every candidate the fact names, evidences, or was retrieved to investigate); `none` otherwise. Accepted retrievals join run state and travel onward only through the matrix's `retrievals` row, in the shares their effective `concerns` (Stage packet matrix) permit.
- `encounters` — withheld-class or instruction-like material met inside evidence, per the read-isolation rule; `none` otherwise.
- `pins` — the constituent and evidence identifiers the stage actually verified.
- `model` — the effective model when observable; `unknown` otherwise.

**Content identity, one algorithm:** a content identifier is the full SHA-256 of the target's raw bytes (displayed 12-hex-truncated in chat, stored full in envelopes and the capsule). Path-kind rules: a named path must resolve, symlinks followed, to a regular file — hashed directly — or a directory, expanded once at setup into its recursive regular files in sorted relative-path order, each pinned individually; anything else is unpinnable — preflight `invalid invocation` at setup, `evidence unavailable` at re-run. Identity is byte-exact and runtime-neutral, so a Claude-produced capsule and a Codex re-resolution of the same input agree or the drift is real.

Validation is mechanical, runs before acceptance, and is executed by the bundled helper — `scripts/deliberate-validate.py` (PEP 723), invoked identically from both runtimes for stage-brief rendering (pre-dispatch packet construction from the run-state store and the canonical matrix), envelope validation, capsule validation, and content-identity computation, so packet composition, validation, and identity share one implementation. A refused render is never dispatched: an off-column request is corrected against the matrix and re-rendered, and a required item the store cannot supply is the store read loss (`store failed: read`), never a memory-composed substitute. It checks: the YAML parses against its schema version; every obliged artifact is present or marked `not produced: <reason>` consistent with the declared status; every exclusion or disposition record is complete in the labeled shape, its `Option` byte-identical to the stored original wording — the run-state store below holds the field and survivors, so the comparison is exact, and a paraphrased record is rejected, never forwarded to Contest; Prune's survivors are an order-preserving subsequence of its input field; **Prune's partition is conserved** — every input-field option appears exactly once, as a survivor or as the `option` of exactly one active exclusion record, so a silent drop and a survivor-with-record contradiction both fail mechanically, with duplicate wordings rejected in fields, survivor lists, and candidate sets so the partition is well-defined; the status grammar holds (`completed` / `exit: <name>` / `failed: <reason>`); every run-state item body is validated per kind to its nested shape, and capsule validation checks every key's nested shape plus terminal-artifact consistency — never top-level key names alone; every declared external-fact reliance (an `Evidence provenance` line) is well-formed; and every record citation resolves to a stored or same-envelope retrieval whose effective `concerns` — the acceptance's amendment included — carries that record's option. These are deterministic shape and consistency checks only: the validator cannot establish that a `not produced` reason is honest or detect reliance a record never declared — undeclared reliance is a stage-contract violation for Contest or the user to catch — and the proof boundary claims exactly the mechanical checks, nothing semantic. Anything else is `stage failed: <stage>` — the orchestrator never repairs a packet, invents a missing field, or accepts free-form output. There is no reasoned fallback: orchestrator judgment never stands in for the validator, and a helper that cannot run is the capability-unavailable exit at preflight or `capability lost mid-run` after it — closing with the emergency receipt (Operational failure rule), never with a capsule the dead helper cannot validate.

**Validator boundary:** the helper's defenses are part of this contract, not implementation detail: YAML is parsed with a safe loader — custom tags and object construction rejected, anchors and aliases rejected before expansion, input past a byte or depth cap rejected before parse; schemas bind fixed key sets and reject unknown keys rather than ignoring them; the helper is invoked with an argv list, never through shell interpolation, and treats every path argument as a literal path; every path is canonicalized (symlinks resolved) and checked against the helper's explicit read set before any read — the echoed evidence authorization, the setup expansion manifest, the method-identity surfaces (the canonical data file included), and the run-state store root — and anything outside that set is refused; and the self-hash bootstrap is non-circular — the orchestrator verifies the validator's own content identifier with the platform hasher (`shasum -a 256` or equivalent), never with the validator itself, before every invocation. A must-block/must-pass fixture set (hostile tags, alias expansion, over-cap input, unknown keys, metacharacter paths, off-column packet-item requests; quoted and template-looking inert prose, authorized paths with spaces, in-column renders) ships in the bundle and runs in the validation ladder before the helper is trusted.

**Run-state store (intra-run exact-state authority):** the orchestrator writes the contract echo, the setup decomposition, every validated stage envelope with its owed concerns amendments, and the capsule-in-progress to a run directory outside the repository worktree, each at validation, before it is acted on — a value-preserving re-serialization: every compared value (wordings, records, retrievals, the close) is byte-exact, while document formatting is not preserved and never claimed. Every orchestrator-written item takes its keyed shape from the `deliberate-runstate/v1` schema (canonical data file, Bundle topology) and is helper-validated at write exactly as envelopes are at acceptance — per-kind nested body shapes, not top-level key names alone; a write failing its schema is a store write failure at that point, never adopted — so brief rendering, absence refusal, and the post-compaction echo verification all run against a pinned shape, never a remembered format. Envelope acceptance is one atomic store write: the validated envelope and its owed concerns amendments land in a single item file, written to a temporary name and atomically renamed, so no failure window can make an envelope visible without its amendments. The store — not conversation memory — is the byte-exact authority for later packet composition, for validation's stored-original comparisons (the field and survivors the `Option` check reads), and for capsule construction; after context compaction the orchestrator rebuilds from the store and the re-read references, never from summarized memory. Its locator is deterministic, never remembered: the store lives at the fixed name `deliberate-run-live/` directly under the runtime's ambient session-scoped temporary root — the path the session environment itself supplies, which survives compaction because it is ambient, not conversational — is created at setup with user-only permissions (it holds withheld-class material), and records the contract echo and a run identifier as its first write; post-compaction the orchestrator re-derives root plus fixed name and verifies the stored echo against the run in progress, and a mismatch is a store read failure, never silent adoption. One live store per session: a pre-existing directory at that name is an orphan from an earlier run, retired at setup exactly as a live store is retired at terminal (below) — inert history, never authority, never left to accumulate. The store is intra-run scratch, not a persistence surface, and its cleanup obeys the governing no-`rm` contract: at every terminal — carrier-bearing or echo-only, `capsule bound exceeded` alone waiting on user direction — the store is retired via `trash` — durably relocated to the user's machine-local Trash, never destroyed — strictly after the branch's artifacts, carrier or bare exit, have finished rendering, never before, its path recorded in the proof boundary where a capsule renders and in the rendered terminal otherwise; the claim is exactly that no live store survives the run, and byte destruction is never claimed. The stakes are stage-facing, not user-facing — withheld-class means withheld from stages, and the capsule already hands the user everything the store holds — so retirement into the user's own Trash breaches nothing the isolation model guards. A retirement failure is disclosed in the rendered terminal with the store path, never silently swallowed. A crash or a truncated final turn may orphan the live store; an orphan is inert history, never resume authority — the pasted capsule stays the only resume input — with exactly one sanctioned use: within the same session, on the user's explicit request, the orchestrator may re-render the terminal carrier from an orphan whose stored echo and run identifier match (re-render only, never resume, the orphan retired immediately after). It sits outside the worktree, so the read-only promise (scoped to user-visible state) holds; it holds withheld-class material, so no stage brief names its path, and a stage that reads it anyway is inside the read-isolation encounter rule. Store failure is named for what failed, never charged to a stage: any setup failure before the store durably holds that first write — creation, orphan retirement, or the echo write itself — is the pre-spend exit `store unavailable`, echo only; a mid-run **write** failure is `store failed: write` — the unpersisted artifact is treated as never validated and the standard failure capsule is built from previously stored state with that artifact `not produced`; a mid-run **read** loss is `store failed: read`, closing with the emergency receipt, because a capsule built from a store the run can no longer read would be memory wearing a capsule's shape. The orchestrator never substitutes memory for a byte-exact obligation.

## Composition seams

The named deliberate-owned overrides to constituent behavior — the only ones. Precedence: inside a run these seams outrank the constituent text; everywhere no seam is named the stage obeys the live constituent, and a conflict no seam covers resolves as a constituent honest exit under the global exit rule, never by stage improvisation.

- **All stages:** constituent ask-the-user moves and permissioned handoffs are transformed — questions return inside the bounded packet as assumptions, gaps, or honest exits, and handoffs terminate the run naming the lane. The stage rules and global exit rule above carry this; it is a seam, not an accident.
- **`ideate` (Generate):** user-supplied seeds are collapse-exempt in de-cluster — a seed dies only on Prune's ledger; and ideate's "ask them to fix the candidate set before handoff" is replaced by the delegated Prune stage, because candidate-set fixing is exactly what the invocation authorized.
- **`option-shaping` (Shape):** the provenance seam ships as a source edit (see The constituent edit) because it changes what the skill accepts standalone; the evidence reaching Shape is the `composition-provenance` packet item — the elided verbatim invocation and delegation spans from setup decomposition — because authorization is evidenced, never asserted; the constraint-consequence split and collision terminals in the Shape move are run-context packet rules layered on top.
- **`making-recommendations` (Recommend):** register-the-lean reads the extracted per-survivor authority notes, never raw invocation wording — and the agent-side first lean is its own named seam: the constituent registers that lean before any structured comparison, but this composition delivers Shape's completed surface in the packet, so the Recommend brief orders the read — register the first lean from frame, constraints, values, soft preferences, authority notes, and survivor wordings **before reading the `surface` and `consequences` items** — and the returned envelope records both registered leans as an obliged artifact; the ordering inside one context is behavioral, not mechanical, and the proof boundary says so (the surface arriving rank-free under `option-shaping`'s contract is the seam's mitigation, not its proof); unstable-fact verification and cheap-check recommendations are bounded by the echoed evidence authorization — an unauthorized check returns as `check first`, never performed; field-readiness failure exits as field-not-ready with a provisional rerun seed; the constituent's add-a-distinct-alternative and null-option move never adds a candidate to the comparison — an alternative that could realistically win is the field-not-ready exit, and one that merely reveals a constraint or would change the recommendation enters the close only as a named agent-derived consideration, never silently; and every post-Prune exclusion — filter, dominance end, collapse, or the rivals of an `only one serious option` close — owes a ledger disposition record in the labeled record shape, with the excluded candidate's authority note routed onward to Contest: the constituent may drop an option mid-comparison, but inside this run nothing disappears off-ledger.

Only `option-shaping` warrants a source edit: its user-provenance assumptions govern standalone behavior, while the `ideate` and `making-recommendations` overrides are pure run-context transformations that leave the standalone contracts untouched.

## Prune method

Independent cuts — permitted regardless of budget — are exactly:

- Applications of echoed price-confirmed constraints (predicate application recorded as agent judgment).
- **Fact-established** mechanism-equivalence: a nameable shared failure reason. When equivalence holds between a user-supplied seed and a generated option, the generated option is the one cut — the seed's original wording is what Recommend's authority packet preserves.
- **Fact-established** dominance at comparable resolution.

A contestable sketch-depth dominance impression never triggers a cut by itself — it may appear only as the disclosed rationale inside a budget-forced cut. There is no "retired non-serious" class: options that read as non-serious at sketch depth are exactly where anti-modal kills would launder, so they die only as fully recorded judgment cuts.

Budget cuts, when survivors still exceed the budget, carry two guards:

- **The value-trade guard:** each cut must be defensible without pricing an unresolved value trade. When the budget cannot be met without pricing one, the run does not invent the weight and does not immediately die: it **overflows the budget with disclosure** — carrying the un-cuttable survivors into Shape so Recommend can pose the trade priced — up to twice the echoed budget (a correctable default). Beyond that, the honest terminal `survivor budget cannot be met without an unstated value trade`. Value boundary outranks capacity preference; the budget was explicitly capacity, not epistemics.
- **Honest disclosure wording:** every budget cut is disclosed as a low-confidence cut of a **distinct mechanism-level candidate whose seriousness was unresolved at sketch depth** — never "a distinct serious bet," which claims what sketch depth cannot establish.

**Floor of two:** only a cut whose predicate source is a direct user rule *and* whose epistemic status is fact-established may reduce the field below two; budget cuts never may.

**Order invariant:** survivors are an order-preserving subsequence of the input field — Prune cuts, never reorders — and validation enforces the subsequence check mechanically: a reordering envelope fails as `stage failed: prune`. Position provenance survives mixed histories: a revived candidate rejoins at its original field position, and a candidate accepted or adopted into a closed field carries explicit insertion provenance (user-chosen position, or appended-by-rule), so the order-provenance field downstream never launders an agent-produced order into user lean.

Every exclusion is a compact labeled record — prose inside each value, the warning and provenance lines omitted when inapplicable, no scores:

```text
Option:                <complete original wording, provenance flag intact — validation rejects paraphrase>
Status:                <active | revived — historized by a revival directive: capsule history only, outside Contest eligibility and every packet>
Delegation:            <what the invocation authorized here>
Predicate source:      <direct user rule | agent-derived proposition>
Cut basis:             <constraint | equivalence | dominance | survivor budget | post-prune filter | post-prune dominance | post-prune collapse | only-serious-option rival>
Epistemic status:      <fact-established at comparable resolution | contestable sketch-depth judgment>
Reason:
Evidence provenance:   <source and retrieval time for each external fact the reason or epistemic status relies on>
Load-bearing premise:
Strongest case:        <written before the kill>
Revive if:
Evidence warning:
```

Recommend's disposition records use the same template with the post-prune and rival cut bases (`only-serious-option rival` covers the rivals an `only one serious option` close sets aside); `Delegation` still names what the invocation authorized there, and `Revive if` is still mandatory. A budget cut the value-trade guard blocks is not an exclusion and never takes this shape: it is a **blocked-cut disclosure** — the candidate, the unpriced trade, why the cut was blocked — a separate artifact riding with the overflow disclosure, outside the ledger and outside Contest's eligibility basis, because nothing was excluded.

## Close and recovery capsule

Close order:

1. **Exclusion check** — exactly one of: `Exclusion check: no live recorded challenge found`; `Exclusion check: live recorded challenges — X, Y`, naming the kill most worth contesting; `Exclusion check: not applicable — no exclusions recorded` (branches where Prune never cut); or `exclusion check unavailable` (Contest failed). **Eligibility:** Contest runs on every branch whose terminal or close carries at least one validated exclusion record with `Status: active` (a record historized as `revived` never confers eligibility, and a blocked-cut disclosure is not an exclusion record), except the failure terminals (`stage failed`, `store failed`, `containment violation`, `constituent drift`, `method drift`, `evidence drift`, `capability lost mid-run`), which stop the run immediately, and the pre-stage re-run refusals (`authority conflict`, `evidence unavailable`): no stage ran this run and the records they carry are the prior run's, so an exclusion check there would claim work this run never did. Detection language only; works under every close shape and every eligible terminal.
2. The recommendation or honest exit — any of the four `making-recommendations` close shapes is a successful completion.
3. **The recovery capsule**, one paste-able fenced YAML document under the versioned schema `deliberate-capsule/v1`, validated by the same bundled helper (chat-first; written to a file only on request). Every rendered carrier — capsule, failure capsule, emergency or bound-exceeded receipt — ends with a completeness terminator as its final key (for capsules, `capsule-complete`, carrying a content identifier over the document body above it), so truncation is visible at sight and a terminator-less or identifier-mismatched paste fails validation as incomplete rather than resuming from a partial document; the store is retired only after the carrier has finished rendering, never before (Run-state store).

The capsule stores the **complete effective contract**: frame; field mode; priced constraints; stated values; soft preferences; evidence inputs and authorization; the **evidence identity** — a content identifier for every named path at run time, source and retrieval time for web research, and for every in-packet input (pasted facts, attachments, the conversation-context capsule) a content identifier over the exact bytes the run-state store holds for it — an input the orchestrator cannot byte-serialize records `no comparable identity` instead — with attachments and conversation capsules still marked `not re-resolvable` (identifiers and provenance only; the capsule never embeds source contents); the **method identity** — a content identifier for every deliberate-owned behavior surface: `SKILL.md`, each behavior-shaping reference it ships, and the bundled validator script, recorded at setup beside the constituent pins; survivor budget; inline-degradation permission; the **invocation wording, stored non-recursively** (capsule-only — no stage packet carries it): the initial run's raw wording, each re-run's directive text within the declared directive-history bound (older texts collapse to their content identifiers, the collapse named in the echo), and the pasted source capsule's content identifier — never a pasted capsule body, and never a supplied evidence payload (attachment and conversation-capsule bodies are identity-only under the evidence identity; inline facts the user typed into the invocation are contract wording and stay); the setup decomposition (candidate-free frame, per-candidate authority notes with provenance, per-candidate provenance flags, the composition-provenance spans, stakes); the full Recommend authority packet (survivor wordings, ordering with provenance, per-survivor authority notes, any overflow disclosure, stakes/reversibility); and **Recommend's registered leans**, verbatim when produced, so the capsule is closed over every envelope-recorded artifact. Each effective-contract field stores its echoed provenance label, so an import restores the exact echo. Then the field, stored separately from its provenance:

```text
Original field:       <complete generated or user-supplied field — present whenever any field was validated; `not produced: <reason>` only on a failure before Generate returned a validated field>
Generation boundary:  <untouched-fixed-points line | Generate not run: closed-to-widening | not produced>
```

Then prior survivors, any disclosed budget overflow with its blocked-cut disclosures, every exclusion and disposition record with its `Status`, the run's accepted `retrievals` in full — each with producing stage, source, retrieval time, and effective `concerns` — and — verbatim when produced, `not produced` otherwise — Shape's comparison surface and recorded constraint consequences, Recommend's close and terminal claim, and the rendered exclusion check line: the capsule is closed over restart for control state: a resume rebuilds any stage's matrix packet, including a Recommend-only or Contest-only restart, from the capsule plus re-resolved evidence — named paths re-read live against the stored identifiers, attachments and conversation capsules re-supplied by the user, and a required input that resolves neither way exits `evidence unavailable`. The rebuild is executable, not aspirational: the helper's `import-capsule` command validates the pasted capsule under the full nested checks, creates the re-run store, and writes the typed restart state — the echo with per-field provenance restored, the decomposition, the pins, and a `capsule-import` item carrying the prior artifacts — from which `render-brief` composes any restart stage's packet; the renderer reads a prior stage's artifacts from the imported capsule exactly until a re-run envelope for that stage supersedes them, and no prior envelope is ever synthesized. The capsule alone never claims to stand in for evidence it deliberately does not embed. Then any provisional rerun seed (marked unaccepted), revival instructions, and, as its final field, **the single proof boundary** — packet isolation achieved or not (claimed only when every stage that ran was fresh on a helper-rendered brief whose render identifier is recorded in run state, with intentionally skipped stages listed and the behavioral residuals named: verbatim dispatch of the rendered bytes, and evidence-content exposure under the read-isolation line), the read-isolation line (`packet-field isolation only; evidence-content encounters: none reported | <listed>`), the pinned constituent source set (the full resolved set: paths and content identifiers), the method identity, effective models when observable, evidence scope actually used, containment class (behavioral vs runtime-enforced), the run-state store path, and what none of it proves. Nothing outside the capsule re-renders the proof boundary; a close that displays it displays the capsule's field verbatim. `not generated` never means `field unavailable`.

**History compaction**, when the whole-capsule bound binds (Declared bounds), runs in a fixed order — older directive texts beyond the verbatim eight (the standing rule), then `revived`-historized records, then prior-run retrieval fact bodies (source, retrieval time, and `concerns` always retained) — each item collapsing to its content identifier with the collapse named in the proof boundary; a capsule that still cannot fit is the terminal `capsule bound exceeded` (branch table): the close and exclusion check stand, the bound-exceeded receipt names what remains oversized, and the store is retained until the user directs a file-written complete capsule or declines — the one terminal where retirement waits on direction, because teardown never precedes the recovery carrier's availability. Collapsed history never becomes authority again: it is identity-only, enters no packet, and a resume that would need it treats it as `not re-resolvable` evidence.

## Re-runs

One principle: **restart at the earliest stage whose input or required artifact became invalid.** The transitions below are derived from the stage packet matrix — a changed input invalidates the earliest stage whose column carries it, and any narrower transition states its testable reason inline. Where Generate never ran (`closed-to-widening`), a restart at Generate resolves to Prune.

A payload's directives are normalized before any restart derivation, in a fixed application order: first, contract-field changes (frame, field mode, constraints, values, evidence and authorization, budget, soft preferences, stakes, declared bounds, degradation permission) produce the effective re-run contract; second, revival directives apply against the active record set under that effective contract — the `authority conflict` lookup runs here, so a payload that revives a constraint-cut option and withdraws or reprices that constraint in the same breath passes; third, provisional-seed acceptances apply; fourth, the implicit resume directive — an unfinished-run capsule (its recorded terminal in the failure set: `stage failed`, `store failed: write`, `containment violation`, `constituent drift`, `method drift`, `evidence drift`, `capability lost mid-run`; or its stored exclusion check line `exclusion check unavailable`) requests its missing artifacts by construction, no typed directive required. A directive that resolves to none of these classes is preflight `invalid invocation` naming the unclassifiable directive, never guessed into a class. The restart stage is the earliest stage any applied change invalidates — matrix-derived for contract fields, drift-mapped for source drift, the drift rule for evidence, the earliest absent-or-invalid obliged artifact for the resume class. When that restart is Generate, revived and accepted options enter Generate as collapse-exempt seeds on user authority — the seeds item renders every non-`generated` provenance flag (`user-seed`, `revived`, `accepted`, `adopted`), so a user-authorized candidate never silently drops from regeneration; rejoin-at-original-position applies only when the stored field itself is reused — historized records stay historized, and revival's pin against delegated cuts survives regeneration. A declared-bound change applies at preflight: a loosened bound is execution-only and preserves every artifact; a tightened bound a stored artifact breaches is preflight `invalid invocation` naming the bound.

Evidence authority is live, checked by identity: at re-run the orchestrator re-resolves every named evidence path and compares content identifiers against the capsule's evidence identity, and re-supplied in-packet material (attachments, conversation capsules, pasted facts) is hashed over its bytes as received and compared the same way — a mismatch, or any input whose stored identity is `no comparable identity`, is changed evidence restarting at its first consuming stage, never inheriting the artifacts the original produced. Drift at an unchanged path invalidates every stored artifact built downstream of that evidence; the matrix routes the evidence item to every stage, so drift in run-supplied evidence restarts at the first stage that ran, and an input first added by a prior re-run directive restarts where it first entered. (Mid-run, the same mismatch is the terminal `evidence drift`; at re-run, drift is expected and routes here.) A required evidence input that no longer resolves — a missing path, an expired attachment marked `not re-resolvable` — exits as `evidence unavailable` rather than silently reusing the stored field. Web-research facts are not re-fetched wholesale: the capsule's retrieval-time provenance makes their age visible; staleness in the survivor share routes through Recommend's unstable-fact rule under the echoed authorization — the only share Recommend's packet carries — and excluded-fact freshness is user-owned by disclosure: no stage revalidates a fact that supports only an active exclusion record (detection-only Contest and the no-auto-revival boundary both stand), so every re-run echo surfaces each active record-cited retrieval with its source and retrieval time, and a revival directive stays the lane for acting on a fact the user knows has moved.

- Only a revival directive changed, and no accepted provisional seed is active → skip Generate and Prune; revival is a full active-state transition, then a cardinality branch — never an unconditional Shape dispatch. The transition: the revived option's exclusion record is marked `revived` — retained in the capsule as history, no longer an active exclusion, out of Contest's eligibility basis and excluded share; its authority note moves to the survivor share; the option rejoins at its original field position on user authority. Then the standing cardinality branches apply to the resulting survivor set: two or more → Shape runs on it; exactly one → the one-survivor terminal, Shape and Recommend never launch; Contest, when eligible, receives only the still-active records.
- Frame or field-mode change → regenerate — for field mode, deliberately wider than the matrix derivation (Prune is its earliest packet consumer), stated reason: field mode decides whether Generate runs at all, so changing it re-decides the field's origin, not just its cuts. A field-mode change also names its candidate base explicitly. Landing on `closed-to-widening`, the directive states which field closes — `prior seeds only` (generated options drop, the drop named in the echo), `prior full field` (generated options are thereby adopted as user-fixed candidates, the adoption named in the echo), or a newly supplied candidate list — then resolves to Prune under the standing Generate-never-ran rule; a mode-change directive without a base is preflight `invalid invocation`, never a silent default. Landing on `seed-and-widen`, the prior candidate set rides as collapse-exempt seeds by default — widening only adds, so no base question arises.
- Constraint change → regenerate under `seed-and-widen`: the matrix routes constraints to Generate as generation-controlling context, and `ideate` relaxes, moves, and inverts them as provocations, so a changed constraint can produce a mechanism-distinct option the stored field never contained — the earlier narrower rationale (field width independent of constraints) is withdrawn as conflicting with the live `ideate` contract, and no narrower reason survives the matrix derivation. Under `closed-to-widening`, the standing Generate-never-ran rule resolves the restart to Prune. No `could this alter the field?` judgment is left to the orchestrator.
- Values change → regenerate: the matrix routes stated values to Generate as generation-controlling context, and no narrower reason survives that.
- Evidence content or authorization change → restart per the drift rule above; an expanded authorization is a Generate-consumed input (new sources can expose mechanism-distinct options), so it regenerates.
- Budget change → re-prune: Prune is the budget's earliest consumer.
- Soft-preference or stakes change → re-shape the stored survivors: Shape is their earliest consumer.
- Degradation-permission-only change (execution-only) → preserve every artifact and re-echo execution mode.
- A revived option is pinned against delegated budget, equivalence, and dominance cuts after any re-prune. Constraint compatibility is never an orchestrator judgment: preflight makes only a mechanical record lookup — a revival directive whose target's active exclusion record carries cut basis `constraint` must also withdraw or reprice that constraint (a contract change, which restarts per the constraint-change transition); a directive that does not is refused as `authority conflict` before any stage launches. Any other conflict between a revived option and an active constraint is found where constraint judgment lives, isolated and ledgered: Shape records the consequence, Recommend owns the filter and its disposition record, and Contest receives the challenge — never a preflight refusal.
- **Accepted provisional seeds split by field mode.** Under `seed-and-widen`, an accepted seed is a new Generate input: Generate reruns and widens around it, collapse-exempt like any seed — the direct-to-Prune shortcut would freeze the field around an option Generate never saw. Under `closed-to-widening`, acceptance is the user adding a candidate to their closed field: the seed joins re-prune directly on user authority, carrying Generate's minimum option shape (handle, core idea, distinct bet), which provisional seeds record by construction.
- A provisional seed stays provisional until the user explicitly accepts it or changes field mode — relaunching with an unrelated directive is not acceptance, and a seed never auto-widens a `closed-to-widening` field.
- A re-run re-resolves and re-pins the live constituents and `deliberate`'s own surfaces against the capsule's recorded identities; those identifiers are evidence of what governed the prior run, never a freeze against evolution. Drift is disclosed in the echo and proof boundary **and converted into invalidation by the source-drift map**, total over the pinned method-identity surfaces at the same file grain the pins record — each surface maps to the earliest stage its change invalidates: `ideate` sources → Generate; `option-shaping` sources → Shape; `making-recommendations` sources → Recommend; `references/methods.md` → Prune (the pinned identity is a whole-file hash that cannot attribute a change to one method, so the earliest method consumer restarts — Contest-only drift is indistinguishable at that grain and deliberately over-invalidates); `SKILL.md`, `references/capsule.md`, the packet matrix and checklists (`references/stage-packets.md`), the canonical data file, `references/schemas.md`, or the validator → no stored artifact is reused past current-validator revalidation, and a capsule under a schema version the current validator does not support and no shipped migration converts is refused at preflight, never reinterpreted. The resume restarts at the earliest of the directive-derived stage and every drifted surface's mapped stage, regenerating the invalidated artifacts under the current method with the drift named — disclosure accompanies invalidation, never substitutes for it, and no artifact produced under a changed method is reused, silently or confessedly. The pasted capsule is orchestrator input: its prior judgments (records, close, proof boundary) enter a stage packet only where that stage's checklist names them.
