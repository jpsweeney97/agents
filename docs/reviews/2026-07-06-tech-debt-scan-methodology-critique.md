## 1. A team's method, distilled twice

## 3. What the vocabulary used to mean

Read the ancestor's artifacts and the instruments are alive. Corroboration meant something no single agent can produce: "3-auditor independent convergence" on the Codex-CLI drift finding; a "fail-open" mischaracterization by one auditor **corrected by another auditor's call-graph trace** and recorded as a resolved contradiction; a suspected god-module investigated and *disconfirmed* "a deliberate non-inflation"; a severity raise **retracted** with rationale ("SY-12's earlier P2→P1 raise has been retracted... the 'fails open' rationale does not apply at this version"). The sanity thresholds were computed and — this is the tell — *argued with*: "Why the watch list is 65% (and why that's not inflation)... it is an accurate portrait of a clean codebase," and the 05-17 audit computed its ratios, named its 48%-watch breach, and defended it. The metrics were labeled honestly as process metadata: "Local synthesis snapshot, not durable proof... treat raw-count, duplicate-merge, corroboration... as on-machine synthesis metadata."

Every one of those sentences describes an epistemic event between minds, or an author bounding what its numbers prove. That is what the scan's counters, labels, and checks are a compressed memory of. The ancestor's rubric taught severity with concrete signals and named inflation traps; the scan's rubric is the skeleton — "Severity: `P0` bleeding today; `P1` compounding; `P2` latent; `P3` cosmetic" (rubric:3) — enforcement thresholds with the teaching deleted. The taxonomy's lenses were questions to ask a codebase ("Are there exported symbols never imported...?"); the scan's are noun lists ("CH complexity/duplication/dead-code/naming/size/smells," taxonomy:5). Under the library's own two-bars lens, the compression cut the provoke and kept the substitute.

## 4. A panel's epistemology worn by one mind

Here is the central seam, earned from this target's text and its one faithful run.

In the ancestor this word meant independence between blind auditors; in the scan the same mind reads both sources, already believing they agree. Two source classes read by one mind is *breadth*, and breadth is worth having; it is not independence, and the label still speaks as if it were.

None of this is prunable on silence alone — the paradigm forbids that move, and the ledger re-read owns usage — but the honest description is that the certification stratum has never certified anything and the panel-residue counters have never counted a panel.

The reframe: this skill believes it is a measurement-and-certification instrument — score the debt, corroborate the findings, certify the artifact `complete` for a future agent. The record says it is, so far, **the house's canonical written answer to how a debt audit would be disciplined, worn as a running method by nothing.** Its scoring-and-certification stratum is a compressed memoir of a six-auditor panel that could cash those words, kept alive by a token-budget optimizer's accident of what survived, certified "Defensible" by fifteen reviews that never ran it, and never once organically fired. The honest repairs are de-certification, not rebuild — the same move baseline's founding made when drift-audit's certification liturgy was killed and the honest core kept:

- **Scope the corroboration claim to what one mind can earn.** `evidence_corroborated` certifies breadth — distinct source classes read by the same judge — not independence; say so where the label is defined, and let `Top Debt Calls` claim "multiply-sourced," not silently borrow a panel's warrant. (The rule's spirit and the singleton demotion stay; they are the gate's real value.)
- **Let the completion stamp say what it is.** `Status: complete` marks the author's own checks passed — an honor-system self-attest, as the 06-15 review named it — not a certification a downstream reader can lean on instead of the Evidence Trail. One sentence at the lifecycle rule; the authority order (:37-41) already points readers at the trail first and is the honest design.
- **Bound the counters the way the ancestor did.** The Metrics packet is local-run metadata ("not durable proof"), not audit evidence; carry that sentence. Fill-or-drop is not the question; honesty about what a filled counter proves is.
- **Restore the single-pass price the compression deleted.** Conservative suppression ("a skipped category cannot be recovered later in a single-pass scan") and the high-stakes ceiling the archival created: a broad high-stakes target deserves the text saying plainly that one capped pass is the library's *only* debt tier now, and that Coverage Gaps carries what the cap cannot.
- **Nothing else.** The named-cost bar, caps, depth split, honest exits, floors, security boundary, template shape, artifact convention, description, and name all stay. No new machinery; the skill's problem was never too little structure.
