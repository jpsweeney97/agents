# Scrutinize-Skill: A Critique of the Methodology and Philosophy

> Saved 2026-07-02. JP asked for `scrutinize-skill` to get the treatment `outcome-interviewer` (`docs/reviews/2026-07-02-outcome-interviewer-methodology-critique.md`, rebuild `26d7ae9`) and `making-recommendations` (`docs/reviews/2026-07-02-making-recommendations-methodology-critique.md`, rebuild `01d9313`) got: pressure on the methodology and philosophy — the epistemology, the stance, the implicit theory of what adversarial review of a skill contract is and can know — not a skill-contract scrutiny, and explicitly not routed through the skill itself. The target is `plugins/review-family/skills/scrutinize-skill/SKILL.md` and its plugin surfaces as of commit `45f28f5`; inline line references resolve against that version (`git show 45f28f5:plugins/review-family/skills/scrutinize-skill/SKILL.md`). JP pre-committed to honoring a non-inversion result, and this critique delivers one. It served as the design brief for the same-day repairs (commit `d7970eb`, review-family 0.6.0), which kept the name, the shape, and the premise.

I read the skill, its metadata, the review-family manifest and changelog, the CANON drift-check script, its full commit history back to the founding version, the skill-usage ledger, and — what the precedent targets never had — the record of its real fires: the 48-skill sweep and its verification aftermath, the making-recommendations re-scrutiny arc, and the judgment-trust apparatus that tested its sharpest lens. What follows is pressure on the method itself.

**TLDR:** This skill survives the treatment that inverted the shaping and deciding lanes, and it survives for a reason worth stating precisely: its method is restraint engineering that *admits* to being restraint engineering. There is no excavation story, no measurement costume — the evidence floor, the proof-class separation, the read-only stance, the no-mental-repair rule are honest about what a text review is. But the treatment finds one structural blindness and two honesty gaps, all three the same shape: **the method is more honest about the target's evidence than about its own.** First, its clearance verdicts silently cover an altitude the review never examines — whether the target's method is *true* — and its sharpest lens actively cannot see that altitude: the bar lens graded MAP "a legitimate noise-reduction forcing function" one day before the methodology critique showed MAP was bias in a lab coat, and both skills later rebuilt for false premises had passed the 48-skill sweep without a Major. Second, its behavioral proof class counts "reasoning" as behavior evidence — the one hatch through which simulation enters wearing observation's badge. Third, its findings and its verdict are authored by the same judge in the same pass, and the recorded fires show that judge is noisy in both directions — yet the text carries no acknowledgment that raw scrutiny findings are argued hypotheses, and its `Required Changes` section commands the author before anything has tested the findings it rests on. The honest reframe is not an inversion; it is a scope amendment: the review grades the contract, not the epistemology, and where a premise smells false its job is to detect and route to the heavier treatment, never to clear.

## 1. What kind of knowledge is a contract review?

The skill's primary question — "will the right skill behave poorly once triggered?" (SKILL.md:22) — is a *forecast*. The review reads text and predicts the behavior of a population it never samples: future agents, across models, contexts, and runtimes, executing under the contract. Every finding of the form "muddled instructions make the agent improvise" (SKILL.md:27) is a prediction about readers the reviewer simulates but does not observe.

The method knows this in its bones — that is what the proof classes are for (SKILL.md:55-59) — and then leaves a hatch open. The `behavioral` class is defined as "realistic dry runs, examples, or reasoning that shows the agent will behave correctly when the skill is triggered" (SKILL.md:58). *Or reasoning.* A dry run is an observation; a worked example is at least an artifact; "reasoning that shows the agent will behave correctly" is the reviewer's imagination of the reader, graded as the same class of proof. This is the single place the method's evidence discipline goes soft, and it is load-bearing: in practice nearly every behavioral claim a scrutiny makes rests on reasoning, because dry runs cost a subagent and the review is one pass. The library owns the settling instruments — `behavior-smoke-test`, the blind forward-test proxies that every recent build runs — and the skill never reaches for them, never even names the cheapest check that would turn a reasoned prediction into an observed one. In a repo whose global rule is "do not claim work is done, fixed, or passing without fresh evidence," the skill that audits *other* skills' validation claims (SKILL.md:31) lets its own sharpest claims ride on simulation filed under `behavioral`.

The repair is not to demand dry runs — a one-pass review that must spawn proxies stops being cheap enough to fire nineteen times a month. It is to stop laundering: a reasoned prediction is a distinct evidence grade, named as such, carrying the check that would settle it.

## 2. The altitude ceiling, demonstrated

Here the track record does what no close reading could. On 2026-07-01, a seven-lens scrutiny of the making-recommendations build (`wf_f95f7d28-628`, 14 agents) ran this skill's method at full strength — and its judgment-bar lens examined the MAP scoring machinery directly and *cleared it*: "a LEGITIMATE noise-reduction forcing function (provokes better scoring, does not make the call for the agent), NOT over-machinery" (handoff 2026-07-01_00-42-19, §3). One day later, the methodology critique showed the same machinery was a noise cure transplanted into a context with no noise to cancel — bias laundered into measurement costume — and the skill was rebuilt around its removal (`01d9313`). The scrutiny was not sloppy; it was *right at its own altitude*. MAP-as-text really does provoke scoring discipline. Whether the scores constitute knowledge is a question the bar lens does not ask, because the bar grades whether structure provokes thinking, not whether the thinking's product is true.

The pattern is not a one-off. The 48-skill sweep (2026-06-13, 61 agents, applying "the repo's own scrutinize-skill standard") returned 0 Rejects and 4 Majors — and neither `outcome-interviewer` nor `making-recommendations` was among the Majors. Both passed as execution-sound. Both were later found to rest on false core premises — excavation, measurement — by a different treatment at a different altitude. Three methodology critiques in three days have now each found the same defect class: *a skill performing an epistemology its method cannot deliver.* That class is the library's best-documented source of deep skill defects, and the dedicated skill reviewer has no failure mode for it. Read the list at SKILL.md:26-35: weak guidance, muddled instructions, UX, workflow drift, routing, validation claims, over-ruling, under-provoking, crude gates. Every entry is execution-altitude. The method can catch a skill that performs its contract badly; it has no eyes for a skill that performs its contract perfectly while the contract's premise is false.

Worse than blind: the bar lens will *actively bless* a false epistemology whenever it wears a forcing-function costume, exactly as it blessed MAP. Structure that "provokes better scoring" passes the provoke test regardless of whether the scores mean anything. The lens's own differential evidence (§8) covers discrimination between over-ruled and provoking structure; nothing in its test battery ever asked it to detect a premise.

## 3. Clearance flows uphill

This would be a bounded, honest limitation if the verdict vocabulary bounded it. It does not. `Defensible` (SKILL.md:71) is unscoped: nothing in the skill says what the verdict covers, so it reads — and in the record, functions — as clearance of *the skill*. The mrec9 arc treated the seven-lens scrutiny as the quality gate for the build; the sweep's verdict tiers were read as library health. When the premise question surfaced the next day, no artifact of the scrutiny said "not examined here." The verdict silently claimed the altitude.

The sharp irony: this skill already knows how to bound a verdict. Bounded Review Scope (SKILL.md:96) exists precisely so that partial *coverage* cannot issue full clearance — state the subset, mark the omitted `unverified`, withhold `Defensible`. The method bounds its verdicts on breadth and never on height. A review that inspected every file can still have examined nothing above execution altitude, and the vocabulary gives that review the same `Defensible` it would give omniscience. The repair is the same move the skill already made once: scope the verdict. Contract altitude is what a scrutiny grades; the truth of the target's method is a different, heavier treatment, and a clearance that has not examined it should say so in one sentence.

## 4. Finder and judge in one pass

A scrutiny generates adversarial findings and then issues the verdict on them — same judge, same pass, same context window. The method's own record shows what that costs, in both directions.

Direction one: over-claiming. Of the sweep's ten flagged criticals, six confirmed, three partial, one refuted under adversarial verification; of the seven unadjudicated P1 findings taken to independent verification, one confirmed, four survived only "partially" (framing overstated), three refuted outright — including *both* systemic findings, the broadest claims in the report (handoff 2026-06-13_21-06-07). The scariest findings had the worst survival rate. Direction two: over-refuting. The one real defect in the making-recommendations build had been raised twice by the build's own eight-lens review and *refuted both times by the pass that raised it*; a fresh scrutiny with an over-refutation convergence check recovered it (handoff 2026-07-01_00-42-19). Re-arguing a finding — for it or against it — is not verification, and the record proves it fails both ways.

The library learned this. Its working practice wraps every consequential scrutiny in machinery that lives entirely outside this skill: independent default-to-refute verifiers, dedup stages, convergence checks, JP's verify-before-acting norm ("the evidence-discipline norm now extends to scrutiny dispositions"). The ledger shows the skill's dominant live form is a lens prompt for parallel finder agents inside those rigs — five fires in three seconds on 07-01, six in a burst on 06-26 — whose output *nobody treats as settled* until a separate layer tests it. The skill's text knows none of this. A solo fire — the three early ledger entries, any future use outside a rig — delivers raw findings under a section named `Required Changes` (SKILL.md:83) and a verdict, with no hint that the method's own record puts unverified-finding survival somewhere near half. `Required Changes` is a command issued before anything has tested the premises it rests on.

The repair is not to bolt a verification stage into the skill — that would fake the independence it cannot have and re-run the same-judge failure wearing a rig costume. It is honesty: findings are argued hypotheses until something independent of the arguing tests them; when a finding drives the verdict, name the cheapest disconfirming check; do not treat your own refutation of your own finding as its death.

## 5. The adversarial stance and its price

The stance — "adversarial review," reject-until-earned inherited from the family — buys recall and pays in precision, and the amendments have all pushed one way. The judgment-trust arc added the anti-toothless guards (SKILL.md:33, :67, :88): right medicine, since a dulled reviewer is worthless, but every added "do not go lenient" line raises the cost of *not flagging*, and the method's precision instruments discipline a finding's form, not its truth. "Every finding must identify a concrete failure path" (SKILL.md:102), the compact evidence pointer (SKILL.md:94), `None found` (SKILL.md:92) — these prevent vague and location-free findings, and a wrong finding can satisfy all three: the refuted systemic closure claim had concrete failure paths and pointers throughout; it was simply false, reachable only through a heading-vocabulary proxy that misclassified. Form discipline is necessary and cannot be sufficient, and the text never says which one it has.

This is the same asymmetry as §1 and §4 seen from the stance side: the method polices the reviewer's *output shape* rigorously and the reviewer's *error rate* not at all. It is the exact critique the interviewer earned for policing question-count while never auditing interpretive drift — transplanted to the adversarial register, where the drift is toward confident, well-formed, wrong findings.

## 6. Severity without stakes

Severity follows the bar (SKILL.md:88): judgment defects keep or escalate by provoke-side weight, trust defects by duplication and overreach. Nothing anywhere weighs the *blast radius of the reviewed skill*. A crude-gate defect in `merge-branch` — a mutating skill whose failure strands repository state — and the same defect in a read-only critique skill take identical severity vocabulary. The library's entire telos is reversibility-proportional spend; its charter gates skills by irreversible-effect capability; and its dedicated skill reviewer grades a contract wielding `git push --force` on the same scale as one that writes prose to chat. The reviewed skill's damage potential should be a severity input, and one sentence makes it so.

## 7. Does it survive its own lens?

The cheap gotcha is available and should be declined honestly. This is a judgment skill mandating a seven-step workflow (SKILL.md:63-71), an eight-section output order (SKILL.md:75-84), and a four-label verdict — and its own lens calls mandated shapes on judgment parts a defect (SKILL.md:32, :67). Read per part, as the lens itself instructs, the tension dissolves: finding *generation* is the judgment part and carries no checklist — the failure modes are explicitly "examples, not a checklist to complete" (SKILL.md:90), the healthiest sentence in the file and one that turns the anti-ossification rule on the rubric itself. The output shape is the *delivery* part, a trust surface: a review whose sections land in predictable order is one the author can consume without re-parsing, and `None found` (SKILL.md:92) explicitly blocks the fill-to-feel-done failure the lens fears. The shape survives its own bar. So does the read-only core — singled-sourced through the CANON script after issue #11 caught five drifted verb lists, which is the trust bar (machinery single-sourced, not copied) applied to itself, late but genuinely.

## 8. The bar lens: the only differentially-proven method in the library

Honesty requires crediting what the precedents' targets never had. The judgment-vs-trust lens (SKILL.md:67, :88) is the single piece of skill methodology in this library with sealed, pre-registered, differential evidence: LOAD-BEARING (11/11 divergence ON vs OFF), calibrated against over-cutting on foreign skills under a cross-model evaluator, anchored 12/14 against a blind human cold judge, safe to act on (cut-then-use), and not constructibly lenient across five red-team probes (`docs/agents/contract-evaluation-methodology.md`). No other sentence of review method here can say anything like that, and any repair that touched the lens would be vandalism.

But the evidence must be read at its own scope. The apparatus proved the lens changes reviews, does not over-cut, and agrees with a human on over-ruled-vs-provoking discrimination. It never tested premise detection, because premise detection was not conceived as a job until Era 66 — the lens's test battery is itself execution-altitude. §2's MAP clearance is not a counterexample to the apparatus; it is the boundary of what the apparatus certified, observed live.

## 9. The life it actually lives

The ledger shows nineteen Claude-side fires, inside the hot core of roughly a dozen skills that account for most of the library's firing, every one in `.agents` — expected, since its targets are skill contracts and this repo is the workshop; the Codex-side life of a plugin-distributed skill is invisible to the ledger, so the count is a floor. The shape of the fires matters more than the count: three solo fires in the founding week, then every subsequent fire arriving in bursts as parallel lens agents inside review Workflows. The method was written as a single reviewer's procedure and is deployed as the finder stage of a pipeline whose judging stages live in rig scripts and session norms. That is not a defect to fix by coupling the text to the rig — the skill should stay runnable solo, and rig orchestration is the rig's business — but it is the strongest reason the §4 honesty amendment matters: the one context where the skill's raw output meets a reader with no verification layer behind it is exactly the solo fire the text is written for.

## 10. What survives, and the honest reframe

Nearly everything survives, and it is worth saying why in one line: the precedents' targets each performed an epistemology their method could not deliver, and this skill's method barely performs an epistemology at all — it is restraints and evidence discipline, and restraints were never the cover story here; they are the declared content. Inspect before judging (SKILL.md:40), the containing-directory default (SKILL.md:42), overlap-coverage honesty (SKILL.md:53), the runtime-claims fence (SKILL.md:61), read-only through a drift-checked CANON core (SKILL.md:100), no mental repair (SKILL.md:101), no generic padding (SKILL.md:102), `None found`, bounded mode, the differentially-proven bar lens — an honest rebuild keeps every one, and this one does.

What the treatment adds is a boundary the method never drew because its authors had not yet watched it be needed: **a contract review grades execution, and its clearance must say so.** The three amendments are one thought worn three ways. Name the defect class the method cannot adjudicate — a skill performing knowledge its method cannot produce — so a scrutiny can *detect and route* to the heavier treatment instead of blessing costume machinery that provokes well. Scope the verdict so `Defensible` clears the contract, never the premise. And grade the review's own evidence with the same severity it grades the target's: reasoned predictions named as reasoned, argued findings named as unverified until something independent tests them, the cheapest settling check named when it matters. One sentence of stakes-weighting (§6) completes the set.

The heart of it: this skill believes adversarial reading of a contract predicts how agents will behave under it. That belief is roughly true at the altitude where the skill lives, and the skill's restraint engineering is the best in the review family. What the record shows is subtler than a false premise — a true method whose verdicts quietly outrun it: clearances that cover questions never asked, simulations filed as behavior, argued hypotheses shipped as required changes. The repair is not a new epistemology. It is making the method as honest about itself as it already is about everything else.
