## 3. A safety method whose only subject has been itself

Here is the central seam, earned from this record alone. The skill's implicit theory is that behavior-preserving simplification is dangerous enough to deserve a two-lane safety envelope, a mechanical secret gate, restorable backups, and a twice-labeled evidence discipline (SKILL.md:18–22, playbook:65–96). Six weeks of history have tested every part of that theory **except the part where work passes through it.**

Every instrument exists because a review predicted a risk, never because work encountered one. The backup helper exists because the skill's own review said improvisation was too dangerous; the restore paragraph (playbook:96) exists because a 06-15 reviewer noticed "restorable" was promised but restore was never explained (`a2ceb80`); the `$HOME` portability fix exists because a sweep caught a hardcoded `/Users/jp/backup` (`8b9cfc1`); the de-dup preserved obligations because an 11-skeptic panel caught four drops (`1d1ab8f`, "Behavior-preserving… no live invocation (zero intended behavior change)"). This is a working immune system — for the text. The method's empirical diet is entirely self-referential: scripts proven by their own tests, contract graded by readers, obligations preserved by panels convened for its own refactor. Nothing outside the bundle has ever depended on any of it.

The cost side of that diet is real and the record prices it: eighteen commits, four founding-day review rounds, a dedicated review-and-build evening for the backup helper, an 11-skeptic-plus-3-skeptic preservation apparatus for one de-dup, three sweep repairs, and roughly fifteen reviews — all spent on a contract that has never once been executed. The library has paid a maintenance bill appropriate to its most-fired trust skill on its never-fired one. That is not an argument for pruning (the paradigm forbids judging by silence, and the 08-01 ledger re-read owns usage); it is the honest description of where the method's life has actually happened: **in its custody chain.**

And the custody chain is, genuinely, the method's success story. The proof epistemology this skill carries — planned verification strength assigned before the edit, observed evidence labeled after, "do not present lint/typecheck-only results or caller audit alone as behavioral proof" (playbook:74) — was born from founding round 2 catching a real circularity, and it has visibly shaped the sessions that maintain the skill: the 06-04 builder marked the smoke test "not run… rather than laundering it through static checks" (verified verbatim); the de-dup commit stated its proof boundary plainly; the 05-30 session closed by naming its own gap ("behavior validation is structural plus contract/smoke-prompt review rather than a live agent run"). The discipline is real, absorbed, and repeatedly exercised — by the skill's editors, on the skill's text, never by an executor on a target. The one agent this skill has ever disciplined is the one holding the pen.

## 4. What the instruments' silence does and does not prove

Per part, under the two-bars lens, judged only on this record:

The backup helper's manifest/hash/exclusion contract is coherent and its secret-exclusion default matches the scanner's verdicts. Nothing in the record contradicts any of it — and nothing in the record has ever exercised it in anger. "Is this reliable?" — the trust bar's first half — is unanswerable at zero runs; single-sourced it is (post-06-14).

**Two live defects, found by this critique, both in the never-exercised seam.** First, the helper litters on failure: it creates the artifact directory before validating that scoped paths resolve inside the root, so a path error leaves a permanent empty artifact behind (reproduced live: exit 2, empty `.backup/20260706-134159-…` in a scratch fixture) — the exact mechanism that produced the 06-14 residue still sitting in `.agents/.backup/`, unnoticed for 22 days in the very repo where the skill's every reviewer lives. Second, the helper's `main()` formats its `argv` parameter — `None` on every real CLI run — into its error message, so its fail-fast line ends `Got: None` (reproduced), violating the error-format contract this repo's own CLAUDE.md carries. Both defects live precisely where zero fires means zero eyes.

**One confirmed review finding is still open.** The 06-15 scrutiny confirmed "Backup helper's `--allow-exclusions` safety override is undocumented in the playbook" — and it still is (verified: zero hits in playbook and SKILL.md at `99d70e9`). An undocumented success-forcing switch on the safety helper is exactly the kind of gap this skill's own method would flag as a protected-surface risk, and the repair traffic that fixed the same review's sibling findings skipped it.

**The closeout's authority is untested rather than inflated.** `Commit Readiness: ready` and the copy-ready Review Packet (SKILL.md:33–36) speak with a certifier's confidence, but unlike baseline's header noun or tech-debt-scan's `complete` stamp, no downstream reader has ever received one, so the record holds no authority-outrunning-warrant scene. The label vocabulary itself is honest — `Behavior Claim` demands "claim plus evidence," the observed labels are bounded to "checks actually run" — and the founding-day adjudication that made the review prompt un-omittable ("make tiny closeouts compact, not skip the review prompt") was a real decision, adversarially made. It stands untested, not wrong. This treatment leaves it alone and names it: **the Review Packet is a letter to a reviewer who has never existed** — and, unlike its sibling's `complete` stamp, it has never claimed to be more than details for follow-up review.

**The judgment stratum is thin, imported, and never exercised.** The skill's theory of what makes code simpler lives in one step: the value order (defect risk → cognitive load → duplication → local patterns → test/debug ease → aesthetics, playbook:70) and the clarity-over-brevity guardrail (:98) whose provenance loops through the founding persona, deletion, and third-party re-import (§1). No fire has ever asked either to do anything. A bare careful agent loses, at most, the safety choreography and the labeled honesty; whether it loses any simplification *judgment* is exactly what zero fires cannot say.
