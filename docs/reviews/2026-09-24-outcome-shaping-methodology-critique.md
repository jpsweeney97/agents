**TLDR:** A hold, and the sealed lean did not move; the findings did. The record locates it mid-loop, in corrections and trade answers that carry content the read never held, while the asked-for restatement mostly returns the read's own words, and the one time the assistant scored a returned restatement as "matches the shape exactly" it treated the outcome the text calls noise as the pass condition. Underneath sits an environmental fact the text does not know: on Claude, JP answers by number and by tick-box, so every answer except a free-text restatement is assent to the assistant's prose, and in one fire the interview ran entirely through option pickers with the prices inside the option descriptions, priced by nobody. Neither regime is the design-exploration shape of workshop-runs-it-field-compresses-it; here the fullest fires are field fires, and the split is by runtime and answer channel. The seam the capsule marks between the user's words and the assistant's compression is real where it appears and is dropped at the handoff boundary every time I could see the receiving lane. The record holds no dissolved or parked want in about twenty conversations, one near-casualty where an unrestated read became the largest build in the corpus after a silent exit the text never guards, and zero cases of a want dying under a trade, which is the one outcome the load-testing section promises. The repairs are honesty at those exact points: what counts as the user's words when the answers arrive as numbers; a re-type trigger keyed on the tell the corpus actually shows; the redirection exit; a trade paragraph that promises what trades do; and a seam that travels.

## 2. The three riders, adjudicated

### Rider 1: trades reshape wants more often than the dossier guessed, and never do what the text promises

The sealed guess was that at most three of twenty-eight fires would show a trade changing the shape. The record refutes it. Of the twenty-one conversations, seventeen priced at least one trade, and in nine of those a trade answer visibly changed the want: a voice rule out of a boundary trade (`codex_2026-07-02_41127230ac.md:1750-1770`); a repositioning narrowed away from one framing (`codex_2026-07-03_b7885346af.md`, reader F4 trade 1); an error-tolerance boundary that had not existed (`codex_2026-08-27_5c148f59b6.md:132-137`); a preservation constraint added inside a trade answer (`codex_2026-08-13_72f0aa6340.md:114-118`); a layering choice plus an unprompted scope narrowing (`claude_2026-08-14_6bbeac6acd.md:306-317`); three batched trades that narrowed a first version on two of three axes and added a part (`claude_2026-08-10_190c39acdc.md:353-372`); a user-authored decision rule substituted for both offered options (`codex_2026-07-09_d4f972db8a.md:300-305`); two trades that ruled out whole designs (`codex_2026-07-13_aa42c04647.md`, reader W0 trades 1 and 2); and one bundled item priced out of a list (`claude_2026-09-23_600a669f37.md:748-770`). That is not a rubber stamp. The trade is the most productive instrument in the corpus after the read itself.

Three things the record shows about what trades do, none of which the text says. First, trades narrow; they never kill. The text's theory sentence is "A negotiable want that dies under its first honest trade was not the want" (`SKILL.md:62`), and in twenty-one conversations no want died under a trade. What a trade did, every time it did anything, was cut scope, add a constraint, or draw a boundary. "What survives, and what the user gave up to keep it, is the shape" is the sentence the record supports; "dies under its first honest trade" has no field referent. Second, the trade's answer is where the user's own content most often arrives: the new constraint in the 08-13 fire, the timing condition in the 07-03 career fire, the finances page in the 08-10 fire, the decision rule in the 07-09 fire all came inside a trade answer, unasked. The text treats the trade as a load test and the restatement as the authorship test; the fires say the trade answer is the authorship channel that actually carries content. Third, the check's finding that a hypothetical trade is answered from the same introspection the text distrusts is right as epistemology and irrelevant to what the fires show: the trades that reshaped wants were mostly not "still worth it if it costs a week" hypotheticals but forced choices between two named shapes ("which matters more", "which size is in the daydream", "which behavior do you want"). The demand-curve sentence describes a cost trade; the field's productive trades are priority trades. The text's example language pulls toward the former.

The trade also has a degenerate form the record shows twice. In the two markdown-reader fires, fused with `grill-with-docs`, every question carried a "The trade:" sentence, twenty-four trades were posed, and twenty-four were accepted (`codex_2026-07-02_27bd4a61b0.md`, `codex_2026-07-03_04c3923400.md`, reader F1). The assistant priced and pre-accepted several itself ("I think that is the right trade for V1") and supplied a recommended answer the user pasted back verbatim. Midway it noticed: "we've now got a run of mostly verbatim/yes answers ... this has partly become drafting with your consent" (`codex_2026-07-03_04c3923400.md:327,344`), and continued in the same rhythm. That is the confabulation pump the text warns about at `SKILL.md:30`, running under trade vocabulary, and the noticing came from the other skill's rule. I do not count these against the trade instrument; I count them as the clearest picture in the corpus of what a trade looks like when the answer is supplied with the question.

### Rider 2: mud re-types in the field, and the census could not see it

The sealed guess was zero field routes to `prototype` or `ideate` and at most two narrated re-types, the instrument live only under test conditions. Refuted. The mud re-typed toward evidence in four field conversations: "The next uncertainty cannot be resolved through more questions. We need a small behavior test" (`codex_2026-08-27_5c148f59b6.md:453`), followed by the test; "that re-types the mud as missing information, not missing words" (`codex_2026-07-09_d4f972db8a.md:294`), followed by the assistant fetching the documentation itself; a blind labeling experiment designed for a voice question no introspection could answer (`codex_2026-07-03_b7885346af.md`, reader F4 item 11); and five read-only subagents dispatched to gather usage and cost data before the first read (`claude_2026-09-23_600a669f37.md`, reader F0). It re-typed toward options in one: a short shaping that routed straight to `ideate` (`claude_2026-08-07_a93e3c528c.md:285`). Contrasting concretes offered as probes, the missing-options move, appear in at least nine conversations, twice self-labeled ("These are my probes, not your list", `claude_2026-09-23_600a669f37.md:544`).

The check's census missed all of it because it probed for the Exits table's route names, and the field's evidence routes do not wear them: "behavior test", "search the docs", "fan out subagents", "clickable mock", "experiment". The one route that does wear its name, `ideate`, fired once. The check's other route counts are a probe artifact of the same kind: the nine field mentions of `making-recommendations` are the loaded body's own text and one upstream use before shaping began (`codex_2026-07-03_04c3923400.md`, reader F1), not exits; no reader found a single handoff to it.

Where the check's finding 2 does hold is subtler than deadness. The text prescribes silent typing ("a label to declare" is the anti-pattern, `SKILL.md:32`), and in eighteen of twenty-one conversations the typing was silent. In one it was made a question to the user: the first read closed with a numbered choice whose options were the mud kinds, each with its consequence stated ("If it's 2, questions won't help, I'll bring you sharply different concrete sketches"), and the user answered "2+3" (`claude_2026-08-10_190c39acdc.md:199-210`). That fire went on to produce the corpus's best trade record, three of three changing the want, and a capsule with every seam marked. One fire is not a law. It is the only observation in the record of the typing being offered for correction the way the read is, and it is the fire the method worked best in. The check's asymmetry (the read is correctable; the diagnosis that picks the tool is not) has one field counterexample, and the counterexample is favorable.

What the record does show about the re-type rule is that its trigger is keyed on the wrong tell. `SKILL.md:39` says to re-type when "an interview that keeps producing confident answers that die under trades". The failure the corpus actually shows is the opposite: confident answers that all pass. Twenty-four of twenty-four in the markdown-reader fires, two bare "Yes" answers that closed a shaping in the thin Codex fire of 08-31 (`codex_2026-08-31_fee35ba6d6.md:769`), a run of pasted-back recommended answers. The examples file names the right tell ("If your reads keep getting instant yeses, get suspicious, not proud", `examples/interaction-examples.md`, Anti-Patterns) and the body's re-type rule does not.

### Rider 3: the asked-for restatement returns the read; the unprompted one carries the content

The sealed guess was about five asks, at most two carrying new content. The record: the assistant asked for a restatement in four conversations, all on Claude or in the first Codex fire, and never in the ten Codex fires that followed. Of the four, two carried something the read had not held: "reassurance" (`codex_2026-07-02_41127230ac.md:1936`, already recorded in the first-live-fire brief), and a noun shift the assistant credited as "your register, not mine" (`claude_2026-07-17_1f1cc49508.md:510-513`). Two returned the read: an itemized enumeration of the read's own list in the user's structure (`claude_2026-09-23_600a669f37.md:713-720`), and, in the cleanest case, a restatement made of two phrases the assistant had written two turns earlier, which the assistant then scored as "your restatement matches the shape exactly" and closed on (`claude_2026-08-26_c18b7ed71f.md:87-102`).

Unprompted restatements are a different population. Three occurred, and all three carried content: a user quoting back his own opening description to say the process was the point (`codex_2026-08-30_179e8e6c02.md:296`), a statement of what a successful meeting would be that the read then absorbed (`codex_2026-08-14_88642b8f93.md:169`), and a long self-authored analysis richer than any read that preceded it (`codex_2026-07-13_aa42c04647.md`, reader W0 item 4). The text already says "Watch for unprompted restatement, it is the strongest signal you get" (`SKILL.md:70`). The record confirms that and adds an asymmetry the text does not draw: the asked-for restatement, after several turns of the assistant's vocabulary, mostly returns that vocabulary, and the assistant cannot score "words you did not supply" when the words are its own. The 08-26 close is the exact failure: the text says a match is noise (`SKILL.md:68,70`), and the assistant treated the match as the pass.

So rider 3 is confirmed in a specific form. The own-words test discriminates when it is not asked for. When it is asked for at the end of a numbered loop it mostly certifies the read, and the one honest thing the assistant did about that in the corpus was to say why it was asking: "I want the decision in your words before it goes into a record, not my wording that you agreed to by number" (`claude_2026-09-23_600a669f37.md:700`). That sentence names the environmental fact section 3 is about.

## 3. The user's words are a channel this environment closes

The method's authorship theory rests on three instruments the user owns: "their words, their trades, their restatement" (`SKILL.md:10`). Look at what the user's turns actually contain in the Claude fires. JP's standing reply contract asks for numbered options wherever feasible, and the Claude harness offers a question tool that renders multi-choice pickers. So the answers arrive as "1. Yes. 2. a", "2", "1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8", "Confirmed", ticked boxes. In the 09-23 fire every answer in twenty-eight turns is a number except the restatement (firsthand read; `claude_2026-09-23_600a669f37.md`). In the 08-31 playpen fire the interview ran entirely through five multi-select questions; the extract shows no read at all, and when I recovered the hidden question inputs from the raw log, the prices were inside the option descriptions ("a scrub pass is owed before any visibility change"), where the user ticked them as attributes of a choice, never answered as trades. The capsule then said "which you accepted prices a scrub pass" and marked three items "my compression, not yet yours, correct any of them"; the user's next message was "Write the plan now", and the plan was commissioned on the unconfirmed compressions (`claude_2026-08-31_b85e21efa5.md:389-416`). The text's own form-tell fired here, five independent questions administered at once (`SKILL.md:50`: "If every question you want to ask is independent, stop: you are administering a form"), and the rule did not.

This is not a routing defect and not an environment complaint. It is an epistemology finding about the method: its test of authorship needs a free-text channel, and the text does not know that the channel can be closed. When answers are numbers and ticks, every answer is assent to the assistant's prose, which `SKILL.md:23` calls weak evidence, and the restatement is the only place the user's words can appear at all. The text makes the restatement ask conditional ("When stakes warrant", `SKILL.md:70`). In this environment it is not a stakes question; it is the only sensor left. The 09-23 assistant understood that and said so. The 08-31 assistant did not ask, and the 08-26 assistant asked and then accepted its own phrases back.

Whether this is a finding about the skill or about JP's environment was the open question the seam handoff left. My adjudication: it is about the skill, because the skill's theory of settlement claims a warrant ("the user saying it back in words you did not supply") that it cannot check, and the environment merely makes that visible. A repair that says what number-answers are, and what to do when the loop has run on them, is honesty about the method's own sensor, not an accommodation of one user's habits.

## 4. Two regimes from one text

The Codex fires run a different method from the Claude fires, on identical text. The Codex shape after the first live fire: a read, sometimes several rewritten as context arrives; one trade, narrated as a step the skill requires ("I'm applying the shaping skill's required trade", `codex_2026-08-14_88642b8f93.md:172`; "Outcome-shaping requires one practical tradeoff test before we treat that as settled", `codex_2026-08-30_179e8e6c02.md:204`); then "That settles the outcome" (`codex_2026-08-13_72f0aa6340.md:442`; `codex_2026-08-14_88642b8f93.md:189`; `codex_2026-08-30_179e8e6c02.md:215`; `codex_2026-08-31_fee35ba6d6.md:769`; "that settles the want", `codex_2026-07-09_d4f972db8a.md:314`; "The intended behavior is clear", `codex_2026-09-08_1f047ca7fb.md:282`), declared by the assistant, with no restatement asked and no capsule in the text's shape. The Claude shape: grounding, a read, independent questions batched and labeled as such, one to three trades, a restatement ask, and a capsule that marks the seam ("Settled, in your words" / "Still my compression") in five of seven fires.

I name this consciously against the remix hazard the dossier flagged. The `design-exploration` critique found instruments running in the workshop and compressing in the field, compliantly. That is not this. Here the fullest fires are field fires on Claude (a personal project, a cross-model repository), and the compressed fires are Codex fires in every venue including the workshop. The split is by runtime and by answer channel, not by venue. Two mechanisms are visible and I offer them as hypotheses, not results: on Codex the body arrives by shell read and the assistant narrates the skill as a checklist it is executing ("the shaping skill's required trade"), which turns "at least one priced trade" into exactly one; and on Claude the harness question tool and JP's numbered-answer rule produce the batched, seam-marked shape with the closed channel described in section 3. Each regime has the other's virtue missing. The Codex regime settles on one trade and the assistant's declaration; the Claude regime asks for words and gets numbers.

The first Codex fire is the exception that shows the text can run whole on that runtime: eight reads, two trades, a refused bare "Yes", a restatement ask, a capsule with one agent-added item marked (`codex_2026-07-02_41127230ac.md:1734-1951`). It also re-read `SKILL.md` before every turn. Whether the later compression is the runtime, the loss of that habit, or the fires being shorter asks is not decidable from the record.

## 5. Settled is declared, then flattened

The text's settlement rule is a conjunction: restated in the user's words, every negotiable part priced, every fixed constraint preserved, no rival read live (`SKILL.md:70`). In the corpus, "settled" was declared by the assistant, on the strength of one trade answer or one "yes", in at least eight conversations (section 4's list plus the wispr fire, `codex_2026-08-27_5c148f59b6.md:175-186`, where "the desired outcome is now settled" followed a "Yes, that's the right boundary, but"). The text says assent to the assistant's prose is weak evidence; the assistant's own declaration is not evidence at all, and it is the modal close.

What the capsule then does with "settled" is the second half of the finding. Where a capsule with seams was produced (six fires), it marked the user's words apart from the compression honestly, and in two of those fires the user corrected the compression when asked (`claude_2026-08-14_6bbeac6acd.md:322-342`: two layout compressions struck). But at the handoff boundary the seam is dropped every time I could see the receiving lane. The 08-26 handoff to `design-exploration` carried "Shape settled via outcome-shaping; capsule in conversation" as its arguments and the receiving lane's decisions-you-might-correct were its own design sub-decisions, not the shaping's compressions (`claude_2026-08-26_c18b7ed71f.md:400-440`). The 08-31 handoff to `implementation-planning` carried the three unconfirmed compressions as the plan's premises. `design-exploration`'s text has no rule for receiving a capsule; it has a rule for what to do if the outcome is still muddy (`plugins/decide/skills/design-exploration/SKILL.md:13`) and a capsule of its own to emit (`:37`). The seam is a property of this lane's output and no lane's input. The text's sentence "The capsule carries no authority the shaping never earned" (`SKILL.md:111`) is true of the capsule and false of the handoff, which carries "settled" and nothing else.

The exit the text never guards is the one the field uses. The only mid-shaping departure the text handles is flight at discomfort ("we're stopping right where it got hard", `SKILL.md:78`). The departures in the record are redirections: the user asks for the next thing. "Enumerate all of the tradeoffs between each option" after one read and one unanswered trade, and the assistant switched skills without a word about the trade or the unsettled read (`codex_2026-07-16_5cb79cda56.md:251-254`); "Write the plan now" over three unconfirmed compressions; "I need to start a fresh session. Pause here" at the exact moment a third trade was posed, complied with mechanically and the trade later inferred rather than re-asked (`codex_2026-07-13_aa42c04647.md`, reader W0 items 3 and 8). None of these users was fleeing discomfort. Each moved on, and the read they left behind was inherited downstream as settled. The 07-16 fire is the record's one near-casualty: one read, one unanswered priced trade, a silent route to `option-shaping`, and the largest build in the corpus (a permanent worktree fleet, a plugin skill, a repo hook) grew from a read no user ever restated. I say near-casualty rather than casualty because the built thing is in daily use and JP endorsed it at every downstream step; what the record shows is that the method's guard against exactly this outcome, the capsule's compression mark, never ran because no capsule ran, and the text has no rule for what to carry when the user leaves before one.

## 6. Exits the table does not know

Every conversation in the corpus that reached an ending went forward. Zero dissolved. Zero parked. The text gives dissolution its most emphatic sentence ("That last one is a success, not a failure", `SKILL.md:12`) and three of its endings (`SKILL.md:72-76`), and in twenty-one conversations none of the three occurred. This is not evidence the text is wrong; a person invokes a shaping lane when they already intend to build, so the selection runs the other way. It is evidence that the claim is untested: the record holds neither a casualty of a want that should have dissolved nor a single confirmation that the lane can end that way. The dossier's expectation E4 held, and holding it says nothing.

The table's forward rows fired unevenly. `design-exploration` fired often and was accepted every time it was named. `ideate` fired once. `option-shaping` fired once, silently, before its row existed. `prototype` never fired by name; two exits were prototype-shaped (a clickable mock the user picked from three offered next moves, `claude_2026-08-10_190c39acdc.md:383`; a behavior test). `grill-me`, `to-questionnaire`, `making-recommendations`, "the relevant review skill", and conversational closure never fired. Exits the table does not name fired repeatedly: `agent-facing-design` three times, `implementation-planning` three times, `synapsis` once, a behavior test once. The table is a map of the Decide plugin's neighbors, and the field's next moves are mostly outside the plugin.

The exit the table is most silent about is the one that produced the record's floor breaches. Four times the assistant settled the want and then made the thing in the same turn: an Excel sheet for a same-day meeting (`claude_2026-08-14_6bbeac6acd.md:318`, "then I can build it"), a meeting talk track (`codex_2026-08-14_88642b8f93.md:189`), a six-step account setup (`codex_2026-07-09_d4f972db8a.md:314-320`), a full proposed design document (`codex_2026-08-31_fee35ba6d6.md:769`). "This lane prepares; it does not design, decide, critique, or implement" (`SKILL.md:82`) assumes every settled want routes to a lane. The field has a class of wants whose next move is a small artifact, and naming a lane for a spreadsheet would be theater. The assistant either invented a destination ("agent-facing design and design exploration for the exact output contract") or crossed the floor without saying so. The floor held in the shaping turns of every fire; what the text lacks is an honest way to leave, so leaving happens silently.

## 7. Instruments the record vindicates

The critique's job includes saying where the record supports the text, and here it does, more than the check predicted.

The read is the engine, as claimed. ## 8. Where the theory claims more than the method can warrant

Four places, each earned above.

"A negotiable want that dies under its first honest trade was not the want" (`SKILL.md:62`) promises an existence test. The field's trades are scope and priority tests. No want has died. The sentence is not false; it is unsupported by the instrument's whole record, and it pulls the agent toward cost hypotheticals ("still worth it if it costs a week?") when the trades that reshaped wants were choices between named shapes.

"The test of a built want is the user saying it back in words you did not supply" (`SKILL.md:68`) names a warrant the agent cannot check after a long loop in its own vocabulary, and cannot obtain at all when the answers are numbers. The record's honest restatements were unprompted; the asked-for ones mostly certified the read; one was scored as a pass for matching, which the same paragraph calls noise.

"Treat the shape as settled only when..." (`SKILL.md:70`) is a four-part conjunction, and the modal close in the corpus is the assistant's one-line declaration after one trade. "Re-type as you go ... confident answers that die under trades" (`SKILL.md:39`) keys the diagnosis on a tell the corpus never showed, while the tell it did show, instant assent, lives only in the examples file.

And one place the text is more honest than the check credited it for. "That makes this lane joint authorship" (`SKILL.md:10`) concedes exactly what the check's strong-form question asked.
