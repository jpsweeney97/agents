## 3. The clock: the gate says no before the draft, and never after

Line the rejections up against the ratifications and one variable separates them: **the position of the consult relative to the decision.**

Every real rejection in §2 shares a shape: the machinery under judgment was still a proposal when the question ran — a user's open ask, a pasted framework, a reviewer's finding, the author's own mid-design candidate. The gate ran at a live fork, and the fork went the lighter way.

Now the other column, which is larger. A 06-12 fold session specified the exact wording to implement in the Skill-tool `args` *before the gate's body loaded*, then implemented it verbatim — the reader matched the pre-gate args text to the written `new_string` near word-for-word. The 07-02 sensor session pre-answered the gate inside its own invocation — "pure telemetry, no agent obligations" was asserted as the invocation's premise, then confirmed as its output. A 06-24 build arrived with the design already adjudicated by a 19-agent squad in a prior session; the reader judged the gate "structurally incapable of producing a rejected or modified-to-lighter outcome in this context." A 06-28 session delivered a fully-formed prompt — mandated sections, a required closing table — in the *same message* as its one-sentence gate verdict, and the table carried a `Confidence` column: a verbatim entry on the gate's own machinery list (SKILL.md:27), blessed unnoticed. On 06-08, in the skill's first week, a cross-model reader already saw it: "the decision to edit preceded the gate consult both times... The gate never had a chance to say no because it was never consulted before the choice, only after."

The text is complicit in this, gently. "This skill is not a ritual; it is a pause before adding structure that future agents must obey" (SKILL.md:8) — but nothing in the file says *when* the pause lands relative to the draft, and nothing distinguishes the two acts the fires show are different in kind: adjudicating a proposal still open, and blessing your own finished design. The second is self-ratification wearing the first's vocabulary, and the method has no sensor for the difference — the same missing-sensor shape the arc found in `grill-with-docs` (contest vs. ratification) and `design-exploration` (workshop vs. field), now located in the library's own judge. The fires prove the gate's question is excellent. They also prove it is routinely asked at the one moment it cannot change anything.

## 4. The grain: a per-edit question cannot see the ratchet

"The core move runs per edit" (SKILL.md:48) — and the dominant field context is precisely a sequence of edits: review-response loops, where an external reviewer files findings round after round and the gate is consulted per finding. There the record is uniform and damning in aggregate even where each verdict is locally defensible: 4/4 justified (06-18), 5/5 (06-19, "no branch point, only ceremony preceding compliance"), 6/6 on a proof-checker surface that "visibly kept growing round over round — new hash fields, new gate conditions, new receipt fields in nearly every pass" (06-27), six-to-seven of six-to-seven (06-29). One reader put the mechanism plainly: because the damage framing is *available* to justify almost any single increment on a security-flavored surface, the per-increment question converges on always-yes, and "the gate was never asked: is continuing to accrete validation machinery, cycle after cycle, itself the problem?"

The skill knows this failure mode. Its 07-02 repair added "context is not free... it accretes with no gate of its own, so on a surface that has already grown, prefer replacing prose to adding more" (SKILL.md:31), and the Whole-Surface Check — "step back when a surface has grown materially, has seen heavy recent change, or makes you hesitate... if the surface feels larger than the work it protects, simplify before adding more structure" — has existed since founding. But it lives at the bottom of `references/calibration.md` (calibration.md:81-86), and the fires never open that file: two readers independently caught sessions skipping it in the exact moments its own trigger named ("about to add... hard rules" — five qualifying occasions in one session, four rounds of new gate predicates in another). The method's answer to the ratchet exists and is homed where the ratchet's victims never look. Meanwhile the one recorded case of the ratchet being *resisted* — "the review ratchet — each pass demanding more mechanism — needed resisting, not following" (06-10) — did it from the body's damage question alone, which shows the resistance needs no new machinery; it needs the whole-surface question to be where the per-edit question is.

## 5. The pass that travels: a gate verdict read as a certificate

## 7. The constitution and the gate: two lives, one file

Fourteen of 88 reads were constitution-reads: the file loaded not to gate an edit but to consult the doctrine — the two-bars lens for a skill classification, the offload paragraph for a merit deliberation, the conventions for an output-shape call. This is a real second life, institutionally load-bearing: `AGENTS.md:71` names this file the canonical home "do not duplicate it here"; `writing-principles` defers to it six times; `scrutinize-skill` embeds the lens as its workflow's step 3; every treatment in this arc, including this one, runs Phase-4 repairs through it. That is not silence as decoration — it is two audiences reading one file for two jobs, and the fires show both jobs paying.

The honest question is whether the co-residence costs anything. The corpus shows one cost, small but real: within Two Kinds of Skill, the offload doctrine now lives twice — as the trust bullet's second-value-source clause (06-25) and as the standalone either-kind paragraph (06-27) — deliberately cross-referenced, not drifted, but the same idea carried in two homes inside one section is the library's named disease in its incubation stage, in the very file whose trust bar says "single-sourced rather than copied." The other candidate cost — that a gate skill's frontmatter fires on design questions while a constitution-reader wants it for classification — produced no observed misroute in 219 files. The dual identity is working; it should simply be *said*, because the file's own opening sentence ("a pause before adding structure") describes only one of its two lives.

## 8. The quilt question, answered

JP's disposition set the rebuild trigger: does the accumulated file read quilted? The stratigraphy is visible — a reader can carbon-date the offload paragraph, the conventions, the audit repairs — and the trust bullet has grown into a hundred-plus-word sentence carrying three ideas. But quilting, as a rebuild warrant, should mean *strata working against each other or against the core*, and the corpus shows the opposite: the founding gate fires constantly and pays; the lens fires on skill targets and is differentially proven; the newest strata are either too young to judge (Tool Returns, Reader Capability, Library Conventions — all under a week old at the corpus's edge) or quietly firing in their home context (offload, in library deliberations). Nothing in 88 read transcripts shows one stratum defeating another. The file reads layered, not quilted — and the layers share one philosophy stated at three altitudes (per-edit, per-skill, per-library).

A ground-up rebuild would therefore destroy the best-evidenced living judgment core in the library to cure a defect that is not architectural. The defects the fires actually show — verdict-scope silence, clock silence, the ratchet blind spot, trust surfaces that don't travel — are sentences, not structures. The sunk-cost framing JP guarded against cuts the other way here: the cost already sunk into this text is exactly the kind that *did* pay, and the record can show where.

## 9. What survives, and the honest reframe

Nearly everything, and with more evidence behind it than any prior hold. The Core Move question survives as the most-fired, most-outcome-changing judgment sentence in the library — 63 of 88 files worked it, and it changed shipped artifacts against user asks, pasted frameworks, sibling reviews, and reviewer piles. The damage question survives with the 07-02 vocabulary repair ("name the value actually being claimed") intact. The two-bars lens survives untouched at its proven scope. The rejected-machinery script survives — it fired 21 times in the read corpus, including the record's best rejections. When Machinery Survives, the Workflow Boundary (with its Era-61 battle record), the no-abstract-exercise fence, the founding compression, the name, the description: all stay. There is no false epistemology here to invert. The premise — that a pause with one sharp question protects agent judgment from accreting machinery — is not a costume; it is demonstrably true where the pause actually happens.

The reframe is one sentence: **the method knows what to ask and has never said when the asking counts or what the answer covers.** A gate whose question runs after the draft ratifies; a gate whose question runs per increment feeds the ratchet it exists to prevent; a gate whose pass travels into commit messages and verification blocks certifies things it never examined. All three are the same silence — the text describes the question and never the question's *reach* — and all three repairs are honesty:

1. **A verdict-scope sentence** (Core Move or When Machinery Survives): a gate pass classifies this increment's shape at this moment — it certifies nothing about the artifact's content, the accumulated surface, or a later revision, and a pass cited in a commit message or verification block should say what was actually asked. (The fires' certificate drift, §5.)
2. **A clock sentence** (Core Move): the pause is before the design hardens — a gate consulted on its operator's own finished draft is ratification, and should either say so or reopen one genuine lighter alternative before blessing. (The consult-after-decision column, §3.)
3. **Bring the whole-surface question into the body** (one or two sentences at the Core Move's edge, absorbing the calibration reference's check): a run of consecutive justified verdicts on one growing surface is itself the borderline case — step back from the increment to the surface before the next yes. Single-home it in the body, where the fires actually read; leave calibration.md pointing at it rather than carrying it. (The ratchet, §4; the never-opened reference.)
4. **Let the output contract match the life** (Output Shape): the template is one honest shape, not the required one — what is owed is the verdict with its damage reasoning and next move, in whatever form the moment takes. (The 25-of-88 followership, §6.)
5. **Extend the self-application clause** (one clause at SKILL.md:61): edits to this skill run this skill's own question — the gate does not exempt its own body. (The 06-08 miss, §6.)
6. **Optional, JP's call:** consolidate the offload doctrine's two in-section homes into one (§7), and add a clause to the opening sentence naming the file's second life as the library's quality doctrine — so the constitution-read is a described use, not an undocumented one.

No new sections beyond what sentence 3 absorbs, no fields, no classifiers, no scores — the repairs are the method's own medicine at its own altitude.
