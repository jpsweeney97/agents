## 5. The proof boundary's weakest field is the one the store cannot own

The method's deepest design commitment is that compared values are never composed from memory: the store is byte-exact authority, packet composition is mechanical or unclaimed (`references/stage-packets.md:230`), and the capsule renders only store-derived values. In the observed fires, that commitment held everywhere it applies — and the one proof-boundary member that is structurally self-report is where a false value got through.

The run-1 failure capsule of `899ffa90` records, in its proof boundary's own effective-models list, `orchestrator: claude-opus-4-8[1m]`. Ground truth, verified firsthand three ways: the spawn request and the orchestrator's `.meta.json` both say `model: sonnet`, and all 252 of its real assistant turns carry `"model":"claude-sonnet-5"` in the transcript's per-turn API metadata. The orchestrator populated a required proof field from a harness banner that lied to it, and presented the value as fact; mechanical validation accepted it, because the schema checks shape, not truth (`SKILL.md:66` requires only "the effective model or `unknown`"). The committed smoke record then compounded it: "both orchestrators self-reported `claude-opus-4-8[1m]` (the `model: sonnet` spawn override did not take on the background orchestrator)" (`docs/smoke-tests/2026-07-15_deliberate-recommend-enum-fix-and-success-capsule.md:6`) — an interpretation the transcript metadata contradicts. The next day's v6 orchestrator did better, recording its identity "ambiguous… unresolved" when its two ambient sources disagreed (`…claude-end-to-end-re-smoke.md:8`) — the honest shape — but nothing in the contract requires that shape, so the difference between a capsule that lies and a capsule that says "ambiguous" is currently stage-agent temperament.

The finding is small in operational consequence and central in method terms, because it is the genre's authority-outrunning-warrant pattern caught on camera in the only consumption this skill's artifacts have ever had. These capsules have never been read by a downstream decision-maker; their only readers were the build arc's own sessions and records — expert readers, steeped in the proof-boundary discipline — and twice the certified form carried a false or wrong-glossed value straight into the durable record. The elaborate honesty of the proof boundary text ("what none of it proves", `references/capsule.md:31`) is real and unusual; the fires show it protects readers only where the store owns the value. Where the field is self-report, the certificate's form lends the content an authority the method never earned — exactly the risk the dossier pre-registered, cashed by the evidence in miniature.

## 6. Isolation relocates incoherence to the seams

The architecture's one observed epistemic cost is not bias — it is equivocation in the assembled artifact, and it is mandated, not accidental.

In the v6 Claude fire, the fixture states a lean toward the interviews candidate. Prune, lean-blind by design, cuts that candidate on price. Recommend's packet carries an empty survivor-share authority-note set — the lean rode out with the excluded candidate, exactly as the matrix intends — and its brief instructs it to register a `user-visible-lean` from what it has, so it substitutes seed authorship: survivor A "the only survivor the user proposed themselves." The assembled close then tells the reader: "This call matches your own visible lean — A was your seed…" (accepted capsule, `work/capsule.yaml:915-918`). Two sections above, the exclusion check names the *actual* stated lean as an excluded live challenge, most worth contesting (`FINAL.txt:8`). Both stages are contract-compliant; each sentence is true under its own packet's definition of "visible lean"; and the one document a human reads asserts agreement with "your own visible lean" in a sense a cold reader will not have — while naming the other, textually-actual lean as excluded — with no sentence connecting the two.

This is the methodological point the two-leans specimen proves: **packet isolation guarantees that no single stage holds enough context to lie about the whole, and thereby also guarantees that no stage holds enough context to keep the whole coherent — and the contract assigns whole-artifact coherence to nobody.** The validator checks cross-field consistency of stored values, not cross-section semantics; Contest tests exclusions against the close's logic, not the close's vocabulary against the echo; the orchestrator is forbidden to alter rendered content; the capsule renders verbatim. In `making-recommendations` run bare, one mind owns the close's coherence. Here the reader inherits the seam, in the artifact whose certified form (§5) invites trust. One observed instance in four full fires is a 25% hit rate on the only axis anyone has ever checked — and it was found by this treatment's reading, not by any instrument in the run.

The repair is honesty, not machinery (§9): Recommend's registered lean and any close language derived from it must name its actual basis — what its packet could see — and never assert unqualified agreement with "your visible lean" when the contract's own design may have routed the visible lean out of its sight. The general seam — assembled-artifact coherence is unvalidated — belongs in the proof boundary's not-proven vocabulary, where the contract already keeps its other honest limits.

## 7. The axiom, the unbought premise, and the first doubt on record

The whole architecture rests on one sentence: isolation is "load-bearing bias mitigation" (`docs/specs/2026-07-13-deliberate.md:109`) — asserted once, parenthetically, to justify the degradation policy, in a 442-line spec otherwise wholly occupied with contract mechanics. The spec knows what would test it and says, with the candor that characterizes this bundle's best writing, that the test has not run: "the decisive empirical check: shallow-prune results against a full-shaping control, hunting excluded eventual winners… until then its result is a plan, not evidence. Contract correctness makes v1 executable; **only that control can tell whether the delegated pruning is worth trusting**" (spec:441, standing debt since v6, restated as the file's last line at spec:442 and again in the lifecycle note). Even that check is the narrow half: it tests Prune's cuts, not the broader claim that a packet-isolated pipeline beats an inline careful agent on the same decision — a differential no document in the corpus ever proposes. All 17 adjudicated review rounds pressured trust, fail-open, recovery, and contract-fidelity properties (verified against the spec's own lineage bullets, v2–v18); zero pressured the premise; and the adjudication apparatus itself (`review-reviewer`) is built to judge review reliability, not design-theory soundness, so the review corpus was structurally incapable of producing premise evidence.

What the fires add: the mechanical half of isolation demonstrably works — the lean verifiably never reached Generate, Prune, Shape, or Recommend in the runs where that was checked — and its observed cost is §6. Whether hiding the lean *changed any cut or the close* is exactly the unrun control; the one candidate the lean pointed at died on a constraint any inline agent would also have applied. The evidence permits no stronger sentence in either direction, and this critique declines to write one.

The build arc contains no doubt (§4). The ecosystem does, and it arrived one day after the arc closed: the 2026-07-17 `synapsis` portfolio adjudication — three deliberation exchanges plus three sealed contrary-case probes, all read-only, zero deliberate runs — used deliberate as the named exemplar of the library's hardening concern: "substantial machinery: a roughly 15,000-line implementation surface, a 10,789-line validator, a 22,396-word specification… Its runtime proof is now strong… Its value rationale remains only partially closed: the… shallow-prune outcome/value control is the next evidence needed before further semantic machinery is treated as justified" (P3, rollout `019f7111`). The sealed probe then *refused* P3 on a material objection this treatment verified against the spec: P3 called the control "separately pre-registered," and the spec says the opposite — the protocol (same-field control, winner adjudication, leakage boundary, case set, pass/fail rule) must first be designed and sealed. The library's most-hardened skill's decisive value check is not pending; it is undesigned. Three independent instruments — the spec, the lifecycle note, and the cross-model adjudication with its probe — now converge on the same single next evidence event, and it remains, correctly, JP-authorized and pre-registration-gated.
