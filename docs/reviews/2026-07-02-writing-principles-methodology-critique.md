# Writing-Principles: A Critique of the Methodology and Philosophy

> Saved 2026-07-02. JP asked for `writing-principles` to get the treatment `outcome-interviewer` (`docs/reviews/2026-07-02-outcome-interviewer-methodology-critique.md`, rebuild `26d7ae9`), `making-recommendations` (`docs/reviews/2026-07-02-making-recommendations-methodology-critique.md`, rebuild `01d9313`), and `scrutinize-skill` (`docs/reviews/2026-07-02-scrutinize-skill-methodology-critique.md`, repairs `d7970eb`) got: pressure on the methodology and philosophy — the epistemology, the stance, the implicit theory of what good agent-facing instruction prose is and what an obligation-editing pass can know — with explicit permission for either an inversion or a hold, and explicit instruction to manufacture neither. The target is `skills/writing-principles/SKILL.md` and `agents/openai.yaml` as of commit `c9eb614`; inline line references resolve against that version (`git show c9eb614:skills/writing-principles/SKILL.md`). Two circularity traps were named and avoided: the critique is not routed through `scrutinize-skill` (whose 0.6.0 premise lens exists precisely to route questions like this one out to this treatment), and it is not a writing-principles-governed prose pass on its own doctrine. This critique served as the design brief for the same-day repairs (commit `ad8b572`), which kept the name, the shape, and the frame.

I read the skill, its metadata, its full commit history back through the founding rewrite to the initial commit — including the 53-check reference file the founding rewrite deleted — the skill-usage ledger, five recovered live-fire transcripts, and the record of the one time the method was turned on its own body. What follows is pressure on the method itself.

**TLDR:** The second non-inversion, and for a different reason than the first. `scrutinize-skill` survived because its method admits to being restraints; this skill survives because its founding move was itself an inversion — commit `c1f05a9` killed a 14-principle, 53-check, risk-tiered prose-quality liturgy and replaced it with one value question. The obligation frame is a defensible unit of analysis for an edit gate, not a false epistemology, and the treatment does not overturn it. What the treatment finds is a warrant defect with one shape worn five ways: the method's central test — is protected work *visible*? — makes one editor's one-pass perception the measure of an obligation's value, and every value the library's own record marks as high sits exactly where one pass cannot see. The incident behind a guard lives in history the method never opens. The reader a guard protects may be a weaker model than the editor — the cross-model arc proved guards divide by model, and this is a dual-runtime lane. The provoke-function of judgment prose is invisible to an obligation-shaped glance, and the lane never imported the library's central quality doctrine. Drift between copies is invisible in a single read — the R11 G-cluster lived in this skill's own body. And the nuance dropped by a "lightening" is invisible to every check its validation ladder names, so the Output grammar commits the exact false-proof pattern its own Challenge #6 polices. The repairs are not a new epistemology: name where value hides from one pass, buy the cheap look, and bound the claim when the look comes back inconclusive.

## 1. Born of an inversion

The history matters here more than in any precedent, because this skill is a *product* of the genre this treatment belongs to. Its pre-founding version was a quality-compliance instrument: fourteen ranked principles, a 53-item self-check across ten verification passes, risk tiers keyed to line counts, a 138-line reference the workflow escalated into. The founding rewrite (`c1f05a9`, 2026-06-08, "Rewrite writing-principles as obligation edit gate") deleted all of it and installed a single stance: "This skill is not a prose-polish checklist. Treat instruction text as a set of obligations future agents must carry" (SKILL.md:10). That is the same species of move the Era-66 and Era-68 treatments performed on their targets — a measurement-shaped costume stripped, a value question installed — executed on this skill before the genre had a name.

So the frame under review is: instruction text is a set of obligations; each material instruction is adjudicated by one question — "What does this make a future agent do, decide, avoid, verify, remember, or maintain?" (SKILL.md:18-21) — and one value test — what user work does the obligation protect (SKILL.md:23); the remedies are delete, lighten, or make concrete (SKILL.md:67-71). Around that core, honest restraints: do not preserve an obligation because it exists, do not polish a bad obligation into a clearer bad obligation (SKILL.md:73), do not pad review-only output with generic writing advice (SKILL.md:93), stop and ask at missing authority (SKILL.md:75), findings-first plus `No edits made` plus wait (SKILL.md:97).

The frame is a unit-of-analysis choice, and mostly the right one: the ambient disease of instruction docs is accretion of duties, and per-obligation adjudication is the correct knife. The epistemology enters at exactly one point — the adjudication of "earns its place" — and in the claims the method makes after it edits. Everything this critique finds lives in those two places.

## 2. Deletion on sight

"If no meaningful protected work is visible, delete the obligation" (SKILL.md:69). Visible — to whom, after what look? The method's read-before list (SKILL.md:52) names repo instructions, companion metadata, referenced examples, validators, workflows, neighboring docs: every one a present-tense surface. The method never opens the history of the line it is about to kill. There is no origin step, no Chesterton move, no instruction to ask *why does this exist* before adjudicating *whether it should*.

Obligations in this library are archaeological. The ambient texts encode incident learnings — the prove-it-first paragraph in `AGENTS.md` exists because the same failure recurred at Eras 4, 11, 13, 19, 34, and 46; the `plugin-eval` fence exists because sessions kept "repairing" an intentional absence. Some guards carry their rationale inline, and it is worth noticing why: rationale-as-armor is what a guard grows when the editing culture deletes what it cannot see. The guards without armor are exactly the ones a visibility test kills. To an editor without the origin, an incident guard reads as an over-cautious sentence — the counterfactual it protects is invisible by construction, in the precise sense that the incident it prevents stopped happening.

The Core Move sharpens this into doctrine: "Preserve protected user work, not original wording or assumed intent" (SKILL.md:23). In this repo, intent is not assumed — it is *recorded*: commit messages, design briefs under `docs/reviews/`, the contract ledger, handoffs. The sentence licenses discarding exactly the evidence the method never reads, under a word — "assumed" — that makes reading it sound like a concession. Reading recorded intent is not deference; intent can still lose the adjudication. But the fence's origin question comes before the fence's fate, and the method has no step where it gets asked.

The asymmetry completes the picture. Adding an obligation crosses the `agent-facing-design` pre-gate (SKILL.md:63), and an always-loaded addition crosses the charter; deleting one crosses a glance. The bias direction is defensible — accretion is the disease, and build-and-prune wants pruning cheap — but the skill never argues the asymmetry, and a defensible bias still deserves the cheapest evidence step before an irreversible-in-effect act. Git makes deletion recoverable in principle; nothing makes it *noticed*.

## 3. A value theory priced at a glance

"Meaningful user work" is the whole value theory, and the method prices its central question at one pass of one reader. Two noise directions follow, and nothing in the method is positioned to notice either. An editor who cannot construct the protecting story concludes the work is absent — absence of evidence read as evidence of absence. An editor fluent enough constructs a protecting story for anything — the same fluency the precedent critiques caught certifying wants and rankings here certifies obligations. In both directions, what adjudicates is the editor's narrative facility, not the obligation's value.

The adjudication grammar has no exit for the honest middle. Delete, lighten, clarify — there is no verdict for "cannot tell from the text whether this work is real." Stop-and-ask (SKILL.md:75) triggers on missing targets and missing authority, never on unadjudicable merit. A method whose hard cases are unanswerable from its inputs, and whose grammar contains no unanswerable, will answer them anyway.

The library's own record prices the question honestly. When eight real tail-guards were seriously asked "does this obligation earn its place?", the answer took the Era 20–35 apparatus — a sealed cross-model differential with a gpt-5.5 actor and blind third-model grading — and returned all eight KEEP, several of them *marginal at a strong model and genuinely additive at a weak one*. That finding is the sharp edge: visibility is model-relative. This is a dual-runtime lane; its edits bind gpt-family Codex sessions the ledger cannot even see. The obligation a Fable-class editor finds unjustified may be exactly what keeps a weaker reader on the rails, and the single-pass visibility test is administered by the reader least able to see that protected work. The record says the earn-its-place question, asked seriously, cost an apparatus; the method prices it at a glance and has no way to say "this one costs more than a glance."

## 4. The bar it never learned

The library's central quality doctrine — judgment prose versus trust prose, protect-and-provoke versus reliable-and-single-sourced — is the only piece of method in this library with sealed differential evidence (11/11 LOAD-BEARING, human-anchored 12/14), canonically homed in `agent-facing-design`. The lane that edits all instruction prose carries no render of it. No form of "judgment," "provoke," or the per-part bar appears anywhere in this skill. The founding rewrite predates the apparatus; the lane was never retrofitted.

Its scope includes every judgment skill's body and behavior-shaping support docs (SKILL.md:29-32). Its remedy for an earned obligation runs one direction: "make the trigger, action, boundary, evidence, and stop condition concrete enough for a future agent to follow" (SKILL.md:71), and Challenge #2 marks the absence of those five properties as a defect unconditionally (SKILL.md:82). Applied to trust prose — lifecycle, safety, proof, routing — that is right. Applied to a provoking question, a forced comparison, an interrogation rhythm — `grill-me`'s one-question discipline, `outcome-shaping`'s own-words close — the same remedy grammar is the ossification `agent-facing-design` warns against: concreteness that pre-makes the call turns provocation into procedure, and "concrete enough to follow" is precisely the wrong test for text whose job is to make the next agent think rather than comply.

The provoke-function is protected work par excellence, and it is invisible to an obligation-shaped glance: it binds no action, verifies nothing, stops nothing. Under the Core Move question it barely exists — "decide" is in the verb list, but nothing downstream of the question treats provocation as a thing an edit could destroy. An editor running this method on a judgment skill sees vague, unbounded, non-concrete sentences and owns remedies that all sharpen them into duties. The one lens that would stop it lives one directory away, in the skill this one names as its pre-gate, and the two texts have never been introduced at the editing altitude. The repair is a pointer, not a copy — `AGENTS.md` forbids duplicating the doctrine, and one sentence deferring per-part bar-setting to the canonical home is the single-sourced render.

## 5. One remedy direction

The trust-side twin of §4: the challenge order can see an obligation that is too loose and never one that is too tight. Vague and Unclear (SKILL.md:82-83) both push toward more trigger, more boundary, more stop condition. Crude-rule overreach — the trust-skill failure the library recorded and repaired at Eras 35/39/44 by *softening* firm rules to graded forms — has no challenge item, and the lightest-form ladder (SKILL.md:70) offers "a boundary, default, example, precondition, or failure behavior" but no graded form of a rule too absolute for its cases. A method whose remedies ratchet one direction manufactures the failure it cannot name: every pass leaves the text more triggered, more stopped, more absolute — and the library's recorded failure on this axis is over-crude, not over-loose.

## 6. The disease it caught in its own body and still cannot name

The library's best-documented prose disease is duplication-drift: the same rule stated in several places, the copies quietly diverging. It is why the CANON scripts exist, why companion-metadata drift is a named standing risk, why R11 ran. And the R11 record's sharpest case was this skill's own body: five within-file drift clusters one week after founding, the worst a live three-way proof-class divergence — Edit Gate false-proof wording, Challenge #6, and Output each carried a different subset of the proof classes, so an agent reading one home was under-covered relative to the others (handoff 2026-06-14_17-08-39, the G cluster). The skill that owns instruction prose grew instruction prose's most-recorded failure inside its own 120 lines, and its method — self-applied, as its case law requires — had not caught it. A purpose-built external harness did.

The challenge order (SKILL.md:81-87) has no drift item. Unjustified, Vague, Unclear, Overbuilt, Unbounded, False-proof, Conflicting — duplication appears nowhere, and Conflicting is about authority resolution, not copies. The blindness is structural, not incidental: a single-pass read of a single surface cannot see the other copies, so the failure mode never presents itself to the method even when — as in its own body — the copies share a file. And the live-fire record confirms the gap from the other side: the highest-impact finding in the skill's best recovered review-only fire (cross-model, 2026-06-09) was a drift finding — the loaded `CLAUDE.md` was a gitignored, already-diverged copy of the tracked `AGENTS.md` — produced by the session's evidence instinct reading git state, entirely off the method's map. When the method's fires find their best findings, the finding class is one the method doesn't name.

The R11 fix's discipline — union-first single-homing, an adversarial preservation pass, "structural validation passes silent drops... preventable only by UNION-first" — lives in a handoff as case law. The skill's text knows none of it.

## 7. "Lightened" is a claim the ladder cannot carry

After direct edits the method reports "whether it was deleted, lightened, or clarified" and "verification performed" (SKILL.md:101-106). "Lightened" is a semantic claim: the obligation's protection survived at lower cost. Every instrument in the Validation section (SKILL.md:114-119) is structural — frontmatter parse, path checks, the local validator, whitespace. By the skill's own proof-class doctrine (SKILL.md:110), structural checks prove parsing and shape only. So the closing report's grammar invites the exact pattern Challenge #6 exists to catch (SKILL.md:86): a structural check implying a class of proof it cannot give. The skill polices false-proof in its targets and commits it in its own closing move.

The near-miss makes it sharper: Validation item 2 asks for "a realistic dry run when practical" for *behavior or trigger changes* — but a lightening is precisely an edit claiming *no* behavior change, so the one instrument that could test the claim is keyed to never fire on it. The honest instruments exist in-house and are proven: the R11 preservation pass walked three realistic invocations against old and new text and confirmed identical routing — the method's own body got the check its method never asks for. Nothing-lost is a behavior claim. It takes a preservation look, or an honest statement of what was in fact dropped; the method's report grammar currently permits it on a whitespace check.

## 8. The life it lives

The ledger holds 13 Claude-side fires — a floor, since Codex fires are invisible and this is a dual-runtime lane. Five landed outside `.agents` (two in `cross-model`, one in `codex-tool-dev`, two on the user-global `CLAUDE.md`): a genuinely global lane, exactly what the paradigm predicts for a merit-borne skill. The temporal shape is stark — twelve fires between 06-05 and 06-13, then one on 07-02 — through the library's heaviest instruction-editing period. Read honestly, this is a niche datum, not a prune signal: the Era 54–69 work was overwhelmingly *material* change, owned by `agent-facing-design` and the build lanes, and the obligation-only edit as a standalone event is rare by the lane's own narrowness.

Five fires were recoverable in full, and they say something the text doesn't. Two were *additions* — a branch-policy note in `cross-model`, the charter-gated keep-side amendment to `AGENTS.md` — with writing-principles summoned as the drafting stage; in all three recovered multi-skill sessions, `agent-facing-design` co-fired. The routing lattice the founding day spent six consecutive commits building (`93285b6` through `0e9d78d`, all 2026-06-08) looked like border anxiety in the history; the fires vindicate it — the pre-gate composition works in practice, unprompted. The realized identity is broader than the charter: not the obligation-subtraction lane but the instruction-prose lane, the careful way to touch a contract, whatever the operation, with the gate riding alongside.

What the record never shows: a deletion contested, a lightening checked, a judgment skill's provocation edited under this method. The method's riskiest moves are exactly the ones its recorded fires have not yet exercised. That is why this critique's warrant claims rest on the method's text, its own body's R11 history, and the adjacent library record — not on an observed casualty. There is no recorded mis-fire, and this critique should say so as plainly as it says anything else.

## 9. Does it survive its own lens?

The cheap gotcha — run the obligation test on the skill's own obligations and dock whatever fails — is declined; a per-part read is more honest. The paste path (SKILL.md:61) is real trust engineering: it keeps a user from being forced into file mechanics to get an edit, and its no-file-edited statement is exactly the proof honesty the rest of this critique demands. Review-only's findings-first, `No edits made`, and wait (SKILL.md:91-97) are honest stop conditions. The proof-class union paragraph (SKILL.md:110) is the best paragraph in the file — single-homed by R11 exactly as the doctrine requires, and the one place the method already practices what §7 asks of it. "Use this order as a fast scan, not a report template" (SKILL.md:79) is the file's healthiest sentence, the same anti-ossification move the scrutinize-skill critique credited in its target. The routing half is heavy — five neighbor lanes, an authority map, stay-in tests — but §8's co-fire record shows it working, and heavy trust machinery that observably prevents misroutes is the trust bar passed, not a defect.

And the Core Move question — "What does this make a future agent do, decide, avoid, verify, remember, or maintain?" (SKILL.md:18-21) — survives untouched as the single best sentence in the skill. The critique is not that the question is wrong. It is that the method treats one asking of it, by one reader, on one surface, as sufficient warrant for the answer.

## 10. What survives, and the honest reframe

Nearly everything survives. The obligation frame, the Core Move, the challenge-before-clarify spine, the lightest-form instinct, the restraints, the paste path, the proof-class discipline, the routing lattice, the name — kept. The name is mildly ironic — the founding rewrite renounced principles-as-checklist, and the body's first move is to disclaim its own title — but it asserts no false epistemology, and it is load-bearing in four live routing surfaces (`AGENTS.md:70`, `agent-facing-design` SKILL.md:89 and its default_prompt, `friction-to-guards:38`, `skill-ux-design` ×2) plus ledger entries that are never rewritten. Renaming would be cosmetics billed at routing cost.

This is a non-inversion with a different shape than the first one. `scrutinize-skill`'s defect was verdicts outrunning a true method; this skill's defect is a method mistaking its perception for the world. The five findings are one thought: the test "is protected work visible?" is administered by a single reader in a single pass, and the library's record locates the value of its best instruction prose precisely off the page — in the incident behind a guard (§2), the weaker reader bound by it (§3), the thinking it provokes (§4), the sibling copies it must stay level with (§6), and the nuance a lightening silently drops (§7). A visibility test pointed at invisible value does not need replacing; it needs to know where value hides and what a look costs.

So the repairs are five instances of one move — buy the cheap look, or bound the claim. History before deletion, with recorded intent read rather than assumed away. An honest "cannot adjudicate from the text" when the look comes back empty, instead of a forced verdict in either direction. The per-part bar before the remedy ladder, pointed at its canonical home, so judgment prose's provocation is named protected work and concreteness stops being the universal medicine — with the graded form joining the ladder for rules too crude. A drift item in the scan, since the method's own body proved the scan blind to it. And a preservation-honest close: a deletion or lightening reports what the old text carried that the new text doesn't, or the walk that showed nothing dropped — never "lightened" on a whitespace check. No new sections, no scores, no machinery: the same stance, wearing evidence.

The heart of it: this skill believes an obligation's value is what a careful reader can see in it. Its own founding was the discovery that a checklist cannot see value at all; the treatment's finding is that a glance cannot either — not because the glance is careless, but because the library's most valuable obligations are load-bearing exactly where no single pass looks. The method's question is right. It just costs more than the method admits, and the repair is to pay honestly: a look at the history, a thought for the weaker reader, a hand kept off the provocation, an eye on the sibling copy, and a close that reports what was actually lost.
