# Grill-With-Docs: A Critique of the Methodology and Philosophy

> Saved 2026-07-03. JP asked for `grill-with-docs` to get the treatment the four precedents got (`docs/reviews/2026-07-02-outcome-interviewer-methodology-critique.md`, rebuild `26d7ae9`; `2026-07-02-making-recommendations-methodology-critique.md`, rebuild `01d9313`; `2026-07-02-scrutinize-skill-methodology-critique.md`, repairs `d7970eb`; `2026-07-02-writing-principles-methodology-critique.md`, repairs `ad8b572`): pressure on the epistemology, the stance, the implicit theory of what exhaustive one-at-a-time interrogation can know, what a glossary and an ADR trail are evidence of, what the stop condition measures, what it means that the griller authors the recommended answer to every question it asks, and what the mid-session write-back does to authority — with the 2–2 record explicitly not a tie to break, and explicit instruction not to pattern-match this target to `outcome-interviewer`. Two circularity traps were named and avoided: the critique is not routed through `scrutinize-skill` (its 0.6.0 premise lens exists precisely to route questions like this one out to this treatment), and it is not conducted as a grilling. A privacy boundary was honored: the two `career`-repo transcripts were read for method behavior only (both turned out to be detection false-positives), and nothing personal appears here. The target is `skills/grill-with-docs/` as of commit `fcf98a3`; inline line references resolve against that version (`git show fcf98a3:skills/grill-with-docs/SKILL.md`). This critique served as the design brief for the same-day repairs (commit `a6dd56c`), which kept the name, the shape, the format files, and the premise.

I read the bundle, its full commit history back to the import, its provenance lock, its three format-file consumers, the domain-doc contract its write-back serves, and — what no precedent target had at this scale — its real fires: thirty marker-matched transcripts across five repositories, from the founding burst of June 5–15 to the resurgence of June 30–July 2, including every question asked, every recommendation issued, every glossary write, every ADR, and every ending. What follows is pressure on the method itself.

**TLDR:** The third hold, with a shape neither prior hold wore. This is the only target in the arc whose epistemology was imported — Matt Pocock's, byte-preserved through eight edge-commits, never re-founded — and the import turns out to be empirically the best-instrumented dialogic method in the library: it routes questions to reality instead of the user, lets code testify against testimony, prices its durable records with a real three-part gate, and its write-back demonstrably pays — the record's best catches are glossary clauses handing downstream adversarial reviewers their ammunition. What the fires expose is not a false premise but a method that cannot tell which of two games it is playing. Against a contesting counterparty it is a decision forge: its recommended answers get shredded, corrected, out-argued, and the artifacts record a real negotiation. Against a ratifying counterparty it is specification-completion wearing an interrogation costume: the griller authors both the question and the answer, the user copies the answer back — through an affordance this library deliberately sharpened the day before this critique — and seventeen consecutive decisions land with zero friction. The method has no sensor for the difference, and it writes the same authority either way: files that `docs/agents/domain.md` binds every future engineering session to obey, that resumed sessions carry as "rulings to honor," that reviewers cite by line number — while the one cooling control, leave writes uncommitted for the user's review, is defeated in practice by a global auto-commit rule and closeout habit. No written record has yet been caught wrong downstream; the record's one casualty is a decision settled by assent that the code already contradicted, caught a phase late. The defect, in one line: authority that outruns provenance. The repairs are honesty, not machinery.

## 1. An epistemology on loan

This target is unique in the arc twice over, and the first uniqueness frames everything: the method is not this library's. Commit `5e52b7c` (2026-06-05) imported it verbatim from `mattpocock/skills` (`skills/engineering/grill-with-docs`, pinned by folder hash in `.skill-lock.json`, never refreshed), and the eight commits since are all edge work: a routing description (`f284286`), an optional ADR field (`b68b952`), consumer bookkeeping (`ae061d4`, `f6feadc`), the write-safety clause with its proof boundary (`1971ab8`), a stop condition and opener ported from `grill-me` rather than derived (`2d2e75b`), a whitespace reflow (`ca87274`), and a display affordance (`be460cd`). The interrogation core — "Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one" (SKILL.md:10) — is byte-identical to the import. `CONTEXT-FORMAT.md` is byte-identical entire. No commit ever subjected the method itself to this library's design gates; every review that touched it (the 48-sweep, the apparatus review, the seven-must-fix wave) worked routing, safety, and delivery edges. Exactly one prior finding ever pressured the method — R-D(a), 2026-06-13: the doc-grounding differentiator lives entirely in `<supporting-info>`, so the interview loop can run as an undifferentiated `grill-me` clone — and it was parked with a reopen bar ("if grill-with-docs ever underperforms as a distinct skill") that nobody ever re-visited.

Imported is not indicted; the question is whether the loan holds. The original's implicit theory is a serious one, and it is worth stating at full strength before pressuring it: precision is enforced by conflict against two arbiters independent of the interrogator's judgment — a written glossary and the code — and durability is priced, not free: only decisions that are hard to reverse, surprising without context, and genuine trade-offs get written down (SKILL.md:86-88). That triad — artifacts testify, records cost something — is epistemically ahead of both skills this arc inverted: `outcome-interviewer` had no external anchor at all, and `making-recommendations` priced nothing. But the import also carries an assumed counterparty it never names. The text is written in the user's first person — "Interview me relentlessly," "ask what I want to be grilled on" — a prompt in the voice of its author, a domain-owning human expert who expects to push back. Nothing in the method asks whether that user is the one who showed up. The fires show three different counterparties showed up, and the method behaved identically for all of them. That is the crack everything else in this critique runs through.

## 2. What the fires actually are

The recorded corpus: thirty transcripts match the skill's body marker (28 Codex rollouts, 2 Claude-ledger sessions). Roughly a third are detection noise — the marker sentence is shared verbatim with `grill-me`, so any session that merely reads either file (scrutiny overlap checks, description audits, the import commit itself) matches; both `career` transcripts are this noise, which is why nothing personal appears here. What remains is about sixteen genuine grillings (eight full, eight partial) plus a handful of fires of a mode the text does not know exists. The venues split cleanly: a founding burst in `cross-model` (June 5–15), a resurgence in `reddit-app` and `markdown-reader` (June 30–July 2) — and zero product in `.agents` itself: no grilling has ever created a `CONTEXT.md` or ADR in this repo. The method's entire output record lives in field repos, which is exactly what AGENTS.md's global-capability doctrine predicts and no strike against it.

The unknown mode first, because it is the purest evidence of where the skill's gravity actually sits: four founding-week fires invoked `$grill-with-docs` after convergence, purely for the docs machinery. "Using `grill-with-docs` for closeout: the design is converged, so I'm not going to reopen questions" — then status flips, glossary capture of terms the user pre-named, commit, push. Zero questions. In one of these the agent wrote three glossary definitions lifted from an ADR with no confirmation from anyone. Users summon this skill as the custodian of `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/` — the invocation token for the artifact layer, interrogation not included. That reading of the skill's identity is corroborated from three independent directions: the R-D(a) park said the interrogation is the undifferentiated part; the June 14 keep-separate panel ruled the durable-doc-writer half is what makes it a different job from `grill-me`; and `decision-record` was later built by reusing this skill's format file as canon. The name says grill. The ecosystem treats it as the registrar.

## 3. The griller holds every pen

Now the interrogation itself. The core instruction makes the griller author of both sides: every question ships with "your recommended answer, always in a fenced Markdown code block in chat for easy copying" (SKILL.md:10) — the fence added by `be460cd` on July 2, hours before this treatment was commissioned, because the July 1 fires showed the user already copy-pasting unfenced recommendations back as answers and wanting it easier. The affordance is owned, deliberate, and it works: in the two sessions served the fenced text, 33 of 33 recommendations were fenced, and the user's dominant reply became a literal paste of the griller's own block, sometimes edited, often a bare "yes."

Whether that is a pathology depends entirely on who is answering, and the record contains both answers. In the founding `cross-model` fires the counterparty — frequently another frontier model's analysis relayed through JP, structured as BLUF verdicts with typed findings — rejected or rewrote nearly everything: one session ran 0/4 recommendations accepted clean, including a griller verdict of "no typed gaps" that was flatly wrong twice over; another had the same invariant gap corrected by the counterparty three arms running; a third re-ran the griller's proposed merge against disk and split it three ways. There the method is a forge: the committed recommendation is a stake for the counterparty to attack, and the attack produces the value — though honesty requires noting the transcripts overstate the griller's own contribution, since most catches arrived in the counterparty's pasted counter-analyses, not through the griller's follow-ups. In the resurgence fires the counterparty is a product owner shaping his own greenfield apps, and acceptance runs 80–100% verbatim: 22 of 24, 16 of 16, 17 of 17 — seventeen consecutive decisions in the Local Images session resolved in the framing direction of the question, zero premises rejected, zero recommendations overturned. Every fire reader independently flagged the same thing: the transcript cannot distinguish well-aimed recommendations from a counterparty that has stopped contesting.

Read carefully, the resurgence grillings are not a failure — they are a different, genuinely valuable product mislabeled. The questions are good: "You accepted `.gif` and `.webp` in the extension list, which can be animated. That is a trap if we leave it implicit" is real trap-hunting, and the session hardened a V1 scope across sizing, selection, SVG, animation, nesting, and identity — a complete, careful specification pass summoned by one token, which is precisely the cognitive-offload merit AGENTS.md tells us to price. But nobody in that room is being grilled. The agent is completing a specification, one proposed decision at a time, and the interview format is the consent UI. "Until we reach a shared understanding" (SKILL.md:10) is satisfied in both regimes — and in the second, what is shared is the griller's understanding, ratified. The method's failure is not that this happens; it is that the method cannot see it happening and does not say it. A run of verbatim acceptances is information the contract currently discards.

## 4. What the stop condition measures

"Stop when I say the plan feels solid or ask to stop, or when further questions stop changing the plan" (SKILL.md:16). The first clause has never fired: across thirty transcripts, no user ever said solid. The founding-week sessions end the same way almost every time — the user pivots to `handoff:save-handoff` mid-question, leaving the final question unanswered and the mandated close (decisions summary, weakest remaining assumption, file report) unrendered. The resurgence shows real, creditable drift: the griller learned to invoke the second clause itself — "I think we're near the point where grilling is no longer changing the core plan" — and offer the exit as a question the user ratifies. Once it even read the clause with precision, ending a 34-question session because the answers had shifted from product clarification to sequencing, which is the clause doing exactly its job.

Note what the clause is, though: a fixed point of the griller's own process, self-graded. The griller generates the questions whose exhaustion is measured and authors the answers that determine whether the plan changes — in the ratifying regime, "further questions stop changing the plan" reduces to "my questions stopped changing my answers." The permission question redeems it in practice (the user can always say keep going), and the close's "single weakest remaining assumption" is the best sentence the stop owns — a real epistemic deliverable when it appears. The observed defect is narrower than the philosophy: the close evaporates. Delivered fully once in the resurgence, partially once (weakest assumption named, file report skipped), and twice steamrolled when the user's ratification of the exit question arrived bundled with next-phase instructions. And under the dominant founding-week ending — the mid-question pivot — nothing at all is reported, in a method whose distinguishing feature is that it has been writing durable files all session.

## 5. Code as arbiter: the best clause, and its one casualty

"If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead" (SKILL.md:14) is the most reliably honored sentence in the record, and it is the clause the arc's first inversion demanded and never had: reality outranks testimony. Fires open by reading the glossary, the ADRs, and the live code before the first question; questions carry "Code reality:" preambles; exploration reframes questions before they are asked — "Code also confirms link resolution already rejects fragments/query/schemes... so our first real design branch is image-path scope rather than whether images belong in V1" — and twice a mid-session re-read of the renderer pivoted an entire plan from "write it" to "spike first." Where the clause idled, the repo was genuinely greenfield. This is the import's deepest virtue and nothing in this critique touches it.

But the clause has a direction, and the record found the gap on its blind side. "Cross-reference with code" binds the user's claims: "When the user states how something works, check whether the code agrees" (SKILL.md:74). Nothing binds the griller's own recommendations to the same check, and the record's one real casualty walked through exactly that hole: Q11 of the Tab Links session settled a closed extension set — `.md`/`.markdown` only — on a griller recommendation issued without reading `MarkdownOpenPanel.swift`, which already supported four extensions. The contradiction surfaced one phase later, during implementation planning, after the decision had been ratified and recorded. A decision settled by assent that the code already contradicted is this method's worst product — worse than an unanswered question, because it wears the costume of a resolution — and the method's own instrument would have caught it if the instrument pointed both ways.

## 6. The glossary: legislation, and where its value actually lives

What is a `CONTEXT.md` entry evidence of? The format is honest about its ambition: "Be opinionated... pick the best one and list the others under `_Avoid_`" (CONTEXT-FORMAT.md:27). This is not description; it is language legislation — the DDD ubiquitous-language bet that choosing and enforcing terms is design work. The library then gives the legislation teeth the import never had: `docs/agents/domain.md` orders every engineering skill to read the glossary before exploring, to "use the term as defined in `CONTEXT.md`," and never to "drift to synonyms the glossary explicitly avoids" (domain.md:23), naming `/grill-with-docs` as the sole producer (domain.md:14). One conversational assent, possibly to a term the griller itself proposed ("propose a precise canonical term," SKILL.md:66), becomes binding vocabulary for every future session.

The headline defensive move — "Challenge against the glossary: call it out immediately" (SKILL.md:62) — fired twice in sixteen grillings, and both firings were real: the July 1 session caught the user's tab answer reversing the recorded "one file at a time" scope term, said "this is exactly the kind of contradiction grill-with-docs is meant to catch," and rewrote the term rather than letting the copies disagree. Twice is thin, but the occasions were thin too — young glossaries, few conflicts to catch — so this is unproven-not-broken, stated plainly. The glossary's demonstrated value sits elsewhere, in a place the skill's text undersells: downstream bindingness. The freshly written "not upscaled" clause was independently cited by three adversarial reviewers to catch the same real defect in the implementation plan; "tabs in one window" caught a `WindowGroup` violation the same way; the settled storage ADR caught a `UserDefaults` drift. The write-back is not a side effect of the grilling; on this record it is the majority of the payoff.

Two bendings deserve names. First, the glossary-and-nothing-else rule (SKILL.md:80) holds in letter and bends in spirit under load: the Local Images session packed sizing behavior, selection promises, failure-copy rules, and layout policy into two freight-train "definitional" sentences, and repurposed `_Avoid_` — the synonym-hygiene field — into a rejected-features list ("clickable image, animated media, inline layout fidelity"). The driver is structural, not careless: an easy-to-reverse scope decision fails the ADR gate by design, is not vocabulary, and the method offers it no third home, so it squats in the glossary. The session even knew it, reciting "product language, not renderer design" fourteen times like a spell against the thing it was doing. Second, the entries' epistemic grade is uniform when their provenance is not: a term negotiated under real counterpressure, a term ratified in a copy-paste run, and a term written unilaterally from ADR text in a closeout fire all land in identical format, and `decision-record` documents the shared convention that a statusless record is "accepted" (decision-record SKILL.md:31). The file remembers none of the difference.

## 7. The ADR gate: honest when run, silent when not

The three-part gate (SKILL.md:84-90) is the import's anti-ceremony masterpiece — "If any of the three is missing, skip the ADR" — and the fires show it doing something rare for structure in this library: refusing work. Declined honestly three times in the resurgence ("not hard enough to reverse to justify an ADR"; "the existing Application Support reader-state ADR is enough"; "easy to revisit," the criterion named), walked explicitly criterion-by-criterion before ADR-0006 and ADR-0018 ("Run the gate: costly to reverse... yes... The gate fires on its own terms"), and applied once with a Revisit-when trigger. When the gate runs, it runs true. But it runs only when remembered, and the record shows it forgotten in both directions: an entire 17-question session with a decision matching ADR-FORMAT's own worked example ("boundary and scope decisions," ADR-FORMAT.md:45) never invoked the gate at all, and the founding-week closeout fires flipped ADR statuses to accepted with no visible gate reasoning anywhere. The second Claude session applied the gate loosely where the first had walked it fully — the gate decays across repeat use in the same lane.

There is also a quieter tension inside the gate's first criterion. "Hard to reverse" selects for exactly the decisions where hot capture is most consequential — the ADRs worth writing are the ones a wrong version of which costs the most — and a grilling ADR is written minutes after the decision exists, by the same agent that recommended it, in the same breath. The counter-virtue is real and should be said just as plainly: rationale captured hot is rationale captured true — `decision-record`'s whole discipline exists because cold capture invites retrofitted tidy justifications, and a grilling ADR never needs to reconstruct why. Heat buys fidelity of rationale and costs settledness of choice. The method currently prices neither; it just writes. The library already built the cooling apparatus — genealogy, supersession, `Revisit when` — in `decision-record` and `ADR-FORMAT.md`; what grilling ADRs lack is only the honesty that they are hot.

## 8. Write-back: the loop is real, the payoff is real, the cooling is broken

Assemble the full chain the way a future session experiences it. A grilling writes `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/*` mid-session, while decisions are minutes old. `domain.md` binds every later engineering session to that vocabulary and those decisions. Resumed grillings load "key rulings to honor" from the previous session's writes and re-verify only that they exist on disk, never whether they were right. The next session's griller treats a day-old glossary entry as a branch-forecloser — "our first real design branch is image-path scope rather than whether images belong in V1," where "images belong in V1" was itself a hot assent from the previous evening. Adversarial reviewers cite the entries by line number as binding product contract. This is the design working: continuity of settled language is the value, and §6's three-reviewer catch is this exact loop paying out. The record also contains — stated plainly, as the commission required — no observed casualty of it: across thirty transcripts, no written glossary term or ADR was later found wrong and caused damage. The one near-miss (three unilateral closeout terms nobody ever confirmed) is a documented risk, not an incident.

The reason this is nonetheless the critique's sharpest finding is that the method's one cooling control is observably defeated. The contract's answer to hot authority is "do not commit — leave the changes for the user to review" (SKILL.md:94): the dirty worktree is the cooling pond, and the user's review-before-commit is the ratification the assent never was. In practice: one session auto-committed twelve consecutive glossary edits mid-grill because the Codex-side global Markdown auto-commit rule lacks the skill-override carve-out its Claude-side twin gained in the philosophy-audit resolution (`contract-decisions.md`, item 2) — the agent noticed once ("the Markdown closeout commit that your global instructions require"), then never surfaced the collision again; both Claude cross-model sessions committed mid-flow; the founding closeout fires committed and pushed on user instruction; and the Tab Links session's glossary edits were folded into the implementation plan's commit with no distinct review checkpoint. Of the resurgence grillings, only the reddit-app and Local Images sessions left their writes cooling as designed; the founding markdown-reader session, the Reading Positions session, and the Tab Links session did not. The proof-boundary sentence — "you recorded glossary and decision text, not verified implementation" (SKILL.md:94) — is the most honest line the adaptations added, and it protects only the session that emits it; nothing downstream ever reads it. So the artifacts carry more authority than any moment in their production earned, the step that was supposed to add the missing ratification usually doesn't happen, and the files themselves are silent about all of it. Authority outruns provenance.

## 9. What transfers to grill-me

The interrogation core is shared with `grill-me` nearly verbatim — the relentless framing, one-at-a-time, recommended answers (unfenced there), explore-instead-of-ask, and the stop sentence differing only in the close (grill-me: "no written artifact unless I ask") — kept deliberately separate by the June 14 panels, whose keep-separate ruling and single-home skip this critique does not disturb. Findings that live in the shared core transfer: the counterparty-blindness of §3 (a ratification run is invisible to the contract), the dead first stop clause and pivot-evaporated close of §4, and §5's directional gap in cross-referencing. Findings in the artifact layer — everything in §§6-8 — do not transfer; grill-me writes nothing, which is precisely why the panels called them different jobs. Per the commission, no grill-me edit is made here; sibling repairs are a follow-on for JP to commission. One environment-level finding also transfers out of this skill entirely: the Codex-side global auto-commit rule needs the same skill-override carve-out the Claude-side rule already has (§8) — that is an always-loaded contract change in a user-level file, charter-gated, and explicitly not made as a side effect of this treatment.

## 10. What survives, and the honest reframe

Nearly everything survives, and more of it under live-fire evidence than any precedent could claim. Explore-instead-of-ask and code-as-arbiter: honored, observed changing plans, kept untouched. The three-part ADR gate: observed refusing ceremony in both directions, kept verbatim. Lazy file creation, the glossary-only fence, inline capture, the scenario probing, the one-at-a-time discipline (respected in eleven of sixteen real grillings, and principled here — "resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one" is the contingency argument the interviewer critique had to construct for its target; this skill states it): kept. The write-safety clause and proof boundary: kept and extended. The format files: byte-stable trust surfaces serving three consumers by relative path, the single-sourcing already working — untouched, in place, because their location is load-bearing independent of anything this critique found. The fence affordance of `be460cd`: kept — it serves the owned workflow, and the record shows it honored perfectly once served (33/33 fenced in the sessions that received the clause, 0/41 in the sessions that predate it — the compliance story is entirely a served-version story). The name: kept. "Grill" oversells the adversarial register in one of the method's two observed regimes, but the philosophy does not demand a rename, and this bundle carries the highest rename blast radius in the arc (two relative-path consumers, five routing surfaces, a seed doc, and a shared numbering namespace) for what would be cosmetics; if anything, the honest half of the name is "-with-docs."

The honest reframe is not an inversion, and it is not the precedents' costume-stripping either — this method's instruments are real, not liturgy. It is a boundary the import never drew because its author never needed it: the method assumes its counterparty supplies the pressure, and its artifacts assume the assent was earned. When both assumptions hold — a contesting counterparty, a reviewed commit — this is the best dialogic skill in the library, and the record's founding week shows it forging real design under real pressure. When neither holds, the same text runs a consent conveyor into a legislature: agent-authored decisions, frictionlessly ratified, instantly binding on every future session, with the designed cooling step silently skipped. The record shows both, shows the method blind to the difference, and shows the environment defeating the one control that was supposed to matter — while also showing, honestly, that nothing has yet broken. The repairs (`a6dd56c`) therefore add no machinery and change no formats. They make the method able to say what it is doing: notice and name a ratification run instead of calling it shared understanding; point the code-check at its own recommendations, where the record's one casualty got through; report files and the open question even when the session is yanked away mid-question; keep glossary and ADR commits isolated and name the bypassed review when the environment forces commits; close with the provenance the artifacts cannot carry themselves — these records are this session's assents, not survived decisions; scan the session's decisions once at close for a gated record that was never offered; and when summoned after convergence with nothing to interrogate, say so, confirm terms before writing them, and route standalone decision capture to `decision-record`, which owns it.

The heart of it: this skill believes relentless interrogation produces shared understanding, and that its glossary and ADR trail record that understanding. What the record shows is subtler on both ends — the interrogation's pressure is borrowed from whoever answers, and the trail's authority is granted by whoever reads it later, while the method stands between them holding every pen and keeping no ledger of which was which. The questions are good, the instruments are real, the records demonstrably pay their way. What was missing is the sentence a registrar owes the future: this is what was actually agreed, by whom, under how much pressure, and how hot it still was when it was written down.
