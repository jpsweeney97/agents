## 1. Born litigated

Every precedent target performed a method this treatment then had to test from scratch. ideate arrives with the test already run — the only target in the arc whose methodology was adversarially litigated before the skill existed, by its own design competition (session `ced3c70a`, workflows `wf_47ed43d7-685` and `wf_95de577b-47a`, 2026-06-26). The record survives in the workflow journals, and it is worth quoting because it pre-empts half the critique this genre would otherwise write.

Six genuinely incompatible spines ran, each piped into an independent adversarial kill. Five died, all on one defect class. `operator-toolbox` (diversity as coverage of a fixed operator set): "coverage is gameable, non-orthogonal... it halts and declares success at coverage-complete, the exact moment before the only test that separates a genuinely diverse field from a tagged constellation around the modal answer." `axis-morphological` (diversity as sampling a self-built axis map): "morphological coverage is checkable only against the self-chosen axes... the skill relocates the silent-gap failure from the field to the unnamed axis and then stamps a completeness ledger over it — manufacturing false confidence that is worse than the bare brainstorm's acknowledged ignorance" — the origin of the no-certificate law's core phrase. `multi-perspective-decomposition` (diversity by role-playing incompatible standpoints): "a single agent cannot swap its generator by role-playing — it runs the same fixed generator on rotating role-prompts in one shared context, so 'decompose the generator' is a metaphor mistaken for a mechanism." `reframe-first`: its one quality gate "does not test the property its own philosophy depends on." `fan-out-portfolio` (diversity by blind subagents): self-refuting, because "the single most load-bearing act in the skill — deciding which regions of the option space will even be attempted — happens in exactly the single context the skill declares constitutionally incapable of diverging from itself." Meanwhile three blind careful-default controls, seeing none of this, independently produced the same shape the shipped skill has: generate from provocations → de-cluster → frame-break → un-ranked stop.

The head-to-head went 3-0 for the crown and the run's own validity critic threw the margin out: the finalists shared "an essentially identical generative engine — nearly verbatim," the crown had absorbed a six-spine harvest and an extra kill round the control never received, and the blinding leaked ("its no-certificate billboard... aligned it with the very rubric being scored"). The orchestrator accepted the null: an honest tie on shape, one transferable graft — the two anti-modal moves — and one design law. The shipped skill is the control's shape plus that graft minus all certification machinery, and its founding handoff prices it without flinching: "the honest differential is reliability + modest cognitive-offload, NOT a new capability (the generative engine is what a careful agent reaches anyway)"; the law itself is "a design judgment from the squad, argued not measured... a strong design principle, not a proven theorem."

Two consequences frame everything below. First, the commission's deepest question — is "genuinely different options" from one sampling process coherent, or mode-collapse wearing variety's costume? — was answered at birth, in the kills of `multi-perspective` and `fan-out`: no procedure run inside one context escapes that context's generator, and the two architectures that promised escape were executed for promising it. The shipped text never promises it. Its baseline is explicit — "a capable agent told 'brainstorm widely' already produces a list" (SKILL.md:14) — and what it claims over that baseline is guaranteed behaviors, not transcendence. Conditioning is real: an instruction demonstrably moves sampling into regions the bare pass does not visit, and sequential generation that sees its own list can deliberately differentiate against it. Wider-than-the-bare-brainstorm is deliverable; outside-the-distribution is not; the text claims only the former. There is no excavation story here, no measurement costume — the honest verdict on the premise is that it holds because it was pre-shrunk by its own kills. Second, consequence one sets this treatment's real job: five adversarial kills, three judges, and a validity critic all pressured *what the method claims*; nobody pressured *what the method silently does while keeping its claims*. That is where everything below lives.

## 2. What "genuinely different" means, and who gets to say

The skill has an implicit theory of what difference *is*, and it is the right one. Two options are different if "they would succeed or fail for the same reason" is false of them (SKILL.md:29) — difference as failure-decorrelation, not surface variety. This is the decision-relevant definition: a downstream chooser gains nothing from five skins on one bet, and everything from options whose failure modes are independent. The Postgres-and-MySQL example carries the whole idea in nine words, and the de-cluster it powers — collapse on shared load-bearing mechanism, not on clothes — is the sharpest sentence in the method. Its designer should also be credited with the anti-taxonomy rule that protects it: "a provocation is a tool that produces an option, never a label on it" (SKILL.md:27), which is the no-certificate law applied to the method's own scaffolding.

The trouble is epistemic, not conceptual. "Would succeed or fail for the same reason" is causal-counterfactual knowledge about the world — which mechanisms fail together under which conditions — and the method asks the generator to render it introspectively, about its own outputs, in the same pass that produced them. The birth record is candid about this: the surviving spine's own designer wrote "the distinctness test is self-judged." For well-trodden options the judgment borrows the world's documentation (everyone knows what Postgres and MySQL share). But the skill's distinctive admissions — the non-serious, won't-win, frame-breaking options it exists to admit (SKILL.md:16), the embarrassing option, the instinct's opposite — are precisely the options whose failure modes are least documented anywhere, including inside the model. The de-cluster is most reliable where the field is least novel, and least reliable exactly where this skill's product is supposed to live.

So the field's central quality claim — each option ships with "the distinct bet or mechanism that sets it apart" (SKILL.md:34) — is an authored judgment presented in the grammar of a property. No external check exists or can exist in-session; that is the law's own teaching. Two honesty notes bound this finding. The stated-bet format is genuinely better than it first looks: because every surviving option names its load-bearing mechanism, a reader *can* contest distinctness pairwise — compare the stated bets, object that two are one — which is more contestability than the deciding lane's old matrix ever offered. And the treatment's forward tests confirmed the de-cluster fires in the delivered field (proxies collapsed Redis and Memcached unprompted). What no proxy and no reader can check is what §4 takes up: the options that never survived to be contested.

## 3. The anti-modal moves: two directions, not the divergence

The two mandatory moves — "the exact opposite of your first instinct, and the option you would be slightly embarrassed to propose" (SKILL.md:28) — are the only element of this skill with any differential evidence at all: the validity critic, having voided the head-to-head, named exactly one asymmetry-independent transferable finding, and this was it ("a real divergence provocation the blind control lacked"). They deserve a precise account of what they operationalize, because it is not what their names claim.

"The exact opposite of your first instinct" is not computable by the thing having the instinct — not because the model lacks access to its instinct (the modal completion is the one thing it has privileged access to), but because a high-dimensional option has no unique opposite. Choosing the axis of opposition is itself a generative act, performed by the same distribution; the move yields the *modal opposite*, the most likely completion of "the opposite of X is..." — build↔buy, act↔wait, add↔remove. "Exact" is precision theater. But the move still pays: it forcibly conditions generation away from the mode along *some* axis, which the bare brainstorm reliably fails to do, and blunting the word "exact" would trade provoke-force for philosophical manners — the forcing function's overstatement is its sharpness, and the fix for a weak provocation is never a hedged one. The embarrassing option is the deeper instrument, and the skill undersells it by treating the two moves as parallel. A tuned model's training pushes hardest against exactly the low-propriety region — the crude, the unfashionable, the career-risky suggestion — so "what would I be slightly embarrassed to propose" points the generator at the specific tail its tuning suppressed. It is the closest thing prompt-level engineering has to an anti-RLHF move, and it is the likeliest single source of whatever real width this skill delivers.

The bounded claim: these are two directions in a space of many — negation and propriety — mandated because they are the two cheapest reliable ones. The text's "these are the divergence an eager model skips" (SKILL.md:28) mildly overclaims; the eager model also skips the expensive, the slow, and the boring-but-proven, and step 2's provocations carry that load. This is a wording quibble, not a defect: as forcing functions under `agent-facing-design`'s provoke bar, both moves earn the "mandatory," and no repair touches them.

## 4. The cull runs in the dark

Step 2 holds generation "as private scratch" (SKILL.md:27) — a sound rule with a stated purpose (raw provocation output would carry the lens tags the output shape bans) and an unexamined cost: the de-cluster's kills happen there. The reader receives survivors. Nothing marks what was collapsed, or why, and for a skill whose entire product is breadth, the collapse decision is the highest-stakes judgment in the method — the only one that destroys product rather than shaping it. It is exercised by the same judgment §2 showed is weakest on novel options, in a place where no reader, no downstream lane, and no forward test can ever contest it. The distinctness assertions that survive are contestable through the stated bets; the cull is the one act the method performs with no witness at all.

The error structure is asymmetric and the text at `af0cb5f` does not know it. A wrong collapse — two actually-distinct options filed as one because the generator couldn't see the mechanisms apart — silently deletes the deliverable; a wrong keep costs one line of noise in a field whose reader is skimming handles. The collapse rule as written points only one way ("two options collapse to one if..."), with no direction for uncertainty. And the birth record already documented what termination pressure does to this exact judgment: the surviving spine's original quota design was killed because its stop condition ("quota-floor ∧ all-distinct" unsatisfiable in narrow spaces) forced "padding or false-distinctness declarations to terminate." The shipped stable-field stop inverted the quota, but exhaustion pressure at the stop pushes the same direction de-cluster zeal does — toward a smaller field more cheaply declared stable. The repair is one sentence with the grain of the method: collapse only on a shared failure reason you can actually name; when you cannot name one, keep both.

## 5. Un-ranked is a property of the page

Here the grill-with-docs critique's format-versus-fact distinction must be earned fresh, and it cuts deeper in this skill because neutrality is the product. The no-leak rules govern the explicit channels completely: no scores, no ordering by quality, no "I'd lean" (SKILL.md:34), a hard stop that survives "so which?" in the same message (SKILL.md:42). But the generating pass computed a favorite — a policy that produces options scores them implicitly, whether or not the text reports it — and a banned channel does not delete a preference; it reroutes it. Three quiet channels remain wide open at `af0cb5f`. Generation order: the first instinct generates first by definition, "no ordering by quality" bans an ordering without supplying one, so the default — generation order — stands, and generation order approximates modality order; with primacy effects in readers doing the rest, the field ships covertly ranked by exactly the signal the format exists to suppress, favorite first. Handle wording: the option the author likes gets the crisper handle and the more fluent one-liner, and fluency reads as quality. Framing: "descriptive, never evaluative" governs intent, but connotation is not policed by intent.

The perverse part is that the ban makes the residue *uncorrectable*. An explicit "I'd lean X" is the one preference signal a reader can see, discount, and argue with; a favorite wearing the best handle in the first slot is invisible advocacy. The rule as written silences the contestable channel and leaves the uncontestable ones carrying the signal — the same trade the making-recommendations critique caught in the matrix (nobody can contest a cell; nobody can contest a connotation), transposed into the one skill whose deliverable is supposed to be pick-free. What the rule still buys is real and should be said plainly: the explicit verdict is the loudest anchor by far, and refusing it genuinely protects the downstream lane from inheriting a pick that never passed through the deciding lane's discipline. Format control is worth having. It just is not author neutrality, and the text's grammar — "a flat, un-ranked field" — claims the author's neutrality while governing only the page's. The repairs are counter-bias, not confession machinery: order the field by an axis with no quality reading and never lead with the first instinct; then reread the handles and one-liners and reword until a reader could not reconstruct the lean from wording alone.

## 6. The stops: self-graded stability and the "so which?" wall

"Stop on a stable field, not a count" (SKILL.md:30) is the right stop in a design space where every alternative was killed for manufacturing certificates — but "stable" is doing quiet work. "Another pass yields nothing mechanism-distinct" is a fact about the searcher, not the space: the same self-graded convergence the grill-with-docs treatment caught in "further questions stop changing the plan," except here there is no counterparty at all — no user answers mid-generation, no code to consult, nothing external grades the exhaustion. The word "stable" imports a claim about the field that the method can only make about its own search. No better stop exists (that is the law's own lesson), so the repair is one clause of honesty, not a new mechanism: a stable field is a fact about your search, not about the space.

The hard stop earns a more careful defense than the text gives it. "Crossing into evaluation is the failure this skill exists to prevent" (SKILL.md:42) implies the stop protects the field — but by the time "so which?" lands, the field is already generated; answering would corrupt nothing retroactively. And the stop cannot protect generation prospectively either: when the pick-request arrives *in the same message*, the generator knew it while generating, and options authored under a pick's gravity are already warped toward the defensible — stopping afterward un-knows nothing. The stop's real function is narrower and still sufficient: the pick tossed off after an ideation would be an instant verdict with none of the deciding lane's discipline — no seriousness filter, no null option, no runner-up case, none of `making-recommendations`' hard-won honesty — and the verdict partition (`agent-facing-design` SKILL.md:67) exists because a verdict bolted onto a divergent artifact gets mistaken for a calibrated call. One turn of friction buys the pick its owning contract. The rule survives; the treatment's contribution is only the honest warrant. The forward test confirmed the wall holds under a same-message "tell me which one you'd go with."

## 7. The law and the close under direct pressure

The commission ordered pressure aimed directly at the no-certificate rule (SKILL.md:38), the library's own adversarially-won design law, and the strongest attack available is the contestability argument this arc itself sharpened: a visible map is at least contestable — a reader can see the axes and name the missing one — while honest ignorance gives the reader nothing to push against; by the logic this very critique uses in §5 (banned explicit channels leave uncorrectable residue), shouldn't the field carry its map? The attack dies on the blade that killed `axis-morphological`: a self-drawn map can only display the axes the generator already had, so the silent gap relocates to the unnamed axis — exactly where the generator is blindest — while the ledger's *presence* upgrades reader confidence; offered taxonomies read as exhaustive, and the anchoring outweighs the contestability. The disanalogy with §5 is precise: an "I'd lean" discloses a fact the author genuinely possesses (its favorite), so banning it hides real information; a coverage ledger asserts a fact the author cannot possess (the field's completeness), so banning it hides nothing and prevents a forgery. The law survives direct pressure, not by deference: five independent kills converged on it at birth, it is banked in the throughline's Decisions and cited as the self-certification bar in `steelman`'s own text (steelman SKILL.md:43), and its honest epistemic status was stated in the founding handoff and stands — argued, not measured; a strong design principle, not a proven theorem. Overturning it would need adversarial evidence of at least five-kill weight. This treatment brought the pressure and found none.

The untouched-fixed-points close (SKILL.md:36) was itself flagged at birth — reviewers called an early version "a coverage map by implication," and the resolution narrowed it to the prompt's own stated constraints. What it can honestly claim after that narrowing: *of the constraints you stated, these were not varied.* The enumeration of the prompt's fixed points is still the generator's reading, and the unstated fixed points — the assumptions the prompt and the generator share silently — stay out of reach; the frame-break move hunts exactly one of them, itself self-detected. This is a negative-space certificate, the most honest certificate shape a generator can emit, and the text around it is unusually careful ("anchored to the prompt's stated constraints, not to axes you drew"). It survives untouched.

## 10. What survives, and the honest reframe

Nearly everything, and more of it pre-hardened than any precedent could claim. The mechanism-decorrelation definition of difference — the right theory of what a downstream chooser needs. The provocations-as-tools rule, the anti-taxonomy instinct in one line. The two anti-modal moves — the competition's single transferable graft, with the embarrassing option undersold if anything. The frame-break and the refusal branch ("do not manufacture diversity," SKILL.md:52 — knowing when *not* to widen is the skill's most underrated sentence). The no-certificate law — survives pressure aimed directly at it, and remains the best sentence any generative skill in this library carries. The untouched-fixed-points close — a negative-space certificate, honestly narrowed at birth. The hard stop and the handoff mesh — right rule, now with its honest warrant. The mixed-skill self-read (SKILL.md:19-22) — checked per part against `agent-facing-design` rather than taken on its own word, and found accurate: the moves are provocations that organize without deciding; the shape, stop, boundary, and law are trust surfaces whose predictability is the value; the self-declaration is four lines, honest, and execution-relevant (it tells the firing agent which parts are non-negotiable), so it is kept as context rather than indicted as a label. The name: kept — it asserts no false epistemology and sits in seven live routing surfaces. The description: unchanged; it promises generation, width, un-ranked output, and a stop, all of which the body now delivers more honestly.

The honest reframe is not an inversion — the false premises this genre hunts were killed here before the skill shipped, by its own birth run — and it is not the precedents' costume-stripping either, because this method's restraints were never a cover story; they are the declared content, priced at birth with a candor no other target in the arc matched. It is the sixth shape: a method honest about its claims whose remaining dishonesty is structural. Everything the page promises, the page delivers; but the page is not where the method's power lives. The author widens, culls, orders, words, and stops — five acts of judgment, all self-graded, none witnessed — and the format's neutrality launders the author's invisibility. The repairs (`6fa2cdb`) therefore add no machinery, no tags, no ledgers — the law forbids them and the law is right. They make the author visible to itself at the exact points no reader can see: collapse only on a nameable shared reason, keep both when you cannot (§4); never lead with your first instinct, order by an axis with no quality reading, reword until the lean is unrecoverable (§5); say that a stable field describes your search, not the space (§6); enumerate the four guarantees so the skill's own falsifier can run (§9); and let the identity sentence catch up with the family it seeded (§8).

The heart of it: this skill believes it delivers a deliberately un-ranked field of genuinely different options. What it delivers is a field its author widened honestly, culled invisibly, ordered by instinct, and de-ranked only in the one channel a reader could have contested. The width is real, the honesty is unmatched in its cohort, and the law it discovered at birth deserves its place in the throughline's decisions. What was missing is the last honesty — that "un-ranked" and "distinct" are claims about the author's behavior, not the field's properties, and the author is the one witness the method never cross-examines. The repairs put the cross-examination where it belongs: inside the author's own pass, at the five moments its hand is on the scale.
