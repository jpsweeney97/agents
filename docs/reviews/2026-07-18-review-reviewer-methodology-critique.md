# Review-Reviewer: A Critique of the Methodology and Philosophy

> JP commissioned the sixteenth methodology-and-philosophy treatment, on `plugins/review-family/skills/review-reviewer`. Target pinned at `.agents` commit `c2a4fb1`; inline line citations resolve against that version (`git show c2a4fb1:plugins/review-family/skills/review-reviewer/SKILL.md`). JP endorsed the critique in full on 2026-07-18 (chat adjudication, session a940e78b); this is the endorsed design brief. The repairs landed at commit `ea2c743` as review-family 0.11.0; the repairs invalidate specific line numbers but not the readings.

**TLDR:** The fourteenth hold, with a shape none of the thirteen prior holds wore: **the method is the family's evidence discipline running one meta-level up, it demonstrably works — and what it does not own is its own success.** This is the arc's second-largest and arguably healthiest corpus: ~145 sessions and ~150 completed fires across seven venues and both runtimes, continuous from the skill's birth morning (authored 01:17, first fire by afternoon — adjudicating the PR review of its own plugin, where it *challenged and rejected* the finding that its own SKILL.md was too heavy) to two days before this critique. Explicit-only holds essentially perfectly: 183 ledger rows, all user-sourced, zero model-chosen summons, and a 43-file noise sweep found zero unledgered fires — the cleanest routing compliance in the treatment arc. When it fires, the core pays extravagantly: adjudicators stood up three-arm sandbox probes to overturn a High-severity claim ("committing the same proof-boundary error it accuses the plan of, in mirror image"), reproduced compile failures via `swift -e` before confirming them, executed a review's claimed git bug in a `mktemp` repo, extracted a plan's embedded runner and ran it to prove a scoring bug, challenged a review's charter citation by reading the charter, caught regressions the adjudicating agent had itself introduced two rounds earlier, and — in the corpus's peak specimen — spawned three blind subagents to read the target with no review text, audited one for contamination and excluded it, then overturned the review's "minor revision" to Major with four blocker-class missed issues. The read-only floor held in every properly-invoked fire; the packet's sections are alive, not ceremony — the 06-16 blind differential's bare-arm charge ("ceremony weight, machinery counts") is adjudicated by the fires and mostly loses. What the corpus indicts is subtler, and it has three faces. First, the **Review Judgment** — the third verdict stratum, a calibration-and-completeness grade on the *reviewer* — is the one instrument whose knowledge-claim outruns its method: the reliable/partially-reliable boundary is undefined and observably inconsistent (the same review-missed-a-real-issue shape drew `partially reliable` twice and `Reliable.` once inside a single session; eleven consecutive deliberate-arc fires stayed `reliable` while their own rationales logged real defects), and the record proves a `reliable` label expires — one pass graded a design `reliable` while accepting an either/or remedy that the next pass, cross-runtime, found charter-noncompliant and reversed; a 10/10-confirmed `reliable` adjudication was followed same-session by a fresh subagent finding a new High neither review nor adjudication caught. The judgment is a fact about this pass's bounded search, exactly Era 77's lesson one level up, and the label's grammar still reads like a fact about the reviewer. Second, the **echo-loop**: across every era, the modal aftermath is JP pasting the packet's own Recommended Next Step back verbatim as the next command — the adjudication drafts its own authorization, the human checkpoint is real but thin, and the record holds exactly one casualty living in that thinness: a nonexistent-function reference that survived two `reliable` adjudications into committed plan text until JP's own re-read caught it. Third, the **boundary of the discipline**: the packet's rigor lives inside the token — a bare-pasted review is owned by the ambient pasted-content rule and gets reproduce-then-patch with no adjudication artifact (working as designed, but a real two-regime seam); under convergence-loop momentum the seam failed twice in one day (a skill-body-load failure that free-improvised a patch with a same-turn commit, and a token-present round that announced "treat the pasted review as a current claim check first, then patch" and merged adjudication into mutation — the corpus's only true floor breaches, neither named by the four-mode failure taxonomy); and the stance has escaped the skill entirely — Codex distilled "treat the supplied review as allegations to test against live files" into a MEMORY.md line that now preloads into sessions and governs pasted reviews without the token, the strongest uptake evidence a skill in this library has ever produced and also a form of the method running unversioned, without its calibration honesty. The anchoring theory, honest since its founding-day correction (the pretense-of-blindness rule lasted nineteen minutes before "this is an anchoring mitigation, not a pristine blind read" replaced it), was outgrown by the fires in one specific direction the text never names: **self-authorship** — the dominant era-E use is an agent adjudicating reviews of its own same-session work, and the fires convergently improvised the disclosure ("the conflict runs deeper than anchoring — the adjudicator is the artifact's author") that the text's sequence-anchoring vocabulary cannot express. The repairs are honesty and naming, no new machinery: scope the Review Judgment as a search-fact that expires, let the label follow the findings, own the Recommended Next Step as the executable work order the record shows it is, name self-authorship and the loop-momentum packet-skip, bless the fires' generalized snapshot-coincidence check, and fix the label typography in the one skill family whose declared value is predictable shape. The stance, the truth verdicts, the dispositions, the Independent Assessment, the packet, the explicit-only boundary, the name: all stay.

## 3. What runs and pays

The commission pre-registered thin ritual — "no independent issues found" as the modal Independent Assessment, partial packet compliance, dead temporal vocabulary. **The corpus refutes nearly all of it.**

- **Packet fidelity is near-total.** In every properly-invoked fire across all twenty deep-read batches, all seven Full Adjudication sections rendered, in order (SKILL.md:121-173); era-E fidelity is 100% of completed fires. Claim normalization into parent/child IDs (SKILL.md:36) runs everywhere and does real work — the fires split compound findings into fact / spec-basis / consequence sub-claims (`R1.a/b/c`) so a true observation and a wrong remedy get separate verdicts.
- **The truth verdicts are earned with the family's best evidence discipline.** Adjudicators reproduced a claimed compile blocker via `swift -e` and *compiled the proposed fix* before confirming R1 (`019f34d6`); executed a review's claimed double-commit git bug in a throwaway `mktemp` repo to exact `exit=1` (`21c566c3`); extracted a plan's embedded `runner.py` into scratch and ran it to prove the wrong-skill-scoring bug (`669733da`); ran a three-arm `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` sandbox probe that **overturned a High-severity claim** and named the review's own epistemics — "The review promoted a documentation inference into a 'source-contradicted' behavioral fact — committing the same proof-boundary error it accuses the plan of, in mirror image" (`2c19e53f`, verified firsthand); and treated a review's self-reported rigor as itself unverified — "R0 ... needs-verification ... do not rely on the count; rely on inspected claims" on a pasted "67-agent/224-citation" sweep (`019e8923`). Reviewer quotations were never accepted as artifact evidence; authority-lane checks read the actual charter to narrow overreaching governance claims twice (`9c700093`, the apparatus CF2).
- **The Independent Assessment is the crown instrument, not the feared ritual.** In the eleven deliberate-arc fires it was never once empty; corpus-wide it found real, separately-actionable defects in a strong majority of fires — including four blocker-class misses in one pass (`019f6166`), two shell races the review never named (`4657f05b`), a plan self-contradiction the pasted review missed entirely on day two of the skill's life (`019e7f68`), and a validator hole folded into the same patch as the review's own findings. The honest limit, stated: on complex era-E targets the corpus cannot fully distinguish "anti-rubber-stamp working" from "a model primed to always find one more thing" — but the found things were repeatedly *proven by probe and folded into shipped patches*, which is the distinction that matters.
- **Dispositions do differential work.** The record shows JP filtering by them — "Patch only the narrow issues" excluding the `reject`ed and `defer`red claims (`019f1016`); a fold instruction explicitly dropping a challenged change ("drop 6's unsupported-context rewording — R7 challenged", `c0fceac9`); a fold declining a review's rewrite because "the adjudication found it misread the note's Codex scope" (`9f33a24b`). Challenged verdicts blocked wrong patches at least five separate times. This is the vocabulary earning its keep at the exact seam it was built for.
- **The floor held in every properly-invoked fire.** Zero mutations inside any completed adjudication turn across the deep-read sample; probes confined to scratchpads; the cleanest observed chain ran adjudicate → patch → "Commit" → "Push" as four separately-authorized steps (`019ea8e0`). The two breaches, §6, are precisely *not* properly-invoked fires.
- **Independence is real enough to cut against its own author.** "New method evidence: `/review-reviewer` re-derives independently even against self-authored text" — the git-cycle arc banked it after an adjudication caught a defect its own prior pass had introduced, plus two more self-introduced gaps (handoff 2026-06-17, verified); the deliberate arc repeated it (C2: "a regression introduced by me between v2 and v3... bias runs against confirming, and it confirms").

Against ~150 fires the observed error record is small and located: the two floor breaches (§6), one casualty (§5), a fire-to-fire diligence gap (the same ADR-FORMAT authority found in one cross-model fire, "not found" by a shallower search two days later), and the calibration incoherence of §4. The bar-OFF differential arm's charge that the packets are "ceremony weight" (bar-differential results, row 11) is answered by the fires: every section is load-bearing somewhere that matters, with one exception — the Review Judgment label, which is where the philosophy bites.

## 4. The Review Judgment: a verdict about a search, one level up

The skill's three verdict strata claim three different kinds of knowledge. Per-claim truth verdicts are existence claims about the target, and §3 shows them earned. Dispositions are recommendations, consumed as such. The **Review Judgment** (SKILL.md:159-169) is different in kind: `reliable` means "mostly correct, well-calibrated, and materially complete for the inspected target and evidence lanes" — a calibration-and-completeness grade *on the reviewer*, whose completeness half is an absence claim. Era 77 established the law for the family: a clearance is a fact about the search that produced it. This skill's text half-knows it — the scoping clause "for the inspected target and evidence lanes" is already there, and the Missed Issues hunt is explicitly bounded to "concrete adjacent surfaces" (SKILL.md:37) — and the fires show the label's grammar still outrunning it, three ways.

**The boundary between the top two labels is undefined, and observed practice contradicts itself.** In one video-editing session, rounds one and two drew `partially reliable` *because* the Independent Assessment caught a real miss the review lacked; round three, same session, same day, same shape — independent finding, review missed it — drew `Reliable.` (`019f10ef`). Across the eleven deliberate-arc fires the label never left `reliable` while the rationales logged "mildly overstated," "one sub-part weaker than stated," "calibration slips" — real-if-minor defects absorbed into the top grade; meanwhile a markdown-reader fire (`019f10ef`'s own later rounds, verified) keyed `partially reliable` to exactly one missed adjacent test. Nothing in the text says what moves the needle. The one label that fired below the top two — `Underpowered`, once, on a PR review that was "technically strong... on nearly every claim it makes" but missed a user-visible recovery defect the adjudicator found (`019f4895`) — shows the vocabulary *can* register the distinction that matters; it just has no rule for when it must.

**A `reliable` label expires, and nothing says so.** The record proves it twice, cleanly. A 07-11 pass graded a review of the skill-use design `reliable` and accepted an either/or remedy as compliant; the next pass, one revision later and cross-runtime, found the retained option violated the charter's One-Owner rule and required outright removal (`6bf2ecb8` → `019f5329`) — the adequacy the first `reliable` blessed was reversed. A 07-12 pass confirmed 10/10 claims by live execution, graded the review `reliable` — and the session's own post-patch fresh-context subagent re-scrutiny found a new High (sealed-id output-integrity) that neither the review nor the adjudication's Missed Issues hunt had surfaced (`669733da`). Neither event indicts the passes — both were true reports of exhausted bounded searches. They indict the grammar: `reliable` reads as a durable property of the review, and it is a timestamped property of one adjudication's search over one snapshot.

**The packet does not own its own fallibility.** Every brief in this genre closes by naming itself a single-judge argued pass; this skill's output contract has no such sentence. The ecosystem supplies the verification layer informally — counter-adjudication happened once, spectacularly (a cross-model agent fed this skill's packet through its own review-reviewer-shaped pass; the challenged R1.b was partially conceded on phrasing and evidentially overturned on substance, `6ad0e4ad`), and next-round reversals function as the error-correction channel — but the text never says its adjudication is itself unverified findings until something independent tests them. For a skill whose entire premise is that reviews must not be taken as authority, the silence about its own authority is the one genuinely un-mirrored surface.

This finding adjoins Era 69's finder-and-judge and Era 77's exhaustion-report laws, and per the sealed commission it was re-earned here rather than imported: the evidence is this skill's own label incoherence, its own reversal chain, and its own missing self-clause — and the difference from both precedents is stated: this target's text already carries nominal scoping and a bounded-hunt clause; the defect is that the label's decision rule and expiry are unwritten, not that the scope is unclaimed.

## 5. The echo-loop: the packet authors its own authorization

The most consistent aftermath pattern in the corpus, present from the second fire to the last: **the user's authorizing turn is the packet's own Recommended Next Step, pasted back verbatim or near-verbatim.** Readers logged it independently in batch after batch — "the user typed almost the packet's own Recommended-Next-Step text verbatim as authorization" (05-31); "user pastes the packet's own Act On Now bullets back as the next instruction" (06-16); "the human checkpoint is closer to a rubber stamp on the packet's self-recommendation than a deliberated go/no-go" (07-08); three of four sessions in four separate era-E batches. The two-word variants — "Patch now", "apply the patch", "Patch the plan" — are the same pattern compressed.

Read fairly, this is the cognitive-offload working exactly as designed: JP summons a careful adjudication precisely so he does not have to re-derive it, and the record shows the checkpoint is real when it matters — he filters by disposition, drops challenged changes, rules on surfaced open questions (one fire routed a contested verdict-partition decision to him via an explicit question rather than resolving it silently, `199822de`). But the design's own language — "Stop after the selected review packet by default" (SKILL.md:18), "Do not provide a detailed implementation plan unless the user explicitly asks" (SKILL.md:173) — imagines a deliberation gap between packet and action that the observed system has closed. The packet's terminal section *is* the next command, and the skill has never been told.

The record holds exactly one casualty, and it lives in that closed gap: on 06-04, two consecutive adjudications of the same plan — both `reliable`, both patched, both committed — let a plan line naming a nonexistent test function (`test_core_docs_document_target_ticket_shape()`) through to committed text, and the catching mechanism was JP's own re-read, not a third pass (`019e9350`, verified firsthand). One casualty in ~150 fires is a record most humans would envy; its shape is still instructive. When the adjudication is the last reader before commit, its Verification Gaps section is the only honest place to say what the executor must still check — and the fires that used it that way (the PR fire whose recommended merge was conditioned on a staleness re-check the aftermath actually executed, `019f44ac`; the token-budget `verify-first` later resolved by live measurement to 4,630-4,749 tokens) show the instrument already knows how. So does the one fire that recommended *against* action ("Do not patch based on this review now") — the honest null exists in the record; nothing in the text encourages it.

## 6. The boundary of the discipline

The skill's rigor is token-shaped, and the corpus maps the boundary precisely.

**Outside the token, the ambient rule owns the review — by design, and the seam is real.** A pasted review without the invocation gets no packet: "Per the standing pasted-content rule I'll act on it: reproduce each finding from committed bytes first, then execute the thirteenth revision" (`dd1af75a`, verified) — reproduce-then-patch, no claim IDs, no judgment, no artifact. This is the explicit-only contract working exactly as written (SKILL.md:13, :25), and it is also two adjacent adjudication regimes selected by the presence of eighteen characters. The evidence discipline demonstrably travels across the seam — the bare-paste path still reproduced findings from committed bytes — but the calibration vocabulary, the challenged-verdict channel, and the record do not.

**Under loop momentum, the seam failed twice in one day — the corpus's only true floor breaches.** In the video-editing convergence loops of 06-28/29 (thirteen token-fires against one evolving plan in two sessions), one round announced "The named `review-family:review-reviewer` skill is not exposed in this session, so I'm using the review text itself as input" and free-improvised straight to a patch **with a same-turn commit** — the skill body failed to load, and the fallback silently abandoned the contract rather than halting (`019f0f62`, verified). Five rounds later in the sibling session, with the skill available and the token present, the assistant simply declared "I'm going to treat the pasted review as a current claim check first, then patch the plan for any claims that hold" and merged adjudication into mutation, again committing same-turn (`019f10ef`, verified). Neither shape is named by the four-mode failure taxonomy (SKILL.md:69-75), which enumerates input failures — no review, no target, no access, anchoring — and has no word for *the contract itself dissolving under session momentum*. The first shape is structurally unrepairable from inside the text (a body that failed to load cannot instruct its own absence); the second is exactly the kind of observed failure a sentence can name. It is also contained: both breaches are era C, one venue, one day; the era-E record — through longer loops on harder targets — is clean, which suggests the 06-28 pattern was a local practice that matured rather than a standing hazard. Say it honestly either way.

**And the stance has escaped the skill entirely.** Codex's persistent memory now carries, and auto-injects at session start, lines distilled from watching these fires: "when the user invoked `$review-family:review-reviewer`, they wanted the supplied review treated as allegations to test against live files, not accepted at face value" — verified live in `MEMORY.md` today, observed preloading into five sessions and re-read in fifteen more. This is the strongest organic uptake evidence any skill in this library has produced: the method's one-sentence soul, extracted by the runtime itself, governing pasted-review behavior with no token present. It is also the method running unversioned — the memory carries the stance without the calibration honesty, the challenged-channel, or any of the §4 scoping this critique asks for, and no edit to the skill reaches it. An observation for the record, not a repair surface.

## 7. Anchoring: the honest theory, outgrown in one direction

The anchoring architecture is the text's most philosophically self-aware region, and the fires honor it with unusual fidelity. Every era-E fire names the structural fact the founding morning discovered — the review *is* the invocation payload, so no pristine blind read exists — and mitigates by target-first re-derivation, exactly as SKILL.md:30 instructs; the one true sequence breach in the corpus (a session whose handoff-load had already summarized the review before the token fired) was self-disclosed under the text's own `anchoring breach` vocabulary with confidence lowered exactly where line 74 says to lower it (`26d80db0`). At the far end, the 07-14 Codex fire built the blindness the text says is impossible: three subagents read the target with no review text, one self-reported exposure to a historical summary and was excluded as independent support — the anti-anchoring intent implemented more literally than its author imagined, and it is the fire that overturned a "minor revision" into four blocker-class missed issues.

What the text cannot express is the anchoring case the fires actually live in: **the adjudicator authored the target** — or authored the artifact the review attacks, in the same session, minutes earlier. This is not an edge; it is the dominant era-E configuration (the entire deliberate arc is an agent adjudicating reviews of its own design), and the fires convergently improvised the same disclosure the text lacks: "I authored Target A and read the review before this adjudication... verdicts below rest on the code, not the review's assertions" (`427a9d45`); "the conflict runs deeper than anchoring — the adjudicator is the artifact's author... treat the absence of larger missed issues with appropriate skepticism" (`6bf2ecb8`); "I sit as both author and adjudicator... the review's sharpest claims contradict my own draft — the conflict of interest runs *against* confirming them, and they confirm anyway" (`e7348be6`). Three sessions, three runtimes-and-days, one invented vocabulary. The text's `anchoring breach` is about reading order; the lived hazard is authorship, and its observed mitigation — disclose, then let probes and external authority carry the verdicts — deserves the same one-sentence residence in the failure-mode list that reading-order anchoring already has. The self-skepticism note on Missed Issues (`6bf2ecb8`'s "appropriate skepticism") is the honest kernel: authorship biases the *absence* claims hardest.

## 8. The temporal machinery, alive in translation

The commission pre-registered the dual-snapshot machinery as the family's most sophisticated dead vocabulary. Partially wrong, in an instructive way. As written — PR review snapshots, the minimum recovery checks of SKILL.md:33 — it fired in exactly one venue: the 06-04 PR #25 loop ran the recovery checks three times, found no recoverable review object each time, and correctly limited itself to current-head disposition ("no recoverable review ID, timestamp, or review-time head SHA... I adjudicated current-state dispositions against current PR head, not a separately proven historical review snapshot"); a 07-09 PR fire verified review-cited head == live head before judging. But the *instinct* the machinery encodes — truth verdicts belong to the snapshot the review saw — generalized far beyond PRs and now runs in essentially every era-E fire as a one-line check the text never mandates for non-PR targets: "review snapshot and current snapshot coincide, so truth and disposition share one boundary," verified live against `git rev-parse` before any verdict, every round. The staleness catches are real: a plan whose cited line numbers had shifted since the review was drafted, re-derived from the live file (`019f0c42`); a plan's own stale baseline-hash caught as a Valid claim with `act` (`019f10ef` round 6); one fire even checked that upstream `main` hadn't moved so the review's line anchors stayed valid. The four-plus restatements of the snapshot-unavailable rule (SKILL.md:33, :45, :87, :103, :128 — the R11 de-dup analysis skipped consolidating them, a settled call this critique does not reopen) protect a distinction the fires demonstrably use. The honest note is small: the generalized practice — the coincidence check on commit-pinned artifacts — is the machinery's real life, and one sentence could bless it as the non-PR default instead of leaving it as convergent folklore.

## 9. Trust-surface drift, priced honestly

This is a trust skill by its own family's classification — its declared value is a predictable packet — so shape defects are its native failure class, and the corpus holds a handful, none decision-changing:

- **The judgment label has no canonical rendering.** `Reliable.` / `reliable` / `` `reliable` `` / `Partially reliable.` / `partially reliable` — five typographies across four days in one repo, logged independently by four readers.
