## 3. The pick is nobody's discipline

Start with the commission's deepest question: does explore-then-settle survive the partition law — "a skill that both explores and adjudicates is two skills"? The honest adjudication is that the lane survives the letter, and the letter is exactly where the trouble hides. The lane renders no verdict of its own; the user approves. That makes it legitimately neither an exploratory skill nor a decision-owning one: it is a pipeline whose settling authority is externalized to the approval seat. But walk the actual sequence the text mandates. Step 4 (SKILL.md:24): "Propose 2-3 genuinely different approaches with trade-offs. Lead with a recommendation and why." The pick among the approaches — a genuine adjudication, the exact act the deciding lane exists for — is made by the author of the approaches, announced before the user has seen the field un-anchored, and governed by six words. Now look at what the library built for that same act one directory over. `making-recommendations` after its rebuild: register your lean and the user's visible lean before comparing; the comparison's job is to attack the leans; the case against before any close with a pick; exchange rates posed priced, never settled silently; sycophancy named as the lane's strongest failure. And look at its carve: "two or more serious options *already on the table*" (making-recommendations SKILL.md:3), with the reverse route — "approaches need shaping before serious options can exist" (:91) — pointing here. The carve is loader-clean and settled (`b5e1de5`, undisturbed), but its consequence has never been stated: the entire honest-advisor apparatus applies only to options the user brought. Options the agent authored moments earlier — the majority of serious choices in this ecosystem, on this record — are picked under no discipline at all. The fires show what that costs and what masks the cost. The recommendation led in all fourteen fires and lost in none: no user ever picked a rival approach. What users did correct, richly in the workshop, is everything *downstream* of the pick — sub-decisions, names, scope, contract details; in one workshop fire four sub-decisions moved at a single checkpoint, one against the assistant's stated lean. The pick itself was ratified every time, usually in the first reply, usually in the same breath as first contact with the field ("A matches what I'm picturing"). `ideate`'s characterization of this lane — "convergent gravity: it leads with a recommendation and develops one toward an approved design" (ideate SKILL.md:17) — is confirmed by every transcript, and the neighbor built counter-bias around its own gravity while this lane never did. A pick that never loses is either uncannily good aim or an anchor working as anchors work, and nothing in the method can tell which — the arc's oldest epistemics problem, earned fresh here on a fourteen-for-fourteen record.

## 4. A field that narrows when no one is watching — and complies

The commission asked whether the 2-3 approaches are a real field or a horse race staged to be won, and instructed crediting :24's restraint — "Do not invent weak alternatives to fill the count" — as the honest sentence it is. Both halves of the answer are in the record, split cleanly by regime. Every workshop fire fielded rivals developed to pickable depth: approaches with their own mechanics, named risks, honest costs, live enough that users pulled elements out of the losers ("the inclusion of AGENTS.md in C feels valuable"), preserved in one artifact as "deferred, not dead." No field fire did. Field rivals, where they existed at all, were real in kind and unpickable in fact — one to two sentences against forty developed lines for the recommendation, or dismissed in a clause, or pre-excluded by constraints the user had already locked (an Electron option offered to a user who had already chosen native-Mac upstream — a dead foil filling the count in exactly the way :24 forbids), or absent entirely: two field fires proposed one design with no rival beside it. Here is the correction this treatment owes the commission rather than a confirmation: **the compressed field shape complies with step 4 as written.** "Propose 2-3 genuinely different approaches with trade-offs, lead with a recommendation" is satisfied by three named architectures, two of them sketched in a sentence — one fire's reader called that "the expected shape for this skill's step 4," and read against the letter, it is. The instrument's floor admits the staged race; the workshop exceeded the floor because its counterparty demanded more, not because the text did. And the text hands the agent no honest exit in either direction: no way to say "only one serious approach exists here" and present it as exactly that (the deciding lane has `only one serious option`; this lane, which authors its fields, has no equivalent), and no route to widen when the field is genuinely thin or the user rejects it — `ideate` hands off to this lane by name (ideate SKILL.md:45) and this lane never routes back, a one-way door of precisely the shape the arc's first treatment found, re-earned here from a mesh that did not exist then: ideate was born 2026-06-26; this skill's only touch since is a rename.

## 5. What approval proves, and the edge it never names

"The design is approved only when the user says so" (SKILL.md:35) is a trust floor, and its primary function — stopping the agent from declaring its own design approved — held in every fire. What the floor does not govern is what the record actually contains: users who never say so. In three field fires the approval moment arrived as an action request — "draft it as a repo doc," "Save that design to docs/specs," "Scaffold this repo for this build" — and the agent treated the instruction as the approval. Twice that was harmless (the action was writing the design artifact itself, which the Artifacts section permits on ask). Once it was the record's only floor breach, re-verified firsthand for this critique: the design turn ended with the contract's own words — "I would treat this as the approved design only after you say so. The next lane after approval is `implementation-planning`" — the user answered "Scaffold this repo for this build," and the very next turn began writing production Swift code; no approval acknowledged, no next-lane question asked, the lane that does not implement implementing. The breach is not ignorance — the rule was recited two messages earlier. It is an unnamed edge: the text says what approval is and says nothing about what to do when the user skips it and orders the next action, so the politest available reading (instruction implies approval) wins, and it wins hardest exactly where it should win least. Around the approval sit the check-ins, and the record is blunt: "check in after sections the user might correct" (SKILL.md:25) essentially never happens as written. Thirteen of fourteen fires delivered the design in one monolithic message with a single approval ask at the end. What does work, in the fires that have it, is decision-grain: one workshop fire's explicit "Check-in — four decisions, two that actually shape it" changed the control default against the assistant's own lean; another's choice-menus resolved scope before development. And one field fire prices the dead instrument precisely: the user's one correction — a scope requirement the whole design had missed — arrived bundled *with the approval*, after the full design was written, where a single check-in on scope assumptions would have caught it before. Finally, the pattern that reframes the whole section: every strong approval in the record was preceded by pressure the user supplied — two stress-test rounds folded in before sign-off; approval withheld and the design routed to an adversarial panel that returned eleven confirmed findings; three rounds of pasted external review, each honestly adjudicated; line-cited critiques revising a patch contract twice. The lane's own text neither invites that pressure before settling nor records whether it happened. Assent to a fluent artifact and assent that survived a panel carry the same word — "approved" — into the same downstream intake.

## 6. The capsule and the contest it forgets

The warm-handoff capsule (SKILL.md:37) has a history worth stating precisely, because it flips the usual direction of this critique: the workshop fires were emitting capsule-substance *before the clause existed* — a compact "Locked: revised lean Approach A, reference dropped… handing to implementation-planning with the sequencing constraint," with the adversarial panel's finding numbers carried as inline provenance, ran twelve days before `4728fdc` wrote the behavior down. The clause codified the workshop's practice. What the record then shows is the codification not traveling: in the six fires that ran capsule-era text, all three workshop fires emitted it (once in exemplary double form — a chat capsule at approval, then a fuller one in the landed spec, with the rejected approaches, the reasons, and a disclosed exploration failure recorded), and none of the three field fires did — a plain contents summary, a one-line "I'll implement it now," a terse next-step sentence, with the capsule beats left scattered in the proposal for anyone downstream to re-derive. The mitigations are real and should be said: this capsule is chat-first, nothing is committed as law, `implementation-planning` reads the full design rather than the capsule (SKILL.md:37's own boundary, and the Era-54–58 two-depths resolution stands undisturbed), and in two of the field misses the same agent carried the work forward itself, so the omission had no visible cost there. But the beat the capsule lacks is the one this record proves matters: nothing in "approved approach / deferred / constraints / next lane" records *how settled the design actually is* — which decisions were contested and corrected during the cycle and which were accepted as offered. The downstream intake (`implementation-planning` SKILL.md:12) requires "settled source material: an approved design," and §5 showed "approved" spanning everything from a survived panel to a scaffold request. The sibling lane already marks this seam — `outcome-shaping`'s capsule separates "what the user confirmed in their own words versus what is still your compression" — and the one fire in this record that recorded its own contest produced the only artifact a future session could weigh rather than merely obey. One sentence adds the beat; no format changes.

## 7. One reality instrument alive, one dead

The commission asked whether the lane's strongest instrument — explore first, read the load-bearing files yourself, never design from subagent summaries alone (SKILL.md:21, the feature-dev fold) — changes designs or decorates them. The answer is the cleanest positive result in the arc's fire evidence: fourteen for fourteen, grounding was real, and in at least ten fires a specific design decision is traceable to a specific thing read. A repo search that found no precedent for embedded orchestration scripts became the stated reason an approach was rejected. A first-hand read of an export tool's corruption behavior became a load-bearing boundary rule. Fetched external protocol documentation reversed an interval-semantics decision mid-session. Raw extraction dumps against a real fixture produced the architecture. And the anti-relay rule itself was exercised twice for real and honored both times — once reading every file a recon subagent named, once catching that two of four parallel subagents had returned literal placeholder junk, refusing to trust them, re-reading the key file by hand, and disclosing the failure in the shipped artifact under "Provenance and honest margin." The fold earned its place; nothing in this critique or its repairs touches it. Its mirror image is the prototype exit (SKILL.md:16): fired zero times in fourteen fires, against at least five occasions that matched its own trigger — including a session that *said aloud, repeatedly*, that key decisions could only be resolved by testing against the user's real files, then settled them by assertion; the resulting gap was flagged one phase later by an external panel as the design's most important unresolved issue. An exit that has never once fired, across the method's entire recorded life, in the presence of its own trigger condition spoken in so many words, is a dead letter as worded. The wording is the fixable part: the trigger lives in the agent's own mouth — the sentence "only running code / real data / the actual files can answer this" *is* the offer moment — and the repair binds the offer to the tell. The scope check (SKILL.md:22) sits between these poles: it fired and decomposed real work in five fires, was legitimately pre-satisfied upstream in two, and was skipped where probes bundled separable subsystems — alive, imperfect, not repaired.

## 8. The mesh that works, and the pressure that is always borrowed

The trigger boundaries (SKILL.md:12-17) are the part of this skill the loader-era work already fixed, and the fires vindicate them: six clean permissioned entries from the shaping lane, `making-recommendations` correctly used beside the lane for pre-existing-option choices and correctly deferred when a heavy comparison deserved its own session, no observed misroute in fourteen fires. Two boundary observations stand. First, the stress-test route-out (:15) has never once been taken as named: on the three occasions a user wanted adversarial pressure on a draft design, it arrived as an inline stress-test, a bespoke multi-agent panel, and an ultracode workflow — never `grill-me`. This is ecology, not defect — the users named their own mechanisms, and the grilling lane's own treatment (Era 71) already recorded its sibling repairs — but it sharpens §5's finding: the lane's best outcomes depended on pressure arriving, and the lane has no move that invites it, so whether it arrives is entirely a property of who is sitting in the approval seat. Second, the exits enumerate outcome-shaping, making-recommendations, grill-me, and prototype — and nothing for width. A lane whose step 4 authors a field has no move for the field being too thin, or rejected, or the ask deserving more than its "natural two or three" (ideate SKILL.md:28); the widening lane routes in and nothing routes back. On this record no fire visibly died for want of that exit — the honest statement is that the door's absence is structural, observed as a one-way mesh edge, not as a casualty.

## 9. Where is the design judgment provoked?

Read per part, as the library's own bar instructs. The trust surfaces are genuinely strong: the no-implementation floor (held thirteen of fourteen, and the breach walked through a nameable edge rather than a soft floor), the approval gate, the anti-relay rule, the artifacts restraint (chat-first, ask-one-path-question, no auto-commit — this lane never grew the write-back problem its grilling cousin did), the boundaries. The judgment side is thinner than it looks. The lane's entire design-quality theory is one sentence of doctrine — "design for isolation: units with one clear purpose and well-defined interfaces… Remove features the outcome does not need" (SKILL.md:26) — plus a self-review that checks form, not thought: placeholders, contradictions, ambiguous requirements, scope fit (SKILL.md:27). Real forcing functions exist, and the fires show them working where they run: the 2-3-field requirement (a forced comparison — the lane's one provoking structure aimed at the design space itself), the scope check, the grounding rule. But §4 showed the field requirement's floor admits a staged race, and the develop phase — the lane's owned generative act, where design quality actually gets made — is provoked by nothing at all: no failure question, no interrogation of the chosen approach, nothing between the isolation sentence and the form-level self-review. In the workshop that gap is invisible because the environment supplies the missing pressure; the two fires where design quality was tested hardest (an eleven-finding panel, a three-round pasted review) both found real defects the develop phase had produced and the self-review had not caught. The honest per-part verdict: this lane's differential value, on its record, is its gates, its grounding, and its cognitive-offload — summon one token, get the full grounded cycle with the floor intact — and that is legitimate merit under the library's own doctrine. But the judgment parts ride closer to the bare agent than the skill's self-image suggests, and the one universal floor the commission flagged — "'Simple' requests still get a design" (SKILL.md:17) — survives adjudication *because* of that: it is not crude-rule overreach, it is the lane's best trust rule, proportionally priced ("a few sentences"), aimed at the documented failure of models self-exempting on "simple," and no fire shows it misfiring. Keep it verbatim.

## 10. What survives, and the honest reframe

Nearly everything survives, much of it with the strongest fire evidence in the arc. The grounding step and the anti-relay fold: fourteen-for-fourteen, design-changing, once heroic — untouched. The scope check: alive and decomposing — untouched. The no-implementation floor and "simple requests still get a design": the lane's identity, held at the floor's named edge everywhere except the edge it never named — kept, with that edge named. The boundaries and the shaping-lane mesh: observed working cleanly across both runtimes — untouched, description unchanged. The artifacts restraint: untouched. The capsule's two-depth resolution and light shape: kept, one beat added. The isolation doctrine and self-review: kept as the context they are. The name: kept — "design exploration" describes what the workshop fires actually do, the philosophy does not demand a rename, and fourteen live routing surfaces price cosmetics honestly. The mining dispositions, the loader boundaries, and the deciding-lane carve: undisturbed.

The honest reframe is not an inversion, and unlike the arc's costume-strippings there is no liturgy here to strip — the text is spare, its restraints are real, and its premise (explore approaches, develop one, get explicit approval, hand off warm) is the right shape for the job it owns. The defect is the fifth hold's own shape: **a contract whose discipline is real but installed in its environment — and whose floor, read honestly, permits the very compressions its instruments exist to prevent.** In the workshop, where the counterparty contests, the lane is everything it says it is: the fires show genuine fields, corrected picks-in-development, withheld approvals, recorded contests. In the field, the same text runs a led recommendation to a plain ratification with no rival anyone could pick, no check-in before the ask, no capsule after it, and no record that any of this was different from the workshop's forged designs — and every one of those compressions is compliant. The lane cannot tell its two regimes apart, and unlike its grilling cousin it does not even hold the pen mid-session on durable files — its authority problem is smaller, one word wide: "approved," carried into a downstream intake that reads it as "settled." The repairs therefore make the text demand what the workshop already does, at the five points the record shows the field dropping it: an honest count with a widening exit instead of a mandatory 2-3 (present the real field, name the only-one-serious-approach case plainly, route to `ideate` when width is what's missing); a pick that carries the deciding lane's minimum honesty inline — a labeled lean, the strongest rival's case in a sentence, the priced trade posed when the choice turns on the user's values — without re-routing the lane's own picks out; check-ins at the decision grain, where the record shows they change outcomes; an approval floor that survives the action-request edge (an instruction to act is the approval moment arriving early: confirm, name the next lane, ask — and on hard-to-reverse designs, offer one round of pressure first, which is what every strong approval in this record had anyway); and a capsule that records how contested the design actually was, so "approved" stops flattening a survived panel and a scaffold request into the same word. No new sections, no schemas, no counterparty classifier: the same lane, made able to say what it did.

The heart of it: this skill believes collaborative exploration plus explicit approval produces a settled design. On the evidence, that is true precisely when the collaborator does half the work — contests the field, corrects the development, withholds the approval until pressure has run. The text mandates the anchor and outsources the honesty: it tells its author to lead with a pick, never to argue against it; to propose a field, never to admit the field is one; to obtain approval, never to notice what kind. The lane's instruments are real, its floor held when named, its grounding is the best-evidenced sentence in the library's design mesh. What was missing is the discipline the library already built for everyone else who renders a pick — owed doubly here, where the same hand deals every card — and the one sentence downstream that says how hard the approval was actually earned.
