# Simplify-Code, Second Treatment: The Armor Worn

> Saved 2026-07-18. JP commissioned the eighteenth methodology-and-philosophy treatment — the arc's first re-treatment of a target — on `skills/simplify-code`, twelve days after the twelfth hold declared "the armor has never been worn," and endorsed the critique in full on 2026-07-18 (chat adjudication, session 4803e8ae). It became the design brief for the repairs. Verdict: sixteenth hold — three small honesty-and-reliability repairs, not rebuild. Target pinned at `.agents` commit `8a32ee0`; line citations resolve against that version (`git show 8a32ee0:skills/simplify-code/SKILL.md`); the simplify-code bundle is byte-identical between that pin and the branch base. The three-point repair set landed at commit `e2ac272` (skills/ dual-runtime local source, no publish train); the repairs invalidate specific SKILL.md and playbook step-8 line numbers but not the readings.

**TLDR:** The sixteenth hold, with a shape none of the fifteen prior holds wore: **the record answered its own predecessor, and the treatment is inside the answer.** On 2026-07-06 at 13:52 the twelfth-hold repairs landed on a skill with zero fires in 39 days of life; at 21:05 the same evening JP typed its token for the first time ever, and by the end of the delta window the corpus holds **roughly forty genuine organic cycles** — a nine-cycle campaign in cross-model that same night, a twenty-six-invocation test-monolith campaign in markdown-reader two days later, two more single fires that evening, one boundary-honoring partial in `.agents`, plus two full-fidelity model-chosen fires inside a sealed trial rig — every organic fire Codex-side, user-summoned, and the transcripts never once mention the treatment whose landing they trail by hours. The observed method is, to a degree no precedent target has matched, **the contract running as written**: the scanner ran in every edit-reaching cycle and its verdict was load-bearing for lane calls, not decorative; 37 full-safety backup artifacts were created with zero exclusions and independently re-verified hashes; the five-label closeout was emitted in every completed cycle with Commit Readiness accurate against the real index all forty times; verification was behavioral every single time — pre-edit baselines, focused-then-full test ladders, AST moved-body comparisons, never a lint-only claim laundered as proof — and ceremony did not compress even under a six-hour, twenty-six-invocation barrage with approvals arriving at six-minute cadence. The boundary discipline fired too: a clean no-op verdict ("No code change was made") delivered under full closeout, two routing declines, and one partial fire where the skill's own Use fence caught a plan-shaped ask and rerouted it to `tech-debt-scan` mid-invocation. And the instrument the first treatment mourned — the Review Packet, "a letter to a reviewer who has never existed" — found its addressee within a day and became the method's actual product: packets consumed by implementation-review passes, thirteen-plus "No findings" verdicts gating slice after slice, three merged PRs, and the skill's observed identity crystallizing as **the executor stage of a composed production line** (tech-debt-scan finds → making-recommendations picks → simplify-code executes → implementation-review checks → git-cycle lands) in which its thin judgment stratum never chose a slice and its trust stratum is exactly what the pipeline consumes. The fires found the method's first real defect precisely where fifteen text reviews could never look — the seam between the closeout and its consumer: a self-authored contradiction (leave scoped changes unstaged by default, then hand the reviewer a packet asking them to block on unstaged files) that JP caught in the fire ("Why do you keep leaving stuff unstaged, and then asking the review to block if stuff is unstaged?"), ordered fixed ("Fix that rule in the simplify-code skill"), and saw repaired *from inside the firing session* — both scar commits of the delta window were authored mid-campaign by the very sessions firing the skill, with the remaining cycles observably complying the same hour, the fastest text-repair-to-uptake loop in the treatment arc; the one blemish is that the staging repair's own proof was a string-presence grep narrated as "a small dry-run check against the exact failure mode" — the skill's static-versus-behavioral law bent by the session repairing it, redeemed only by compliance evidence the commit could not yet cite. What the fires indict is small and specific. First, **the strength-label vocabulary — the skill's founding epistemological repair — runs on the scripts' rails, not the agent's discipline**: the planned/observed labels appear reliably exactly where the backup helper's `--planned-verification` flag structurally demands text, and vanished in all three fast-lane fires, including one where the playbook's label mandate sat verbatim in context; one manifest carries a bare command string because the helper validates nothing. Substance held everywhere; the words are fair-weather. Second, **the backup's promise ends at creation**: 37 artifacts sit on disk past their own stated retention horizons with no cleanup mechanism, no restore ever performed, free-text retention in five wordings, and an aftermath the text does not own. Third, honestly named: **the armor has been worn and never struck** — every guard rail that justifies the machinery's weight (scanner positive/uncertain, exclusions, `--allow-exclusions`, weak-plan approval, the stop-label, protected-surface opt-in, restore) remains unexercised, because all forty fires ran down the happy-path corridor of clean test/refactor targets; the choreography is now proven, the damage-prevention still isn't, and no casualty exists anywhere — no revert, no fix commit attributing a break to any campaign slice. The repairs are three small honesty-and-reliability edits — carry the strength labels into the fast-lane closeout, make the helper validate the label it records, own the artifact aftermath in one sentence — plus nothing else: the lanes, scanner, backup contract, closeout, packet mandate, description, routing carve, and name all stay, now with live-fire evidence *for* them for the first time. And one finding belongs to the genre, not the target: **the treatment is not a neutral instrument.** A skill with six weeks of silence fired nine times within eight hours of its brief landing, and the arc must own that observing a method can be what summons it.

## 3. What ran and paid

## 4. The consumption loop, and the defect only it could find

The first treatment's saddest sentence — "the Review Packet is a letter to a reviewer who has never existed" — died within a day. The observed aftermath of nearly every cycle is the packet being *checked*: pasted "Implementation Review" passes verifying the packet's claims against the live repo, thirteen-plus bare "No findings" verdicts gating whether the next slice proceeds, one formal blocker, and three merged PRs as terminal artifacts. The packet is the coupling of a production line, and the skill's observed identity is **the executor stage of a composition** — tech-debt-scan supplies the backlog, making-recommendations picks the next slice, simplify-code executes and emits the packet, implementation-review consumes it, git-cycle lands the result. Two consequences worth naming plainly. First, the skill's judgment stratum — the value order (playbook:70), clarity-over-brevity (playbook:100) — never chose a slice in any fire; selection arrived from upstream skills and JP. The judgment lives in the pipeline, not in this skill; what this skill contributes is disciplined execution and a consumable proof surface, and that is exactly what its founding design promised. Second, the method's first real defect was a **consumption-seam defect**: the closeout's leave-unstaged default and the packet's staging-verification request contradicted each other — visible only when a real reviewer received a real packet, caught by JP in the fire, repaired mid-campaign (§1), and closed in the live text at SKILL.md:38. Fifteen text reviews graded this contract; none could have found that, because the seam only exists when the packet has a reader.

One blemish inside the repair loop, said precisely: the staging repair's validation was a Python heredoc asserting five string-presence conditions over the edited prose, narrated in the commit's closeout as "a small dry-run check against the exact failure mode" (verified verbatim). That is a structural check wearing a behavioral name — the exact inflation the playbook's own step 7 forbids in edit verification. The repair was in fact vindicated behaviorally, but by the *subsequent cycles'* observed compliance, which the commit message could not yet cite. The fires hold the method's discipline better than the method's own repairs did.

## 5. The fair-weather vocabulary

The planned/observed strength labels are the skill's founding epistemological repair — born the day a review caught the evidence gate's circularity — and the fires show them running **on the scripts' rails rather than the agent's discipline**. Where `--planned-verification` structurally demands a string, the words appear: 36 of 37 manifests carry a proper strength label, and the full-safety closeouts echo them ("Planned verification strength: strong plan. Observed evidence label: strong behavior evidence…"). Where no script demands text, the words vanish: **all three fast-lane fires omitted both labels** — including campaign 2's first cycle, where the playbook's step-7 mandate sat verbatim in the context window (verified: every strength-vocabulary hit in that cycle is quoted skill text, none agent-authored) — and the one non-conforming manifest (`stalemate-blocking-press-policy`) records a bare command string because the helper accepts any free text (verified: `planned_verification: uv run pytest tests/test_stalemate_emit_gate.py -q; ./tools/check.py`). The substance held everywhere — the evidence behind every absent label was genuinely strong and behavioral — so this is not dishonesty; it is the honesty *instrument* proving fair-weather: present exactly where machinery forces it, absent exactly where only discipline would carry it. For a skill whose declared value includes labeled verification honesty, the labels should not depend on which lane happens to have a CLI flag.

## 6. The unowned aftermath

Every backup manifest promises a retention future — in five different free-text wordings, only 16 of 37 matching the script's own default — and nothing anywhere keeps the promise. No cleanup mechanism exists in the skill, the repos, or any schedule (verified by grep across the bundle); both campaigns merged 10+ days ago, so by their own retention language all 37 artifacts (5.3MB) are past due; all 37 remain. No restore was ever performed (affirmatively evidenced by the unbroken landed history, not just mtimes). One target repo made the `.backup/` ignore durable — by its own later audit, with a commit message crediting "simplify-code snapshots" — while the other relies solely on the helper's local `info/exclude` line. The backup is insurance that has never paid a claim, with silently recurring premiums. That is not an argument against the insurance — one restore would justify all 37 artifacts, and the artifact cost is trivial against the mutation risk it prices — but the text should own what the record shows: retention is a recorded expectation, not an enforced behavior, and cleanup is manual work belonging to the user after acceptance. One honest sentence closes the gap between what the manifest promises and what the world does.

## 7. What the fires still cannot say

The rig adds the one controlled datum the organic record lacks: same task, same fixture, the skill taken 2-of-3 times when present and the work done competently inline when not — consistent with the offload-and-discipline account of the skill's value (the shape and proof-surface of the work change; the outcome, on happy-path targets, visibly less so), at n too small to lean on. ## 8. The observer effect — a finding for the genre

Thirty-nine days of dual-runtime silence; a treatment lands at 13:52 declaring the silence total; the first fire in the skill's life at 21:05 the same evening, followed by two boundary probes and a use-case interview that spawned `work-router`'s design that night, then a 26-invocation campaign two days later. The transcripts are silent on the cause — the first-fire session contains zero substantive reference to the treatment (verified: the only "methodology-critique" strings in it are git-log branch-name echoes during the mid-campaign fencing merge) — so causation is unprovable from the record. But the arc should not launder the timing: the overwhelmingly plausible reading is that **the treatment resurfaced the skill in its user's attention, and the observation summoned the fires it then measured as absent.** For this target the effect was benign — the fires vindicated the method. The genre-level lesson is not benign: a methodology critique's fire census is not a passive read of a standing corpus; it can be the intervention that creates the next corpus, and future briefs (including the 08-01 ledger re-read) should treat post-treatment fire surges as partially endogenous before crediting or debiting any skill for them.

## Repairs

Scaled to what the record warrants — three small honesty-and-reliability edits, each classified per part under the `agent-facing-design` gate:

- **R1 — carry the strength labels into the fast lane.** SKILL.md:36's fast-lane compact review hook enumerates "absolute paths, behavior-preservation claim, command/result, and why fast-lane eligibility held" — add the planned-verification and observed-evidence strength labels to that enumeration (with the same lane-independent expectation stated where step 7 defines the labels, if wording needs an anchor). This is aligning an output enumeration with an existing obligation the fires show dropping exactly where no script forces it — trust part, no new machinery, single-sourced.
- **R2 — make the helper validate the label it records.** `create_simplify_backup.py` accepts any free text for `--planned-verification`; require the value to begin with `strong plan`, `moderate plan`, or `weak plan` (fail fast per house error format), with a focused test per the playbook's own Script Maintenance rule. Machinery, justified on the trust bar by an observed data-quality hole in a safety artifact's manifest; the lighter path (the playbook's existing `<strong|moderate|weak plan>` syntax hint) is the thing the field already ignored once.
- **R3 — own the artifact aftermath.** One or two sentences at playbook step 8: nothing enforces the recorded retention; the artifact persists until manually removed; cleanup after acceptance is the user's (or a directed session's) work, with `trash`. Context, not machinery; closes the promise-vs-world gap §6 documents.
- **Nothing else.** The lanes, scanner contract and conservative-gate authority, backup contract, evidence ladder, five-label closeout, fenced Review Packet mandate, staging rule (now fire-proven), description, routing carve, and name all stay — for the first time on live-fire evidence *for* them rather than absence of evidence against them. The cosmetic bold-header drift needs nothing. The unexercised guard rails are not repairable by text — only by a fire that strikes them.

Transfers, recorded here and owed to other owners, not this treatment's edits: **T1 (ledger/miner)** — the usage ledger over-counts fires (capsule-load on routing questions, re-injection on prose echoes) and under-counts them (untagged self-invoked cycles, multi-cycle chains: one tag → two cycles; one tag → six slices); the 08-01 re-read must not read rows as fires, in either direction. **T2 (the genre)** — the observer effect of §8 belongs in the methodology-critique lane's own self-understanding.
