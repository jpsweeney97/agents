**TLDR:** A hold. The July repairs took as sentences and did not change where a design gets corrected. In the record since 2026-07-03, the lean is labeled in nearly every fire, the strongest rival's case is spoken in about half, named decisions appear in almost every design message, a pressure round is offered in about a dozen cycles, approvals arrive in words in the large majority, and the no-implement floor held in all but one of about fifty cycles. That is uptake, and it is real. Value is a different column. The recommendation won about nine picks in ten; a rival won twice at approach grain, both times a rival developed past the "sentence or two" the text asks for. Named-decision check-ins, the instrument the text trusts ("a named decision invites correction", SKILL.md:26), moved about one decision in ten, almost always a binary lean-labeled choice on a personal project. Every material correction in the corpus, the kind that found a premise the lane had presented as settled to be false, came from a pressure round in each cycle where one ran, and from the first real run or a later review where none did. The text gates that pressure on the lane's own reversibility judgment (SKILL.md:36); the record shows that judgment spoken aloud seven times and wrong twice, and it shows pressure paying off on designs the lane had called cheap. So the honest statement of what an approval in this lane proves: the user assented to a design the lane presented, after correcting on average one named detail; whether the design's premises were true was tested by pressure when pressure ran and by reality otherwise. Three further facts the July record could not see: the approval seat accepts a relayed model's "Approved" as the user saying so in seven cycles, with one fire refusing it correctly; the capsule's settledness beat was written accurately three times and wrongly once, and no receiving lane was ever seen reading it; and the prototype tell fires in the field in a vocabulary the text does not name ("untested", "unverified", "cannot guarantee"), and is moved past more often than offered. The repairs the record warrants are honesty at three joints, again with no machinery: ungate the pressure offer from reversibility, make the settledness beat default to "accepted as offered" and name relayed approvals, and widen the prototype tell to the words the fires actually use. This is the nineteenth hold; the shape is July's shape re-measured, not a new one, and the tally is history, not evidence about this target.

## 3. Where a design actually gets corrected

This is the finding the axis did not name and the fires forced. The text's theory of correction is in one sentence: "a fluent finished artifact invites assent, a named decision invites correction" (SKILL.md:26). The record's theory is different, and it is the same on both runtimes and in every venue.

Named decisions invite assent. Where they were posed, they were accepted about nine times in ten (section 2), and the tenth was a small binary choice. Fluent finished artifacts also invite assent: every single-message design was approved on the next turn or after a "too loud" compression (`codex_2026-08-14_a8a111c9be U4-U5`; `codex_2026-08-13_40881248ec U15-U17`, where eight review corrections entered the design through the compression without an individual check-in). Neither instrument produced the corrections that mattered.

Pressure produced them. In every cycle where a pressure round ran before settling, the design changed, and in most the change was a premise the lane had presented as settled and grounded that turned out to be false. The list is long because the pattern is the finding: the pre-registered adjudication category space was "not exhaustive (two outcomes have no category)" (`claude_2026-07-13_4817f7346c U8`); a charter verdict presented as settled was "Partially valid" under review (`claude_2026-07-16_0c1bd65e92 A37`); "Nothing proceeds silently" became "The permission asks would silently never have fired" (`claude_2026-08-14_eadf28e58c A30, A57`); "there is no settings storage anywhere" became "That was wrong. I had searched the builder file, not the built page" (`claude_2026-08-26_2d2df42ca5 A14, A69`); the grill's own High finding rested on a wrong code read caught only in the planning lane (`claude_2026-08-26_51bf2f0a18 A134`); "The 'covered token' test is reversed" and "The design text contradicts the prototype that produced its own evidence" (`claude_2026-08-30_0e37c3c400 U8, A50`); a settled-frame case "never produces a truncated successful package" (`claude_2026-09-01_2c7111614f U9`); "fully reversible satellite-by-satellite via retire" was "false as designed" (`claude_2026-07-17_4ee810028d A31, A51`); the commission's "gitignored" premise was wrong and the worktree base-branch default was missed by the assistant's own docs search (`claude_2026-09-24_190065ea0b A20, A23`); "every script asserts its sha256" was incorrect (`claude_2026-09-24_efc08140cb A53`); the headline invariant "ordinary scrolling cannot invalidate or diff the 3,863-row collection" was false through an existing publication (`codex_2026-07-10_ee5ddb6dde A27, U8`); a two-option field had omitted the option that won (`codex_2026-09-07_21d52530cc A50`); eight required corrections, none fatal (`codex_2026-08-13_40881248ec A21`); "exchange failures cannot always leave run.json untouched" (`codex_2026-07-08_3668b2c3a4 A19`). Grounding was real in every one of these cycles and it was the grounding's own claims that were wrong. The self-review step (SKILL.md:28) left a visible trace in two cycles and caught none of these.

Where no pressure ran, the same class of defect arrived later and cost more. Now the gate. The text offers pressure "On a hard-to-reverse design" (SKILL.md:36). The lane judged reversibility aloud seven times. Twice the judgment was wrong in the direction that skips pressure: "the reversibility is real, so the bet is cheap ... The gate is a prediction the lane cannot make reliably, placed in front of the one instrument the record shows correcting designs. That is a method finding, not a contract finding: the text's stated theory of where correction comes from is the thing the fires contradict.
