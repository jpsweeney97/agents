# Agent-Facing Design: A Critique of the Methodology and Philosophy

> Saved 2026-07-02 at JP's request, to be re-readable. This is the critique delivered in-session when JP asked for pressure on the philosophy of `skills/agent-facing-design/`, following the example of the outcome-interviewer methodology critique. The target is `skills/agent-facing-design/` (SKILL.md, references/calibration.md, agents/openai.yaml) as of commit `5dc9082`; inline line references resolve against that version (`git show 5dc9082:skills/agent-facing-design/SKILL.md`). Evidence boundary: this is a text/design-level critique — its claims about consequences are predictions from structure, not observed incidents; growth figures were computed from git history during the session; ledger and audit figures are quoted from the framework-challenge and philosophy-audit records, not independently re-mined. The body below is reproduced from the session verbatim.

I read the skill and its calibration reference, the metadata, the full commit history back to the founding version (`4e52679`), and the three documents that form its recent biography — the 328-agent philosophy audit, the disposition that adjudicated it, and the framework challenge. What follows is pressure on the philosophy itself — the founding dichotomy, the damage epistemology, the theory of value — not on frontmatter, routing, or delivery hygiene. Where the adjudicated audit already covered ground (M-items, blast-radius scaling), I take its adjudication as read and operate above it, not through it. One honesty note before starting: this is a single-pass, text-level critique, the same evidentiary class as the audit it post-dates — its claims about consequences are predictions from structure, not observed incidents.

**TLDR:** The skill's philosophy stands on a founding dichotomy — context versus machinery — that is not a property of the text it classifies: to a compliant model, all loaded text is machinery of varying stiffness, and the distinction that actually does the skill's work (deterministic mechanics versus semantic decisions) sits demoted in a subordinate clause while the rhetorical binary holds the title. Its justification theory hears only catastrophe, so every value type the gate excludes — reliability, offload, composability — has had to be smuggled back in as annex prose, which is why the skill has quadrupled since founding while preaching smallness. Its epistemics terminate in an armchair counterfactual judged by the interested party, never once tested with instruments the repo demonstrably owns. And it exempts itself: a mandated pause with a fixed question sequence and an output template, promoted to quasi-ambient authority by an `AGENTS.md` pointer while staying charter-exempt as "just a skill." The honest reading is that this is a constitution — codified taste plus a governor on the model's structure-compulsion — wearing a risk calculus as its cover story. As taste it is excellent. As a risk calculus it cannot lose an argument, and it never has.

## 1. The founding dichotomy doesn't cut where it claims to

The core question — "Am I adding context that helps an agent think, or machinery that makes the decision for it?" (SKILL.md:21) — presupposes that the difference is a property of the artifact. It isn't. It is a property of the reader under load. To an LLM, every sentence on a loaded surface is instruction with soft force: examples anchor, boundaries exclude, and calibration.md's "usually overfit" / "usually weaker" glosses (calibration.md:26-28) are a classifier written in prose. The audit's own corrected M40 concedes the point from the other direction: agents follow rules, worked examples, and boundary clauses "with equal behavioral force." Taken seriously, that dissolves the partition — prose that reliably produces one behavior is a router written in English, and a required field an agent can fill with "n/a" narrows nothing.

What actually varies across designs is different: whether the semantic decision is pre-made, and who can override the constraint (a hook outranks a validator outranks a schema outranks a paragraph). The skill *knows* the first axis — "a deterministic mechanic that does not make the semantic decision" (SKILL.md:40) and the Semantic Scripts test (calibration.md:54) are its two best sentences. But that working distinction lives in a subordinate clause while context-versus-machinery holds the title, and the skill spends its life adjudicating a binary that can't bear weight. The symptom is that calibration.md must keep issuing corrections against its own headline categories — "Length is not the test" (calibration.md:9), "heavy prose that forces review theater is still machinery" (calibration.md:30). A taxonomy that needs a standing errata sheet for its central cut is measuring the wrong variable and knows it.

## 2. The damage gate only hears catastrophe — and the annex is its errata

Machinery earns its place only through the list at SKILL.md:38: deletion, credential exposure, corrupted state, broken recovery, stale authority, unsafe actions, security failures, loss of user trust. That is a catastrophe ontology. Absent from the justification vocabulary entirely: structure whose value is aggregate rather than protective — the field that makes two hundred handoffs queryable, the convention that makes outputs comparable across sessions, the sensor. No single wrong value in any of those damages the work, so none can pass the gate as written; their value exists only at corpus scale, and the gate's frame is per-edit. The repo has already paid for this blindness once: the framework challenge's crux finding was that prune had no sensor for sixty-four eras, and a usage sensor is precisely the artifact class a damage-only test can never demand.

Now watch what the skill's own growth has been doing about this. The founding version was the gate alone. Then Two Kinds arrived to say damage-prevention is "its sharpest case, not its only one" and admit reliability and faithful transformation as value (SKILL.md:49). Then cognitive-offload arrived to admit the-prompt-you-never-typed as value (SKILL.md:51). Then, today, Library Conventions arrived and blessed the close packet — a mandated output shape, machinery by the skill's own taxonomy — justified "so the next reader — human or skill — can branch without re-parsing prose" (SKILL.md:66). That is composability, a value the damage list nowhere names. Every major annex is a patch admitting a value type the founding ontology wrongly excluded. The skill has been writing the refutation of its own gate, section by section, while leaving the gate's text untouched. When the philosophy needed to bless real machinery, it reached outside its own justification vocabulary — and never went back to fix the vocabulary.

Meanwhile "loss of user trust" sits at the end of the list as the infinitely elastic member, vague enough to justify anything. In hard cases it is the member doing all the work — which means the list is not a test; it is a lexicon for rationalizing verdicts reached on taste. Note also the one-sided pricing: the gate itemizes what machinery costs when wrong, and offers its absence only an imagined equivalence ("could lighter context produce the same result"). Nothing asks what missing structure costs, at what frequency. A maximin rule on one side of the ledger; an optimist's thought experiment on the other.

## 3. Prose rides free

Machinery must pass a damage test; context must merely be "clear" (SKILL.md:29). But context is not free. It spends the same currency — the attention and behavior of every future reader — and it accretes with no gate at all. The skill's own body is Exhibit A: 468 words at founding, 1,981 today. The judgment/trust commit alone nearly doubled it (859 → 1,528 words); every substantive touch since has grown it, including today's, and each addition passed the philosophy *because each was context*. The gate has never once fired against the addition class this repo actually commits. The corpus contains almost no semantic validators, routers, or scoring fields to police — its revealed pathology is paragraph accretion: the eighteen-file proof-boundary boilerplate, the only-growing ambient surfaces, this skill's annex. The philosophy polices a threat model imported from elsewhere — form-filling enterprise pipelines — and waves through the failure mode of its own house.

Per-edit gating is structurally blind to this, because accretion is a corpus-level phenomenon and every straw individually passes the did-this-break-the-camel test. The token acknowledgment, the Whole-Surface Check, triggers on "has grown materially" and "makes you hesitate" (calibration.md:79) — the epistemics of a feeling, attached to no schedule, live only when someone was already editing. A philosophy whose declared enemy is accretion but whose only mechanism is an admission gate loses to accretion by default. It is a doorman, not a gardener, and the evidence is that nothing here has ever gotten smaller.

## 4. The armchair counterfactual

Every application of the gate has the same shape: the agent that just imagined a piece of structure asks itself whether lighter context "could produce the same result" (SKILL.md:34), answers from imagination, and proceeds. Three problems compound. The judge is the interested party, consulted at the moment of maximal motivated reasoning. The vocabulary pre-loads the verdict — "machinery," "overbuilt," "the machinery has become the customer" (calibration.md:66) are pejoratives before they are categories; rename the poles "guidance" and "guarantees" and the borderline cases flip. And the counterfactual is never tested. This repo owns `skill-benchmark`, owns a purpose-built circularity-escape methodology (`contract-evaluation-methodology.md`), and pivoted this very day to behavioral evidence — yet the claim "lighter context produces the same result," the load-bearing empirical premise of the entire gate, has never once been run as an experiment. In an apparatus saturated with adversarial verification, the design gate is the one single-judged instrument: no second reader, no refutation pass, no external anchor. The gate demands that machinery prove itself with itemized damage while standing on no observation at all.

## 5. Two Kinds of Skill: a taxonomy that cannot be wrong

The judgment/trust section presents itself as a discovered natural division. The audit already established that the bars actually attach to output-consequence, not skill-kind (M23, M29 — adjudicated; I take it as read). The philosophy-level problem is different: the section runs on kind-thinking — *what sort of thing are you? live up to your kind's virtue* — grafted onto what is fundamentally a consequence question. Kind-thinking generates classification work (mixed skills, per-part bars, which-nature-is-this-paragraph), which the skill then pays down in more prose. And the rescue clause — "This is a lens, not a label" (SKILL.md:57) — completes the immunization: when the taxonomy misleads, it was only ever a lens; when it lands, it is the section's spine. A distinction that cannot misfire cannot inform.

The section is also quietly changing genre. "Be tempered, not timid... do not soften a conclusion merely because it is uncomfortable" (SKILL.md:48) is advice about the *content of judgments*, not the design of agent-facing surfaces. The design gate is accreting a general theory of good agenthood — which is how a checkpoint becomes a constitution (§7), and how a 468-word gate becomes a 1,981-word codex.

## 6. Cognitive-offload: the benediction

The offload doctrine installs a value source that defeats the most natural skeptical instinct by fiat — "*a capable model does this anyway* never by itself proves the skill valueless" (SKILL.md:49) — and forbids the instrument that could check it — "do not build an offload metric" (SKILL.md:51). The framework challenge already adjudicated the installation problem and banked a test, so I will not re-litigate whether offload is real; it probably is. The residue at the philosophy level: within this skill, offload functions as a universal solvent. The text says exactly the right sentence — offload "is why a well-built skill is worth summoning, not a pass on being well-built" (SKILL.md:51) — and then provides no way to catch the pass. There is no named symptom of offload-claimed-but-absent: the user keeps re-supervising, the runs keep getting corrected, the summons quietly stops happening. The Goodhart rationale for refusing a *metric* is respectable; refusing *falsification conditions* is a different act, and the skill conflates the two. A lens with no failure condition is not a lens. It is a benediction, and every skill in the library may claim it.

## 7. The gate cannot pass its own gate

Run the skill on itself. It is "a pause before adding structure" (SKILL.md:8) — a mandated stage for a class of edits; a fixed two-question sequence; a four-item "be able to say plainly" list (SKILL.md:70-75); a five-field output template (SKILL.md:107-113). By its own taxonomy, that is machinery: structure future agents must follow, narrowing their judgment about when structure is justified by pre-installing the answer. What itemized damage justifies it? Nothing on the damage list fits overbuilt process except the elastic member — so the gate's own existence is licensed by exactly the move it forbids everyone else. The anti-ossification clause (SKILL.md:57) names this failure mode and prevents none of it; a disclaimer is not a mechanism, the same way outcome-interviewer's "leave room to reject the frame" did not neutralize the anchor.

And the stakes are no longer skill-sized. `AGENTS.md` names this file "the canonical gate... do not duplicate it here"; the description fires it on any material agent-facing change in a repo whose primary work is skill editing; the ledger shows it fourth-hottest in the library at 46 fires. Library-wide law now lands *here* — the verdict partition and close packet were written into this skill the same day they were adjudicated — and its merit doctrine is co-authored with an always-loaded surface. That is ambient-grade authority accumulating in a build-and-prune container. The `AGENTS.md` pointer passed through the charter once; everything that flows through the pointer afterward inherits ambient reach with zero gating. The M4 carve-out closed exactly this loophole for unattended fire and irreversible tools — gate by capability, not packaging. The authority loophole is the same shape, and it is still open: the repo's deepest normative content is charter-exempt because of its storage format, inside a governance system whose central claim is that gating should never follow storage format.

## 8. Vindication or antibody?

The audit arc is the philosophy's best natural experiment, and its result is disquieting in a way the disposition doesn't name. Fifty-three findings went in. What came out accepted was exactly the set expressible as prose — one-sentence idempotency lines "in lighter non-machinery form," a merged clause, conventions written down — while everything machinery-shaped was rejected or parked. Each rejection had independent, defensible grounds (text-only evidence, no behavioral incident — real reasons; I am not reopening them). But notice that a self-sealing minimalism would produce the *identical* pattern: remedies survive if and only if they are phrased in the philosophy's preferred medium. The gate won every argument about itself, in its own vocabulary, judged from inside its own frame — and both outside critics converged on the structural reading that the audit's master variable was never entered in a contest.

Context-primacy has the same untested status here. Its strongest rival is not enterprise process-worship; it is the position this repo practices daily and never names: *structure is what makes agent output composable and machine-checkable*. Every schema-forced `agent()` call in the Workflow tooling, every typed findings report, the close packet itself — the repo's own instruments run on required fields and mandated output shapes, and they work because of them. The 328-agent audit that judged this philosophy was executed on schema-validated subagents; four died on schema retry caps and the apparatus stood. The philosophy files all of that under "machinery" and moves on; it has never staged the contest between judgment-preservation and composability. When a philosophy wins every argument conducted in its vocabulary, nothing in the method can distinguish *correct* from *unfalsifiable*. That is the same black hole the outcome-interviewer critique found at convergence: when the gate says no, what exactly said no — the evidence, or the aesthetic?

## 9. No theory of the reader

"Machinery removes or narrows judgment" (SKILL.md:27) is a cost only where judgment worth keeping exists. The presumption against structure therefore encodes an empirical bet about model capability — and the skill legislates it as timeless law with no reader model at all. The readers are heterogeneous: the same surfaces serve a frontier main loop and small subagents in a fan-out, Claude and Codex, calm sessions and degraded end-of-context ones. Structure that insults the first reader is load-bearing for the last. The framework challenge's own best defense of skills — value that "lives in the variance," pinning the high tail of a stochastic model's distribution under load — is a defense of exactly the fixed steps and firm shapes this philosophy's judgment-bar grades as cost. And the bet is never scheduled for re-examination in either direction: as models improve, the presumption should strengthen and the exception list shrink; nothing will notice. A design law whose truth depends on the capability distribution of its readers, stated without a reader model or a revision trigger, is a snapshot wearing the grammar of principle.

## 10. What survives, and the honest reframe

Much survives. The semantic/deterministic line — keep deterministic mechanics in code, leave semantic decisions to the agent (calibration.md:54) — is genuinely excellent, the best single idea in the library's meta-layer, and it deserves the title role the context/machinery binary currently occupies. Inspect-the-live-target-before-deciding (SKILL.md:14) is the best epistemics in the skill. The no-silent-substitution rule (SKILL.md:42) is real respect for the user's authority and survived the audit's negation adversary on its own strength. The Tool Returns lens (calibration.md:70) applies the good distinction at exactly the right seam. And the underlying instinct — that agent judgment is the scarce resource, and that pre-deciding semantic calls in schemas is how agent systems rot — is right, early, and worth building a philosophy around.

But the most honest reading of the whole artifact is this: it is not an engineering discipline, it is a constitution — a codified taste (minimal, prose-first, judgment-respecting) fused with a governor on a known model pathology, the compulsion to perform diligence by generating process. As restraint engineering it is excellent, the same way outcome-interviewer's restraints were excellent. The damage test is the cover story that makes the taste feel like risk analysis — and the tell is that in every hard case the verdict is delivered by the elastic member or the armchair counterfactual, neither of which can push back. Cover stories accrete features as if they were true: a two-kind ontology, an offload theology, library law, an output packet, each patch defending the story rather than the function. If the skill knew itself as a constitution, it would claim its authority honestly — *this is the house style, and the house has good taste* — promote the semantic/deterministic line to the headline, price prose in the same currency as schema, grow a removal rhythm to match its admission gate, and name what evidence would count against each of its beliefs.

The heart of it: this skill believes it is a neutral gate weighing structure against damage. Under pressure, it is the immune system of a minimalist aesthetic — usually right, never wrong by construction, and incapable of hearing an argument in any vocabulary but its own.
